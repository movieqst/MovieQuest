🎥 Did You Know?

Omaha Beach in Ireland

The D-Day landing was filmed on Ballinesker Beach at Curracloe Strand, County Wexford, Ireland, because Normandy's beaches had strict filming restrictions.

A $12 Million Sequence

The Omaha Beach opening alone cost about $12 million, over 15% of the film's $70 million budget, and used more than 2,500 Irish Army reservists as extras.

Based on Real Brothers

The story was inspired by the Niland brothers: three of the four were reported killed, and the fourth, Edward, was later found alive after escaping a Japanese prison camp in Burma.

Real Boot Camp

The lead actors went through a gruelling 10-day boot camp run by retired Marine Captain Dale Dye; only Matt Damon, who played Ryan, was spared.

Five Oscars

The film won five Academy Awards: Best Director for Steven Spielberg, plus Cinematography, Sound, Sound Effects Editing and Film Editing.
