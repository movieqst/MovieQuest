📝 Synopsis

October, 1957. "It" awakens, and the small town of Derry, Maine will never be the same. Seven children face an unthinkable horror that appears in many forms, including Pennywise the clown (Tim Curry), who lives, hunts and kills from the town's sewers. Thirty years later, the surviving adults who were brave enough return to stop the new killing spree, this time for good. Stephen King's classic, complete in high definition.

🎥 Did You Know?

Tim Curry's Pennywise

Tim Curry's turn as Pennywise the Dancing Clown is still remembered as one of the scariest clowns ever put on screen.

ABC's Biggest Hit of 1990

The two-part mini-series aired on ABC on November 18 and 20, 1990, and drew about 30 million viewers, making it ABC's biggest success of the year.

Derry in British Columbia

The town of Derry, Maine was actually New Westminster, British Columbia, where the mini-series was filmed over about three months in 1990.

An Emmy Winner

IT was nominated for two Emmys and won for Outstanding Music Composition for Richard Bellis's score.

Written by a King Veteran

Lawrence D. Cohen, who wrote the screenplay for Carrie (1976), adapted the novel with director Tommy Lee Wallace.
