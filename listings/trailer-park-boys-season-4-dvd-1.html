📝 Synopsis

Ricky, now Trailer Park Supervisor, has turned Julian's trailer into a dope factory and spends his days playing hash hockey with Bubbles. When Julian, Mr. Lahey and Randy all get out of jail on the same day, things at the park start to unwind, and the boys decide to grow enough dope to buy the trailer park. Along the way come the Green Bastard, Conky's return, a run-in with Sam Losco, J-Roc's arrest and the accidental kidnapping of Rita MacNeil and her band. By the end of the season there is a new park owner, but it's not who you think.

🎥 Did You Know?

Gemini Winner

Trailer Park Boys won the 2004 Gemini Award for Best Comedy Series, as the front of this box proudly points out.

Rita MacNeil Guest Stars

Cape Breton singing legend Rita MacNeil plays herself this season, when the boys accidentally kidnap her and her band.

The Last Season in Real Parks

Season 4 was the last one filmed in real trailer parks around Nova Scotia. From Season 5 on, the show used its own Sunnyvale set built in Dartmouth.

It Started with One Last Shot

Creator Mike Clattenburg first put early versions of Julian and Ricky in his 1998 short film One Last Shot, which was entirely improvised.

Back on Netflix

The show ran seven seasons on Showcase, then Netflix revived it in 2014 for five more seasons, specials, films and an animated series.
