🎥 Did You Know?

King Wrote It

Stephen King wrote the teleplay himself, adapting his own best-selling novel, and makes a cameo as Teddy Weizak.

Four Nights on ABC

The mini-series aired on ABC in four parts between May 8 and May 12, 1994: The Plague, The Dreams, The Betrayal and The Stand.

A Ratings Hit

About 19 million homes watched all four parts, with each night drawing around a third of the TV audience.

Emmy Winner

The Stand earned six Emmy nominations and won two, for Outstanding Makeup and Outstanding Sound Mixing.

Boulder in Utah

Mick Garris directed all four parts. Plans to film in Boulder, Colorado fell through, so the Boulder scenes were shot in Utah, with more filming in Nevada, Pennsylvania and New York.
