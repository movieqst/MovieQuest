🎵 Did You Know?

Lisa Loeb's Big Break

"Stay (I Missed You)" went to No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100, making Lisa Loeb the first artist to top the chart without a record deal. Ethan Hawke, her neighbour, passed the song to director Ben Stiller.

The My Sharona Scene

The Knack's "My Sharona" plays in the film's famous convenience-store dance scene and returned to the charts after the movie came out.

Ben Stiller's Directing Debut

Reality Bites was Ben Stiller's first feature as director; he also co-stars as TV executive Michael Grates.

Ethan Hawke Sings

Track 10, "I'm Nuthin'", is sung by Ethan Hawke himself, in character as slacker musician Troy Dyer.

Big Mountain's Reggae Hit

Big Mountain's reggae cover of Peter Frampton's "Baby, I Love Your Way" became a worldwide top-10 hit thanks to this soundtrack.
