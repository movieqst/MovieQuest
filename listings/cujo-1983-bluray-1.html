🎥 Did You Know?

How Many Cujos?

Cast and crew remember anywhere from five to 13 trained Saint Bernards playing Cujo. A man in a dog suit and a mechanical dog were used for some of the attack shots.

A New Director

Peter Medak was the original director but left a few days into filming over creative differences. Lewis Teague took over.

A Different Ending

In Stephen King's 1981 novel, young Tad does not survive. The studio insisted on changing the ending for the film, and King accepted it.

Maine in California

Although the story is set in Maine, the film was shot in northern California in late 1982.

Dee Wallace's Favourite

Dee Wallace, fresh from E.T., found the shoot so draining she was treated for exhaustion afterwards, yet she calls Cujo her favourite of her own films.
