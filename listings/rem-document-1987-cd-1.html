🎵 Did You Know?

R.E.M.'s Breakthrough

Document was R.E.M.'s fifth album (hence "R.E.M. No. 5" on the cover) and their first to go platinum in the US.

The One I Love

"The One I Love" was the band's first US top-10 hit. Despite the title, it's a bitter song about using people, not a love song.

The End of the World

"It's the End of the World as We Know It (And I Feel Fine)" became one of R.E.M.'s best-known songs, with its rapid-fire stream-of-consciousness lyrics.

Last Album on I.R.S.

Document was R.E.M.'s last studio album for the independent label I.R.S. Records before they signed with Warner Bros.

Enter Scott Litt

It was the first R.E.M. album produced with Scott Litt, who went on to co-produce their biggest records, including Out of Time and Automatic for the People.
