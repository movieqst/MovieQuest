🎵 Did You Know?

Written for a Man

"Girls Just Want to Have Fun" was written and first recorded by Robert Hazard, from a male point of view. Lauper rewrote parts of the lyric, and her version reached No. 2 on the Billboard Hot 100.

The Last Song Added

Lauper and Rob Hyman of The Hooters wrote "Time After Time" late in the She's So Unusual sessions, after producer Rick Chertoff insisted they needed one more song. The title came from a TV Guide listing for the 1979 film of the same name.

First Number One

"Time After Time" hit No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100 in June 1984, Lauper's first American chart-topper, one place higher than "Girls Just Want to Have Fun".

A Record-Setting Debut

She's So Unusual (1983) was the first debut album by a female artist to produce four Top 5 Hot 100 hits, and Lauper won the Best New Artist Grammy in 1985.

Turned Down by Others

"True Colors", by Billy Steinberg and Tom Kelly, was passed on by Anne Murray before Lauper recorded it. It reached No. 1 on the Hot 100 on October 25, 1986.
