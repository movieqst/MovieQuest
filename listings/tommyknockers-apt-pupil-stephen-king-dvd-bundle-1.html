🎥 Did You Know?

Filmed in New Zealand

The Tommyknockers' New England town of Haven was shot in Puhoi, New Zealand, with Auckland warehouses used for the interiors, including the spaceship.

A Two-Night ABC Event

The Tommyknockers aired on ABC on May 9 and 10, 1993, as a two-part, 181-minute mini-series; this disc has one part on each side.

From Different Seasons

Apt Pupil is based on Stephen King's novella from his 1982 collection Different Seasons, the same book that gave us The Shawshank Redemption and Stand by Me.

A First Attempt Abandoned

Apt Pupil was first filmed in 1987 with Nicol Williamson and Rick Schroder, but money ran out after about ten weeks and the film was never finished.

After The Usual Suspects

Bryan Singer made Apt Pupil as his follow-up to The Usual Suspects (1995), casting Ian McKellen as the hidden Nazi war criminal.
