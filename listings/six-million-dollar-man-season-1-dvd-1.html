📝 Synopsis

Grievously injured in the crash of an experimental aircraft, Colonel Steve Austin's shattered body is covertly rebuilt via the miracle of modern science known as bionics. Equipped with atomic-powered limbs that make him "better, stronger, faster" than the average mortal, Austin (Lee Majors) can now run at speeds exceeding 60 miles per hour, overturn cars with ease, and spot an encroaching enemy from over a mile away. Under the watchful eye of OSI director Oscar Goldman (Richard Anderson), Steve repays his debt to the taxpayers by taking on perilous missions of a highly classified nature. This digitally remastered 6-DVD set features 13 uncut episodes, all three made-for-TV movies in their original, unedited versions, and over two hours of never-before-seen bonus materials. 🎥 Did You Know?

Based on a Novel

The series was based on Martin Caidin's 1972 novel Cyborg. The first pilot TV movie aired on ABC in March 1973.

That Famous Opening

"Gentlemen, we can rebuild him. We have the technology." The opening uses real NASA footage of the M2-F2 lifting body crash from 1967.

Shatner Guest Stars

William Shatner guest-stars as a troubled astronaut in "Burning Bright", one of the season's 13 episodes.

The Bionic Sound

The slow-motion running and the "ch-ch-ch-ch" bionic sound effect became the show's trademark, copied in playgrounds everywhere.

Where Jaime Came From

Lindsay Wagner's Jaime Sommers first appeared in season 2 of this show and was so popular she got her own series, The Bionic Woman.
