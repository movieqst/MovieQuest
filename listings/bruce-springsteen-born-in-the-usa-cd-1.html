🎵 Did You Know?

Seven Top 10 Singles

Seven of the album's twelve songs reached the US Top 10: "Dancing in the Dark", "Cover Me", "Born in the U.S.A.", "I'm on Fire", "Glory Days", "I'm Goin' Down" and "My Hometown".

A Blockbuster

It sold over 17 million copies in the US and about 30 million worldwide, was the best-selling album of 1985, and stayed in the Billboard top 10 for 84 straight weeks.

The First American CD

Born in the U.S.A. was the first compact disc manufactured for commercial release in the United States, pressed at CBS's plant in Terre Haute, Indiana.

Courteney Cox Gets Pulled Up

In Brian De Palma's "Dancing in the Dark" video, Springsteen pulls a young Courteney Cox out of the crowd to dance with him.

An Annie Leibovitz Cover

The famous jeans-and-flag cover photo was taken by Annie Leibovitz.
