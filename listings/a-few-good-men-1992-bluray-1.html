📝 Synopsis

Tom Cruise, Jack Nicholson and Demi Moore star in Rob Reiner's unanimously acclaimed drama. Cruise stars as a brash Navy lawyer who's teamed with a gung-ho litigator (Moore) in a politically explosive murder case. Charged with defending two Marines accused of killing a fellow soldier, they are confronted with complex issues of loyalty and honour, including its most sacred code and its most formidable warrior (Nicholson). Superbly directed with a trio of powerhouse performances and an outstanding supporting cast including Kevin Bacon, Kevin Pollak and Kiefer Sutherland.

🎥 Did You Know?

Written on Cocktail Napkins

Aaron Sorkin began writing A Few Good Men on cocktail napkins while working as a bartender at Broadway's Palace Theatre.

A Sister's Story

Sorkin's sister Deborah, a Navy JAG lawyer, told him about a Guantanamo Bay hazing case involving a "Code Red" that inspired the story.

Stage to Screen

It began as Sorkin's Broadway play before Rob Reiner turned it into this 1992 film; the Blu-ray includes a From Stage to Screen featurette with both men.

Three Scenes, One Famous Speech

Jack Nicholson appears in only three scenes, and reportedly delivered his "You can't handle the truth!" speech an estimated 40 to 50 times.

Seinfeld Changed the Cast

Jason Alexander was set to play Sam Weinberg but became unavailable when Seinfeld was renewed, so the role went to Kevin Pollak.
