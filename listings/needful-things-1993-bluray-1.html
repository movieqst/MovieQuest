📝 Synopsis

Welcome to Castle Rock, Maine, a lovely place to live... if you don't mind selling your soul! Acting greats Ed Harris (The Abyss) and Max von Sydow (Three Days of the Condor) head an exceptional cast that includes Bonnie Bedelia, J.T. Walsh and Amanda Plummer in this supernatural thriller that's dark, haunting and a hell of a good scare. Sheriff Alan Pangborn (Harris) has a devil of a problem: suddenly all the residents of his sleepy little town are dying... to kill each other. But at least business is still booming, especially at a new antique store. The shop's mysterious owner (von Sydow) has something for everyone, and his prices are always reasonable: just one small favor... eternal damnation!

🎥 Did You Know?

A Heston Behind the Camera

Needful Things was directed by Fraser C. Heston, son of Charlton Heston; he records the commentary on this disc.

The Rare Three-Hour Version

TBS later had the film re-edited into a roughly three-hour mini-series with about an hour of deleted scenes added back; it has never been released on home video in the US.

Welcome to Castle Rock

The story is set in Castle Rock, Maine, the fictional town that also appears in King's The Dead Zone, Cujo and The Body (Stand by Me).

A Cult Favourite

Panned by critics in 1993, the film has since built a cult following, largely thanks to Max von Sydow's devilish Leland Gaunt.

Castle Rock Entertainment

It was produced by Castle Rock Entertainment, the company Rob Reiner named after King's town.
