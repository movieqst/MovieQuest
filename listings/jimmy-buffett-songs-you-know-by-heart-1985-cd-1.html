🎵 Did You Know?

Greatest Hit(s)

Released in 1985, the compilation only covers 1973 to 1979. The parenthetical "s" in the subtitle "Jimmy Buffett's Greatest Hit(s)" nods to "Margaritaville" being his one big chart hit at the time.

A Slow Burn

The album peaked at only No. 100 on the Billboard 200 in 1985, yet it was certified 7x Platinum by the RIAA in 2005 and became Buffett's best-selling record.

A Posthumous No. 4

After Buffett died in September 2023, the album re-entered the Billboard 200 at a new peak of No. 4, its first time in the top 40, and hit No. 1 on the album sales chart.

Marvin Gardens

"Why Don't We Get Drunk" was written under the pseudonym Marvin Gardens, a Monopoly property. It began as the B-side of "The Great Filling Station Holdup" before becoming the bigger fan favourite.

Chart Numbers

"Margaritaville" peaked at No. 8 on the Hot 100, spent 22 weeks on the chart and entered the Grammy Hall of Fame in 2016. "Come Monday" was his first Top 40 pop single, and "Cheeseburger in Paradise" reached No. 32.
