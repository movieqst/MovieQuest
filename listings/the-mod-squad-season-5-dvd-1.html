📝 Synopsis

Pete, Linc and Julie were three young rebels who became undercover cops, with no badges and no guns, under the watch of Captain Adam Greer. In the fifth and final season they take on a narcotics ring, a gang of motorcycle robbers, loan sharks, kidnappers and a string of art thefts. The Complete Season 5 collects all 24 episodes on 8 discs, from the two-part premiere "The Connection" to the series finale "And Once for My Baby".

🎥 Did You Know?

The Final Season

Season 5 was the show's last. The series ran on ABC from September 24, 1968 to March 1, 1973, for 124 episodes, and this set ends with the series finale "And Once for My Baby".

Based on a Real Squad

Creator Bud Ruskin based the show loosely on his own experience in the late 1950s leading a squad of young undercover narcotics cops. It took ABC almost 10 years to green-light his idea.

Spelling and Thomas

The series was produced by Aaron Spelling and Danny Thomas through Thomas-Spelling Productions, with a theme by Earle Hagen.

Award Winner

The Mod Squad earned six Emmy nominations and four Golden Globe nominations, and Peggy Lipton won a Golden Globe for playing Julie Barnes.

Tyne Daly Guest Stars

Future Cagney & Lacey star Tyne Daly guest stars as Prudence Gordon in "Good Times Are Just Memories" on Disc 3.

The Squad Returns

The whole original cast reunited for the TV movie The Return of the Mod Squad on ABC in 1979, and the show was remade as a 1999 film with Claire Danes, Giovanni Ribisi and Omar Epps.
