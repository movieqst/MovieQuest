🎥 Did You Know?

Timberlake's Tax Nightmare

The very first episode sent fake government agents to Justin Timberlake's house to seize his home and belongings over $900,000 in unpaid taxes. His near-tears reaction became one of the most famous moments in reality TV.

Frankie Muniz Loses His Porsche

Malcolm in the Middle star Frankie Muniz was the show's first victim, convinced that his Porsche had been stolen from outside a store.

Created by Ashton

Ashton Kutcher, then starring on That '70s Show, created the series with Jason Goldberg and hosted every prank. It premiered on MTV on March 17, 2003.

Dax Shepard's Big Break

Dax Shepard was one of the original Punk'd pranksters before going on to star in Parenthood and host the Armchair Expert podcast.

The Show That Wouldn't Die

Punk'd was revived on MTV in 2012, on BET in 2015, and on Quibi in 2020 with Chance the Rapper as host.
