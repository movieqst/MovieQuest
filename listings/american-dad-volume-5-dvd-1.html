🎥 Did You Know?

A Super Bowl Premiere

American Dad! premiered on Fox right after Super Bowl XXXIX in February 2005 and drew 15.1 million viewers.

From the Family Guy Team

The show was created by Seth MacFarlane with Mike Barker and Matt Weitzman. MacFarlane voices both Stan Smith and Roger the alien.

A Skier in a Fishbowl

Klaus the goldfish is really a former East German Olympic ski jumper whose brain was put into a fish by the CIA.

Revenge Is the American Way

Volume 5 is all about getting even: Steve takes on Bar Mitzvah boy Etan Cohen in "Bar Mitzvah Hustle", Francine discovers Stan's back-up wife in "Wife Insurance", and Roger unleashes hot dog havoc in "The Weiner of Our Discontent".
