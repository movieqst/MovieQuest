📝 Synopsis

In season six, the Sunnyvale denizens are up to their usual hijinks. Bubbles decides to go legit and opens a feline dream vacation area and theme park called "Kittyland". Ricky becomes a garbage man and enlists Trevor to move people's lawn furniture to the curb to be collected, and Julian gets into the real estate business. Randy gets fed up with Lahey's drinking and becomes the Associate Trailer Park Supervisor, Cory and Trevor open a "Convenients Store", and Ray gets kicked out of the park for living in his old rig. Things get unhinged when Mr. Lahey finds super 8 footage from Halloween 1977 proving the Boys caused him to be kicked off the police force.

🎥 Did You Know?

Welcome to Kittyland

This season Bubbles tries to go legit by opening Kittyland, a feline dream vacation area and theme park for cats.

Lahey's Halloween Tape

The season's big twist comes when Mr. Lahey finds super 8 footage from Halloween 1977 that proves Ricky, Bubbles and Julian got him kicked off the police force.

A Park of Their Own

Like Season 5, this season was filmed on the show's own Sunnyvale set in Dartmouth, Nova Scotia, after the production stopped shooting in real trailer parks.

It Started with One Last Shot

Creator Mike Clattenburg first put early versions of Julian and Ricky in his 1998 short film One Last Shot, which was entirely improvised.

Back on Netflix

The show ran seven seasons on Showcase, then Netflix revived it in 2014 for five more seasons, specials, films and an animated series.
