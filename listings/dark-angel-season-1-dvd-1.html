🎥 Did You Know?

Cameron's First TV Series

Dark Angel was the first television series from James Cameron, fresh off Titanic. He co-wrote the pilot with co-creator Charles H. Eglee.

Jessica Alba's Big Break

Jessica Alba beat about 1,200 other actresses for the role of Max, and the part earned her a Golden Globe nomination.

Seattle Played by Vancouver

The show's post-apocalyptic Seattle was filmed in and around Vancouver, British Columbia.

Pick Up the Second Season Too

Season one introduces Max, Logan and the Manticore escapees. The story continues in the Complete Second Season, which we also have listed.
