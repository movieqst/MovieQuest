📝 Synopsis

He can trim hedges, cut grass... and control the world. A virtual reality researcher (Pierce Brosnan) applies his techniques to a mentally challenged gardener named Jobe (Jeff Fahey) with spectacular results in The Lawnmower Man. 🎥 Did You Know?

Stephen King Sued

The film was sold as "Stephen King's The Lawnmower Man", but it bore little resemblance to his short story. King sued to have his name removed, won, and when New Line put his name back on the home video release, the studio was held in contempt of court.

A New Jobe

For the sequel, Matt Frewer (Max Headroom) took over the role of Jobe from Jeff Fahey.

Early VR on Screen

The Lawnmower Man was one of the first films built around virtual reality, with groundbreaking computer-generated cyberspace sequences for 1992.

A Sequel That Crashed

Lawnmower Man 2 (also released as Beyond Cyberspace) reportedly cost about $15 million and earned only about $2.4 million.

Bond Before Bond

Pierce Brosnan played scientist Dr. Lawrence Angelo three years before his debut as James Bond in GoldenEye.
