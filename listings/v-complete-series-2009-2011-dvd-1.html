🎥 Did You Know?

A Remake of the 1983 Classic

This series reimagines Kenneth Johnson's 1983 miniseries V, about seemingly friendly alien Visitors who arrive in giant motherships hovering over Earth's major cities.

A Lost and Firefly Reunion

Elizabeth Mitchell (Juliet on Lost) plays FBI agent Erica Evans, and Morena Baccarin (Inara on Firefly) plays Anna, the Visitors' calm, chilling queen.

Original Stars Return

Jane Badler, who played the villain Diana in the 1980s series, returns in season two as Diana, Anna's imprisoned mother.

Two Seasons Only

ABC cancelled V in May 2011 after two seasons and 22 episodes, ending on a cliffhanger. These two sets are the complete series.
