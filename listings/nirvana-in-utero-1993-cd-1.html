🎵 Did You Know?

Recorded in Two Weeks

Steve Albini recorded In Utero in about two weeks in February 1993 at Pachyderm Studio, a remote studio in Cannon Falls, Minnesota.

Nirvana's Last Studio Album

Released in September 1993, In Utero was Nirvana's third and final studio album, and it debuted at No. 1 on the Billboard 200.

The Anatomical Angel

The cover art is a transparent anatomical model with wings, based on an idea by Kurt Cobain, who also designed the collage of models and flowers on the back.

Store-Friendly Version

Some big US retailers refused to stock the album, so a version was made with "Rape Me" listed as "Waif Me" and a different back cover. This copy has the original artwork and titles.

Columbia House Pressing

"CRC" on the disc means this is a Columbia House record club pressing, made in Scarborough, Ontario.
