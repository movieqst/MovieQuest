🎥 Did You Know?

A Brat Pack Pairing

Rob Lowe and Demi Moore star in both films, made back to back in 1985 and 1986 at the height of the Brat Pack era.

Man in Motion

John Parr's "St. Elmo's Fire (Man in Motion)" went to No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100 for two weeks in September 1985, and David Foster's "Love Theme from St. Elmo's Fire" reached No. 15.

Georgetown Grads

The seven friends in St. Elmo's Fire are recent Georgetown University graduates, and the film is named after their favourite bar.

A Summer Hit

Made for about $10 million, St. Elmo's Fire took in about $38 million at the box office after opening June 28, 1985.

From the Stage

About Last Night... is based on David Mamet's play Sexual Perversity in Chicago, and was the first feature film directed by Edward Zwick, later of Glory and The Last Samurai.
