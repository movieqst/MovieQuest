🎥 Did You Know?

King Was Won Over

Stephen King said that "everything that I ever disliked about the Kubrick version of The Shining is redeemed for me here."

Kubrick's Overlook, Rebuilt

The production rebuilt the Overlook Hotel sets using the blueprints from Stanley Kubrick's The Shining.

New Faces, Old Roles

Carl Lumbly plays Dick Hallorann (Scatman Crothers in 1980) and Alex Essoe plays Wendy Torrance (Shelley Duvall in 1980).

Two Stories, One Sequel

Mike Flanagan had to reconcile King's novel, where the Overlook burns down, with Kubrick's film, where it survives; he asked King's permission to bring the hotel back.

Both Cuts Included

This set has the 152-minute theatrical version and the 180-minute director's cut, each on its own Blu-ray.
