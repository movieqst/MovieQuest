🎵 Did You Know?

River Phoenix's Last Film

The Thing Called Love was the last film River Phoenix completed. He died on October 31, 1993, a few weeks after it opened.

Phoenix Wrote Songs, Too

River Phoenix wrote two songs for the film, including "Lone Star State of Mine", but that song is not on this soundtrack album. The album features Giant Records artists and country acts who appeared in the film instead.

A Director Swap

Peter Bogdanovich took over the film in September 1992, after original director Brian Gibson left to make What's Love Got to Do with It.

Box Office Flop

The film grossed about $1 million against a $14 million budget. Paramount held back a wider release after Phoenix's death.

Blame It on Your Heart

Harlan Howard and Kostas wrote "Blame It on Your Heart", a No. 1 country hit for Patty Loveless. In the film, Phoenix sings it at a Bluebird audition, and Deborah Allen's version is on this album.
