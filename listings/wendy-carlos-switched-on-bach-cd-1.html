The album that brought the Moog synthesizer to the world. Wendy Carlos's virtuoso electronic performances of J.S. Bach, including Brandenburg Concerto No. 3, "Air on the G String", "Jesu, Joy of Man's Desiring", the Sinfonia from Cantata No. 29, Two-Part Inventions, the Chorale Prelude "Wachet Auf" and Preludes and Fugues from The Well-Tempered Clavier. 🎵 Did You Know?

A Classical Chart-Topper

Switched-On Bach was released in October 1968, reached No. 10 on the Billboard 200 and held No. 1 on the Billboard Classical Albums chart from January 1969 to January 1972.

Three Grammys

The album won three Grammy Awards in 1970, including Best Classical Album and Best Engineered Classical Recording.

One Note at a Time

The Moog synthesizer of the day could only play one note at a time, so every part was recorded separately and layered on a custom 8-track recorder Wendy Carlos built in a basement studio.

Platinum Classic

It was certified platinum in 1986, only the second classical album ever to reach that mark.

Who Made It

Produced by Rachel Elkind and Wendy Carlos, with Benjamin Folkman assisting on the performances.
