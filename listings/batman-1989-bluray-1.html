🎥 Did You Know?

Casting Uproar

When Michael Keaton, then best known for comedies, was cast as Batman, fans were so upset that Warner Bros. reportedly received around 50,000 letters of protest.

Nicholson's Big Payday

Jack Nicholson took a lower upfront fee in exchange for a share of the box office and merchandise, a deal said to have earned him around $60 million.

Oscar-Winning Gotham

Production designer Anton Furst built a huge Gotham City backlot at Pinewood Studios in England, and the work won him the Academy Award for Best Art Direction.

Prince on the Soundtrack

Prince wrote and recorded a full album of songs for the film, and his single "Batdance" went to No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100.
