🎥 Did You Know?

Written at Tower Records

Andrew Kevin Walker wrote Seven while working at a Tower Records store in New York City, a time he later described as depressing.

Pitt Fought for the Ending

Producers pushed for a softer finale, but Brad Pitt insisted on keeping the original 'what's in the box' ending and refused to change it after test screenings.

A Real Injury on Set

Pitt slipped during the rain-soaked chase and put his arm through a car windshield; the cast he wore was written into the script.

The Famous Opening Titles

Kyle Cooper's scratchy, journal-filled title sequence, set to a Nine Inch Nails remix, was hugely influential and is still studied by designers.

Beat Showgirls

Seven opened on September 22, 1995, the same day as Showgirls, and topped it; it went on to earn over $327 million worldwide.
