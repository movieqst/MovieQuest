📝 Synopsis

🎥 Did You Know?

Based on a Japanese Novel

The film is adapted from Hiroshi Sakurazaka's 2004 light novel All You Need Is Kill.

Three Titles

It was released in cinemas as Edge of Tomorrow, but the home video release pushed its tagline, Live Die Repeat, so hard that many fans know it by that name.

Real Exosuits

The battle suits were practical props weighing up to about 40 kg (85 lb), and Tom Cruise and Emily Blunt trained for months to move in them.

Emily Blunt Bulked Up

Emily Blunt trained intensively for three months to play the war hero Rita Vrataski, the "Full Metal Bitch".
