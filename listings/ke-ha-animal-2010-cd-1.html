🎵 Did You Know?

Debut at Number One

Animal was released in January 2010 and debuted at No. 1 on both the Billboard 200 and the Canadian Albums Chart, with about 152,000 copies sold in its first US week.

A Record-Setting Lead Single

"Tik Tok" spent nine consecutive weeks at No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100 and was the longest-running No. 1 of 2010. It was the first Hot 100 chart-topper of the 2010s, on the chart dated January 2, 2010.

Before the App

"Tik Tok" was co-written by Ke$ha, Dr. Luke and Benny Blanco, and came from a night of coming home half-drunk after partying. It has sold over 18 million copies worldwide as of 2019.

Four Hit Singles

Animal produced four singles: "Tik Tok", "Blah Blah Blah" (featuring 3OH!3), "Your Love Is My Drug" and "Take It Off". All four reached the top ten in Canada.

The Dollar Sign

Ke$ha stylised her name with a dollar sign on this album, years before she dropped it and became simply Kesha. In 2010 Billboard named her its artist of the year.
