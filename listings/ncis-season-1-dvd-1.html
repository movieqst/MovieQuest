📝 Synopsis

NCIS: The Complete First Season follows an elite team of special agents who operate outside the military chain of command. Led by Special Agent Leroy Jethro Gibbs (Mark Harmon), a tough, highly skilled investigator who's willing to bend the rules to get the job done, this tight-knit crew travel the world investigating everything from murder and espionage to terrorism and kidnapping in this 6-disc set featuring all 23 episodes of the first season.

🎥 Did You Know?

Born on JAG

Gibbs and his team were introduced in a two-part episode of JAG before NCIS premiered on CBS on September 23, 2003.

Kate's Arrival

Sasha Alexander joined as Secret Service agent Kate Todd, a role first played as Vivian Blackadder by Robyn Lively in the JAG pilot.

Enter McGee and Palmer

Sean Murray first appears as Timothy McGee in "Sub Rosa", and Brian Dietzen debuts as Jimmy Palmer in "Split Decision".

Ari's First Strike

"Bête Noire" introduces Ari Haswari, the terrorist who becomes the main villain of the first two seasons.

From Bellisario

Co-creator Donald P. Bellisario, who also created JAG, Magnum, P.I. and Quantum Leap, records the commentary on "Yankee White" for this set.
