📝 Synopsis

She can jump to the top of high buildings, single-handedly battle vicious thugs, run down high-speed vehicles and hear top-secret codes. She's The Bionic Woman... and she's back for more thrilling adventures in the gripping second season. With only brief interactions with her former love, Six Million Dollar Man Steve Austin (Lee Majors), Jaime Sommers (Lindsay Wagner in her Primetime Emmy Award-winning role) is on her own to test the limits of her incredible state-of-the-art bionic limbs. But with the secretive Office of Scientific Investigations (OSI) sending her on increasingly dangerous and deadly covert missions, will she survive long enough to realize her full potential as the world's first female cyborg? It's non-stop action and excitement as Jaime battles enemy foreign agents, hostile extraterrestrials and the fan-favourite fembots in 24 extraordinary episodes, including two crossover episodes with The Six Million Dollar Man!

🎥 Did You Know?

An Emmy-Winning Role

Lindsay Wagner won the 1977 Primetime Emmy for Outstanding Lead Actress in a Drama Series for this season as Jaime Sommers.

Brought Back by Fans

Jaime died in her first Six Million Dollar Man appearance, but viewer response was so strong that the writers revived her and gave her her own series.

Enter the Fembots

Season two introduced the Fembots, the lifelike female robots with removable faces, in "Kill Oscar", a fan favourite crossover with The Six Million Dollar Man.

Max the Bionic Dog

Max, the bionic German shepherd, also debuted this season and later became a regular companion for Jaime.

From the Creator of V

Series creator Kenneth Johnson, who provides commentary here, later created V and The Incredible Hulk TV series.
