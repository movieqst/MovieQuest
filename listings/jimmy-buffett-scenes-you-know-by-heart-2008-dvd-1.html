🎵 Did You Know?

Songs Became Scenes

The title crosses out "Songs" and writes "Scenes": it's the video companion to Buffett's 1985 greatest-hits album Songs You Know by Heart, with the same 13 songs plus two bonus songs.

Store Exclusive

When it came out in June 2008, the DVD was sold only at Wal-Mart, Sam's Club and Buffett's own Mailboat Records website.

Buffett's Own Label

Mailboat Records is Jimmy Buffett's own independent label, which he started in 1999 for his live albums and later releases.

Five O'Clock Somewhere

Bonus song "It's Five O'Clock Somewhere" was Buffett's 2003 duet with Alan Jackson. It spent eight weeks at No. 1 on the Billboard country chart and won the CMA Award for Vocal Event of the Year.

Friends in the Credits

The booklet thanks guest players including Mac McAnally, Michael Utley, Robert Greenidge, Ralph MacDonald, ukulele star Jake Shimabukuro and Little Feat's Bill Payne.
