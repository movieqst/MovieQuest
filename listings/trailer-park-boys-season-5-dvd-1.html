🎥 Did You Know?

A Park of Their Own

Season 5 was the first filmed on the show's own Sunnyvale set, built on land the production bought in Dartmouth, Nova Scotia, after residents of the real trailer parks complained. The "A Park of Our Own" featurette on this set covers the move.

Commentary in Character

Two of this set's commentary tracks are done in character: one by Mr. Lahey and Randy, and one by Lucy and Sarah.

It Started with One Last Shot

Creator Mike Clattenburg first put early versions of Julian and Ricky in his 1998 short film One Last Shot, which was entirely improvised.

Bubbles' Glasses

Mike Smith's famous Bubbles glasses came from a Texas flea market, bought for 50 cents.

Back on Netflix

The show ran seven seasons on Showcase, then Netflix revived it in 2014 for five more seasons, specials, films and an animated series.
