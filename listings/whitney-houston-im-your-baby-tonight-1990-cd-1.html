🎵 Did You Know?

Two No. 1 Hits

The title track and "All the Man That I Need" both went to No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100.

A New Sound

Whitney's third album brought in L.A. Reid and Babyface for a more R&B and new jack swing sound than her first two records.

"Nippy"

The licence plate on the cover reads NIPPY, Whitney Houston's childhood nickname.

Duet with Stevie Wonder

"We Didn't Know" is a duet with Stevie Wonder, who also produced it; he appears courtesy of Motown.

Clive Davis

The album's executive producer was Arista founder Clive Davis, who signed Whitney in 1983 and guided her whole career.
