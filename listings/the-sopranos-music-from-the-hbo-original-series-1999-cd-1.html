🎵 Did You Know?

The Theme Song

The opening theme, "Woke Up This Morning", is by A3 (Alabama 3), a band from Brixton, London. This CD has the "Chosen One Mix" heard over the opening credits.

Silvio on Guitar

Track 5 is by Little Steven & the Disciples of Soul. Steven Van Zandt, Bruce Springsteen's E Street Band guitarist, played Silvio Dante on the show.

Picked by David Chase

Series creator David Chase chose much of the show's music himself, and he is credited as a soundtrack executive producer with music supervisor Martin Bruestle.

Seasons One and Two

The album collects songs from the show's first two seasons and came out in December 1999. A second volume, Peppers & Eggs, followed in 2001.

Made in Canada

This copy is the Canadian pressing (CK 63911) by Sony Music Entertainment (Canada), Toronto, with bilingual credits on the back.
