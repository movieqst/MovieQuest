🎥 Did You Know?

A Lifelong Dream

Denis Villeneuve had dreamed of adapting Dune since first reading the novel at age 12, and waited until after Arrival and Blade Runner 2049 to take it on.

Awards

The film won six Academy Awards, including Best Cinematography, Best Original Score for Hans Zimmer, Best Sound and Best Visual Effects.

The Music

Hans Zimmer turned down Christopher Nolan's Tenet so he could score Dune, a novel he had loved since he was a teenager.

Filming Locations

The deserts of Arrakis were filmed in Jordan and Abu Dhabi, while the ocean world of Caladan was shot along the coast of Norway.
