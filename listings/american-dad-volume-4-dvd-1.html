🎥 Did You Know?

The Christmas Episode Left for Last

"The Most Adequate Christmas Ever" first aired on December 16, 2007 but was left off Volume 3. It closes out Volume 4 as the final episode on Disc 3.

A Two-Season Volume

The 14 episodes span the back half of the show's third season and the start of its fourth, which is why the set was released in two parts alongside Volume 3.

Uncensored Audio

"Tearjerker" and "Oedipal Panties" include an uncensored audio option on the DVDs, and all 14 episodes have audio commentary.

Hello, Steve Hely

"Red October Sky" was written by Steve Hely, who later wrote for The Office (US), 30 Rock and Veep. Its title mashes up the Cold War thriller The Hunt for Red October with the film October Sky.

Release Day

Fox released Volume 4 on April 28, 2009, with a suggested retail price of $39.98 US and $54.98 Canada. It shipped in a slim clear three-disc case.
