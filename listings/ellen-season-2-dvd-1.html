🎥 Did You Know?

Now Called Ellen

Season 2 was the first under the name Ellen. The show had premiered as These Friends of Mine, but was renamed to avoid confusion with NBC's new hit Friends.

A New Line-Up

Holly Fulger and Maggie Wheeler's Anita from Season 1 were gone, and Joely Fisher (Paige) and David Anthony Higgins (Joe) joined Ellen and Arye Gross's Adam.

Emmy-Nominated Spa

Ellen DeGeneres earned an Emmy nomination for "The Spa", in which Ellen ends up stuck on a wrought iron gate trying to escape a health resort.

American Gladiators

In "Gladiators", Ellen becomes a contestant on the real TV show American Gladiators and woos gladiator Nitro.

Thirty Kilo Man

Dan, the over-eager pizza guy from "The Thirty-Minute Man", returns for the two-part "Thirty Kilo Man" that closes out this set.
