🎥 Did You Know?

Born on The Practice

Alan Shore and Denny Crane were introduced in the final season of David E. Kelley's The Practice in 2004, and Boston Legal spun off with them that fall.

Emmy Winners

James Spader won the Emmy for Outstanding Lead Actor in a Drama for Alan Shore in 2005 and 2007, and William Shatner won Outstanding Supporting Actor in 2005, after winning a guest Emmy for playing Denny Crane on The Practice.

Scotch on the Balcony

Almost every episode closes with Denny and Alan on the office balcony with a scotch and a cigar, talking over the case and their friendship.

Till Death Do Us Part

In the series finale, Denny and Alan get married so Alan can care for Denny as his memory fails, the ultimate act of the show's famous friendship.

The Final Season

Season five was a shortened final run that wrapped up the series in December 2008, with the two-hour finale shown on ABC.
