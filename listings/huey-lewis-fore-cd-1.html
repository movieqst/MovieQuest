🎵 Did You Know?

A Number One Album

Fore! was released on August 20, 1986 and went to No. 1 on the Billboard 200. It was later certified triple platinum in the US.

Two Number One Singles

The album produced five top-10 hits on the Billboard Hot 100. "Stuck with You" and "Jacob's Ladder" both went all the way to No. 1.

A Hornsby Song

"Jacob's Ladder" was written by Bruce and John Hornsby. Huey Lewis was producing Bruce Hornsby's album, and when Hornsby didn't like his own band's arrangement, the song went to Huey Lewis and the News.

Hip to Be Square

Fore! and "Hip to Be Square" became famous all over again in the 2000 film American Psycho, where Patrick Bateman calls it "the group's most accomplished album".

Self-Produced

The album was produced by Huey Lewis and the News themselves, the follow-up to their 1983 blockbuster Sports.
