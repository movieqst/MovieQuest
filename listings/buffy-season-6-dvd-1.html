🎥 Did You Know?

Once More, with Feeling

Season six includes the musical episode "Once More, with Feeling", written and directed by Joss Whedon, who also wrote all of its original songs. The set has a behind-the-scenes featurette and karaoke music videos for it.

A New Network

After five seasons on The WB, Buffy moved to UPN for its sixth season.

Back from the Dead

The season opens with Buffy brought back to life by her friends after her sacrifice at the end of season five, and the quote on the back cover is her confession that she had been in heaven.

The Trio

Instead of one big villain, season six pits Buffy against the Trio, three nerdy wannabe supervillains: Warren, Jonathan and Andrew.

Dark Willow

Willow's growing addiction to magic builds to one of the series' most famous turns, when she becomes "Dark Willow" in the final episodes.
