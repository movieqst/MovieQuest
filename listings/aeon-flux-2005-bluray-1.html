🎥 Did You Know?

From MTV's Cult Cartoon

The film is based on Peter Chung's MTV animated series Æon Flux, which aired from 1991 to 1995.

Theron Hurt on Set

Charlize Theron was hospitalized with a neck injury during stunt work, pausing filming for about a month.

Shot in Germany

Though set in the walled city of Bregna in the year 2415, the film was shot in and around Berlin and Potsdam.

The Studio Recut

Director Karyn Kusama said Paramount heavily re-edited her version; her cut reportedly had nearly 30 more minutes of footage.

Creator Not Impressed

Series creator Peter Chung was openly critical of the film, saying his character "does not actually appear in the movie."
