🎥 Did You Know?

Three Books in One

The story combines Hergé's The Crab with the Golden Claws (1941), The Secret of the Unicorn (1943) and Red Rackham's Treasure (1944).

Spielberg Meets Hergé

Hergé and Spielberg admired each other's work; Spielberg took up the film rights after Hergé's death in 1983 and re-optioned them in 2002.

A Golden Globe First

It was the first motion-capture film, and the first non-Pixar film, to win the Golden Globe for Best Animated Feature.

An Oscar Nod for Williams

John Williams' score earned an Academy Award nomination for Best Original Score.

Spielberg and Jackson

Peter Jackson co-produced the film with Spielberg and Kathleen Kennedy; Weta Digital handled the visual effects and animation.
