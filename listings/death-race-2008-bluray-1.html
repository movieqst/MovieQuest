🎥 Did You Know?

Remake or Prequel?

It was sold as a remake of Roger Corman's cult classic Death Race 2000 (1975), but director Paul W.S. Anderson says in his commentary that he thought of it more as a prequel.

Frankenstein's Voice

David Carradine, who played Frankenstein in the 1975 original, returns for a voice cameo as Frankenstein.

Made in Montreal

Principal photography began in Montreal in August 2007.

A Franchise Is Born

Death Race earned about $75.7 million worldwide and led to three direct-to-video follow-ups: Death Race 2, Death Race 3: Inferno and Death Race: Beyond Anarchy.

Two Cuts on One Disc

This Blu-ray includes both the 1 hr 45 min theatrical cut and the 1 hr 51 min unrated cut; the director's commentary plays over the unrated version.
