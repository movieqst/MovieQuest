🎵 Did You Know?

The Film Everyone Will Be Toking About

The cover's famous tagline is a pun on the film's stoner humour; the little "Soundtrack" caret was added to the movie poster's line for the album.

Last Day of School, 1976

Richard Linklater's film follows a group of Texas teenagers on the last day of school in May 1976, and every song here is from that era.

Early Roles for Future Stars

The film gave early roles to Matthew McConaughey ("alright, alright, alright"), Ben Affleck, Milla Jovovich, Parker Posey and Adam Goldberg.

No Led Zeppelin

The movie is named after a Led Zeppelin song, but Linklater couldn't get the rights to use any Zeppelin music in it.

A Second Helping

The soundtrack sold so well that a follow-up, Even More Dazed and Confused, was released later in 1993.
