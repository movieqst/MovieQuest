🎥 Did You Know?

Five Decades of King

The set spans 36 years of Stephen King adaptations, from David Cronenberg's The Dead Zone (1983) to the 2019 remake of Pet Sematary.

Christopher Walken's Psychic

In The Dead Zone, Christopher Walken plays Johnny Smith, a teacher who wakes from a five-year coma able to see people's futures.

King Wrote His Own Scripts

Stephen King wrote the screenplays for Pet Sematary (1989) and Silver Bullet himself, and the teleplay for The Stand.

Six Hours of The Stand

The Stand is the full 1994 ABC mini-series, nearly six hours long (359 minutes), starring Gary Sinise, Molly Ringwald and Rob Lowe.

Corey Haim and Gary Busey

Silver Bullet (1985), based on King's Cycle of the Werewolf, stars a young Corey Haim as a boy in a wheelchair and Gary Busey as his Uncle Red.

Two Takes on Pet Sematary

Both versions of Pet Sematary are here: Mary Lambert's 1989 original, with Fred Gwynne as Jud, and the 2019 remake with Jason Clarke and John Lithgow.
