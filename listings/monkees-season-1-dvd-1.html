🎥 Did You Know?

Cast from 437 Hopefuls

Producers Bob Rafelson and Bert Schneider ran ads for "folk & roll musicians" in 1965 and saw around 400 applicants before choosing Micky Dolenz, Davy Jones, Michael Nesmith and Peter Tork.

Stephen Stills Didn't Make It

Stephen Stills auditioned but wasn't cast. He suggested his friend Peter Tork instead, then went on to form Buffalo Springfield, as the booklet in this set recalls.

Emmy Winner

The Monkees won the 1967 Emmy for Outstanding Comedy Series, and director James Frawley won for directing.

Last Train to Clarksville

The band's first single, "Last Train to Clarksville", hit No. 1 in 1966 and plays in many of these first-season episodes.

Monkees on Tour

The final episode, "Monkees on Tour", is a mini-documentary of the band's first concert tour, directed by Bob Rafelson.
