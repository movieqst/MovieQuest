🎥 Did You Know?

King's Favourite

After an early private screening, Stephen King left the room for about fifteen minutes to compose himself, then told Rob Reiner it was the best film ever made from his work. He has said the story is autobiographical.

Based on The Body

The film adapts King's 1982 novella The Body. Screenwriters Raynold Gideon and Bruce A. Evans were nominated for the Oscar for Best Adapted Screenplay.

The Real Castle Rock

Brownsville, Oregon stood in for the fictional town of Castle Rock. A year later Rob Reiner named his new production company, Castle Rock Entertainment, after it.

A Small-Budget Hit

Made for about $8 million, Stand by Me took in about $52 million at the box office in 1986.

The Title Song

The film is named after Ben E. King's 1961 hit "Stand by Me", which plays over the closing credits.
