🎥 Did You Know?

TV's First Prime-Time Cartoon

The Flintstones premiered on ABC on September 30, 1960, and was the first animated series to air in prime time, running six seasons until 1966.

Modelled on The Honeymooners

Hanna-Barbera based Fred and Barney's friendship and schemes on Jackie Gleason's sitcom The Honeymooners, moved to the Stone Age.

No "Meet the Flintstones" Yet

The famous theme song didn't arrive until season three. These first-season episodes open with the instrumental "Rise and Shine" by Hoyt Curtin.

Yabba Dabba Doo

Alan Reed, the voice of Fred, is credited with coming up with "Yabba dabba doo!". The script reportedly just said "Yahoo!".

Sponsored by Cigarettes

Winston cigarettes were an original sponsor, and early commercials showed Fred and Barney smoking, a reminder that the show was first aimed at adults.
