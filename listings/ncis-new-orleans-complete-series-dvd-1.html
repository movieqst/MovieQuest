🎥 Did You Know?

Born on NCIS

The series was launched by a two-part backdoor pilot, "Crescent City", in season 11 of NCIS in 2014. Both parts are included as special features.

Quantum Leap's Scott Bakula

Scott Bakula, of Quantum Leap and Star Trek: Enterprise, leads the team as Special Agent Dwayne "King" Pride.

Seven Seasons in the Big Easy

NCIS: New Orleans ran seven seasons on CBS from 2014 to 2021, 155 episodes in all, filmed on location in and around New Orleans.

Lucas Black and Vanessa Ferlito

Lucas Black, of Sling Blade and The Fast and the Furious: Tokyo Drift, played Agent Christopher LaSalle for the first six seasons.
