🎥 Did You Know?

Natural Light Only

With the exception of one campfire scene, the entire film was shot using only natural light.

Chasing Winter to Argentina

When the snow melted too early during the Canadian shoot, the production relocated to southern Argentina to keep filming in winter conditions.

Method in the Cold

DiCaprio, a vegetarian in real life, ate raw bison liver on camera and slept inside animal carcasses during the freezing shoot.

A Digital Grizzly

The bear attack sequence was created entirely through CGI, with stunt performers in harnesses standing in for the animal during filming.

Shot in Order

Director Iñárritu insisted on shooting the film in chronological order, believing it would help the cast's performances build naturally scene to scene.
