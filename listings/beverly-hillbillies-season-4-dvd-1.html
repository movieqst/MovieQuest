🎥 Did You Know?

American Gothic

The cover recreates Grant Wood's famous 1930 painting American Gothic, with Jed holding the pitchfork and Granny at his side.

Now in Colour

Season 4 (1965-1966) was the first season of The Beverly Hillbillies filmed in colour, after three seasons in black and white.

Flatt and Scruggs

Bluegrass legends Lester Flatt and Earl Scruggs, who played the show's theme "The Ballad of Jed Clampett", guest star as themselves in "Flatt and Scruggs Return" on Disc 4.

The Official Season

CBS released these "Official" season sets with every episode as originally broadcast, after years of incomplete public-domain discs of the early episodes.
