📝 Synopsis

Mia Wasikowska (Alice in Wonderland), Michael Fassbender (X-Men: First Class) and Academy Award winner Judi Dench (Shakespeare in Love) star in acclaimed director Cary Fukunaga's daring new adaptation of the timeless romance. When orphaned governess Jane Eyre (Wasikowska) arrives at imposing Thornfield Hall, she's intrigued by her brooding wealthy employer, Rochester (Fassbender). His dark moods and the strange occurrences in the house lead her to discover a terrible secret that he had hoped to hide from her forever.

🎥 Did You Know?

An Oscar Nod for the Costumes

Michael O'Connor's period costumes earned the film an Academy Award nomination for Best Costume Design.

Fukunaga's Second Feature

Director Cary Joji Fukunaga made Jane Eyre right after his debut Sin Nombre; he later directed True Detective and the Bond film No Time to Die.

From the 1847 Novel

Moira Buffini's screenplay adapts Charlotte Brontë's 1847 novel, telling Jane's story in flashback from the moment she flees Thornfield.

Score by an Oscar Winner

The music is by Dario Marianelli, who won the Oscar for scoring Atonement; a bonus feature on this disc follows him and Fukunaga at work on the score.

A Hidden Commentary

Besides the director's commentary, this disc hides an Easter-egg commentary with Rob Meyer and Ameer Youssef.
