The Good Witch (Hallmark Channel / 2008)

DVD / Hallmark Channel Movie / Catherine Bell / 2000s

📝 Synopsis

A mysterious yet charming woman arrives in a small town and causes a stir when people think she is an actual witch. The town's moral committee, led by the mayor's wife, becomes convinced she is evil and starts a campaign to drive her out, which puts the town sheriff, a struggling widower, in a tough spot as he slowly falls for her. As their relationship grows, she helps the sheriff's children and several members of the community face their problems. But is it common sense or magic that's helping the townsfolk?

🎥 Did You Know?

Start of a Franchise

The film was such a hit for the Hallmark Channel that it was followed by six sequel movies and then the TV series Good Witch, which ran for seven seasons from 2015 to 2021.

Grey Gables

Cassie Nightingale moves into Grey Gables, an old mansion the townspeople believe is haunted, and opens a shop called Bell, Book & Candle.

Made in Ontario

Although it is set in the small American town of Middleton, the film is a Canadian production that was shot in Ontario.

From JAG to Hallmark

Catherine Bell, best known for eight seasons as Lt. Col. Sarah MacKenzie on JAG, went on to play Cassie in every Good Witch movie and the series.
