🎵 Did You Know?

Diamond Certified

Greatest Hits has sold over 10 million copies in the US and was certified Diamond by the RIAA.

Album of the Decade

Billboard and Nielsen SoundScan named it the No. 1 catalog album of the decade for 2000 to 2010.

Two New Songs

The 1994 compilation included two songs not previously on a Seger album: a cover of Chuck Berry's "C'est La Vie" and the new song "In Your Time".

Cole Seger, 17 Months

The photo on the back cover is Bob Seger's young son Cole, credited as "Cole Seger – 17 Months".
