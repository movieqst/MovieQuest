📝 Synopsis

Miracles happen in unexpected places, even on death row at Cold Mountain Penitentiary. There John Coffey, a prisoner with supernatural powers, brings a sense of spirit and humanity to his guards and fellow inmates. Tom Hanks leads a stellar cast in this uplifting story of guards and captives, husbands and wives, prisoners and a mouse named Mr. Jingles. Frank Darabont returns after The Shawshank Redemption to adapt another Stephen King tale, nominated for four Academy Awards including Best Picture.

🎥 Did You Know?

Four Oscar Nominations

The Green Mile was nominated for four Academy Awards: Best Picture, Best Supporting Actor for Michael Clarke Duncan, Best Sound and Best Adapted Screenplay.

Darabont Returns to King

Frank Darabont followed his 1994 debut The Shawshank Redemption by adapting another Stephen King prison story.

A Serial Novel

King first published The Green Mile in 1996 as a serial novel, released in six slim monthly paperback instalments.

A Big Hit

Made for about $60 million, the film took in about $290 million at the box office.

Where It Was Filmed

The prison scenes were shot at Warner Hollywood Studios, with location filming in Shelbyville, Tennessee and Blowing Rock, North Carolina.
