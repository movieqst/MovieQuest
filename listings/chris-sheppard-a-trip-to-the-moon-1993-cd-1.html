📝 About This Album

Level 4: a compilation of progressive house, trance and hardcore, compiled by Canadian DJ Chris Sheppard. Sixteen early-'90s UK and European club and rave tracks, from Nu-Civilisation's "Wake Up" and House Of Gypsies' "Samba" to Coco, Steel & Lovebomb on Warp Records, Acen's rave classic "Trip II The Moon" and Alpha Team's "Speed Racer". A snapshot of the 1992 to 1993 Toronto club scene that Sheppard helped build.

🎵 Did You Know?

Canada's Dance DJ

Chris Sheppard was one of the leading figures in Canadian dance music in the 1980s and 1990s, spinning in Toronto clubs and hosting a nationally syndicated dance radio show.

Before Love Inc.

A few years after this compilation, Sheppard co-founded the Eurodance group Love Inc. with Brad Daymond and singer Simone Denny. Their hit "Broken Bones" won Best Video at the 1998 MuchMusic Video Awards.

Where the Title Comes From

Track 15, "Trip II The Moon" by Acen, was a 1992 UK top-40 hit and is regarded as a classic of early-'90s breakbeat hardcore. The Guardian ranked it the second-best track of that era.

Made in Canada

The CD was compiled by Chris Sheppard and Markus Klinke and released in 1993 by Quality Music & Video of Scarborough, Ontario (catalogue QCD 2020).
