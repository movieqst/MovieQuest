📝 Synopsis

Originally aired as These Friends of Mine, this rarely seen debut season of Ellen stars Ellen DeGeneres as mildly neurotic Los Angeles bookseller Ellen Morgan, whose nervous motor-mouthing often gets her in trouble, especially with her best friends Adam (Arye Gross), Anita (Maggie Wheeler) and Holly (Holly Fulger). Whether working at Buy the Book or socializing with her friends, Ellen gets herself in buckets of hot water whenever she can't stop talking!

🎥 Did You Know?

These Friends of Mine

The show premiered on ABC on March 29, 1994 as These Friends of Mine. It was renamed Ellen to avoid confusion with NBC's Friends, which debuted that September.

Two Lost Episodes Found

"The Tape" and "The Mugging" were filmed with Season 1 but didn't air until Season 3. This set restores them to the season where they belong.

A Rarely Seen Season

Holly Fulger appeared only in this first season, and Maggie Wheeler's Anita disappeared after it too, which is why this season looks so different from the rest of the show.

Five Seasons on ABC

Ellen ran for five seasons, ending in 1998. Its 1997 "Puppy Episode" became one of the most talked-about episodes in TV history.

Buy the Book

Ellen Morgan works at Buy the Book, a Los Angeles bookstore, the setting for much of the show's comedy.
