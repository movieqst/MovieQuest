🎵 Did You Know?

Best-Selling Soundtrack

Saturday Night Fever spent 24 straight weeks at No. 1 on the Billboard 200 and was for years the best-selling soundtrack album of all time, with over 40 million copies sold.

Album of the Year

It won the Grammy for Album of the Year in 1979, the first soundtrack ever to win it.

Written Without Seeing the Film

The Bee Gees wrote most of their songs for it in a weekend at a French chateau before they had seen any of the film; producer Robert Stigwood asked for them.

Four No. 1 Hits

"Stayin' Alive", "How Deep Is Your Love", "Night Fever" and Yvonne Elliman's "If I Can't Have You" all hit No. 1 in the US from this soundtrack.

A Beethoven Disco Hit

"A Fifth of Beethoven", Walter Murphy's disco take on Beethoven's Fifth Symphony, topped the Billboard Hot 100 in 1976.
