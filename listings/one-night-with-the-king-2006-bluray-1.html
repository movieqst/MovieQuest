📝 Synopsis

With lush cinematography, a mesmerizing score and an all-star cast that includes Peter O'Toole, Omar Sharif and John Rhys-Davies, One Night with the King is a sweeping epic about Hadassah, the young Jewish orphan who becomes the biblical Esther, Queen of Persia. Born into poverty, Hadassah (Tiffany Dupont) grows up to catch the eye of the powerful King Xerxes and becomes his bride. But when the state decrees that all Jews will be put to death, she struggles to save her people while hiding her own heritage, in an inspiring story about courage, faith and destiny.

🎥 Did You Know?

The Story of Esther

The film retells the biblical Book of Esther, in which a young Jewish orphan named Hadassah becomes Queen of Persia and risks her life to save her people.

Based on a Novel

The screenplay was adapted from Tommy Tenney and Mark Andrew Olsen's novel Hadassah: One Night with the King.

Shot in India

The film was shot in Rajasthan, India, whose palaces and forts stood in for the ancient Persian court of King Xerxes.

Two Screen Legends

Peter O'Toole and Omar Sharif, who starred together in Lawrence of Arabia (1962), both appear in supporting roles.
