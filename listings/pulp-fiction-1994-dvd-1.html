🎥 Did You Know?

Awards

Pulp Fiction won the Palme d'Or at the 1994 Cannes Film Festival, was nominated for seven Academy Awards including Best Picture, and won Best Original Screenplay for Quentin Tarantino and Roger Avary.

Box Office

Made for about $8 million, it became the first independent film to earn more than $200 million worldwide.

Mystery

The contents of Marsellus Wallace's glowing briefcase are never revealed. Fans have argued about it for decades, and Tarantino has said there is no answer.

Comeback

The role of Vincent Vega revived John Travolta's career and earned him his first Oscar nomination since Saturday Night Fever.
