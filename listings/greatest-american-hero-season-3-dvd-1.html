📝 Synopsis

Believe it or not, he's walking on air again! It's the third and final season for reluctant superhero Ralph Hinkley (William Katt), his lawyer girlfriend Pam Davidson (Connie Sellecca) and by-the-book FBI agent Bill Maxwell (Robert Culp), but the high-flying fun is far from over! In this season, Ralph is reunited with the 'green guys' from space, stumbles into danger at his high school reunion, fumbles his way to his and Pam's wedding, and tangles with doomed rockers, ruthless ranchers, strange gamers, killer magicians, the KGB, Central American revolutionaries, a genetic Nazi monster and more. All 13 third-season episodes of the action-comedy hit created by Stephen J. Cannell.

🎥 Did You Know?

Believe It or Not

Joey Scarbury's theme song "Believe It or Not" reached No. 2 on the Billboard Hot 100 in 1981, one of the biggest TV theme hits ever.

Ralph Hanley, Briefly

After a would-be presidential assassin named John Hinckley made headlines in 1981, the hero's surname Hinkley was changed to "Hanley" for part of season one, then quietly restored.

The Lost Instruction Book

Ralph loses the suit's instruction manual in the pilot, which is why he spends the series crash-landing and figuring out its powers by trial and error.

André the Giant Guest Stars

This final season features guest appearances by André the Giant, Bob Saget, David Paymer, June Lockhart and radio DJ Rick Dees.

Stephen J. Cannell

Creator Stephen J. Cannell also gave TV The Rockford Files, The A-Team, 21 Jump Street, Hunter and The Commish.
