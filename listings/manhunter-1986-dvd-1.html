📝 Synopsis

The unforgettable debut of Hannibal Lecter. FBI agent Will Graham (William Petersen) has captured the diabolical Dr. Hannibal Lecter (Brian Cox), nearly losing more than just his mind in the process. But when Graham is called out of retirement to hunt the psychopath known as "The Tooth Fairy" (Tom Noonan), he must once again confront the horrors of Hannibal the Cannibal. Joan Allen, Dennis Farina, Kim Greist and Stephen Lang co-star in this shocking thriller directed by Michael Mann and adapted from the novel Red Dragon by Thomas Harris.

🎥 Did You Know?

The First Hannibal Lecter

Brian Cox played Hannibal Lecter (spelled "Lecktor" in the film) five years before Anthony Hopkins made the role famous in The Silence of the Lambs.

Red Dragon on Screen

Manhunter is the first film adaptation of Thomas Harris's novel Red Dragon, which was filmed again in 2002 with Anthony Hopkins and Edward Norton.

Michael Mann's Style

Director Michael Mann was also producing Miami Vice at the time, and Manhunter shares its bold colour palette, synth soundtrack and cool 1980s look.

A Name Change

The title was changed from Red Dragon to Manhunter, reportedly because producer Dino De Laurentiis's previous film Year of the Dragon had flopped.

From Will Graham to Grissom

William Petersen, who plays FBI profiler Will Graham, later starred as Gil Grissom on CSI: Crime Scene Investigation.
