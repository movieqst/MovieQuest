# Vukovar: free pictures and documents to look for

**The rule:** only public domain, CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA can go on the page. Anything else becomes a link card ("See also"), never a download.
**Be warned:** almost every photo taken in Vukovar in 1991 is still under copyright (news agencies, Croatian and Serbian photographers, the UN, the ICTY). Free pictures of 1991 are rare. Most of what we can use is US government material, Wikimedia Commons photos with free licences, and photos of memorials and museum objects taken later.
**When you find one:** download the largest size and send it with the link to its page, so I can check the licence and credit it.

## D. Court and UN documents (text and links)
- **ICTY, Mrkšić et al. Trial Judgement (2007)**: icty.org/x/cases/mrksic/tjug/en/070927.pdf. Quote the text; the exhibit photos are not free.
- **ICTY case information sheet** (with a small map): icty.org/x/cases/mrksic/cis/en/cis_mrksic_al_en.pdf. Link card.
- **UN Security Council resolution 1037 (1996)**, the UNTAES mission: digitallibrary.un.org/record/202323. Link card.
- **MSF, *Speaking Out: the war in the former Yugoslavia*** (convoy minutes): msf.org PDF. Link card.

## E. Museums (photos only if they carry a free licence, or link cards)
- **Vukovar City Museum** (Eltz Castle): muzej-vukovar.hr. Its collection was looted in 1991 and returned from Serbia in 2001: a good story for the debrief, as a link.
- **Memorial Centre of the Homeland War, Vukovar** (the old barracks): outdoor tanks, guns and an aircraft. Commons photos of the outdoor displays are usually fine (freedom of panorama in Croatia).
- **Place of Memory – Vukovar Hospital 1991**: link card.
- **Croatian Memorial-Documentation Centre of the Homeland War** (centardomovinskograta.hr): books and photos are copyright; link cards. Its book *The Town Was the Target* (2011) is a free PDF to read (we use it), not to copy pictures from.
- **Humanitarian Law Center, Belgrade** (hlc-rdc.org) and **Youth Initiative for Human Rights** (yihr.rs): reports to link, for the balanced debrief.

## F. Books to read or link (not to copy pictures from)
