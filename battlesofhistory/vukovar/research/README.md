## Notes on the map (for the battle map and the chapters)
- Three front lines: 14 September (wide ring from Vinkovci to the Danube), 30 September (Vukovar and Borovo Naselje cut off from Vinkovci except by a corn-field track), 10 November (Borovo and Vukovar split apart).
- JNA: Operational Group "North" (from 30 September), "South" (from 30 September); 1st Guards Mechanized Division brigades; 51st, 18th, 211th, 252nd, 453rd brigades. Croatian: 204th Brigade inside Vukovar; 109th Brigade at Vinkovci; "Elms/3 Guards" (3rd Guards Brigade elements) at Nuštar.
- Dated arrows: 14 September, 30 September, 16 October (several), 3 November, 8 November.
- Ovčara is at the bottom right of the map (about 5 km south-east of the town).
- Colours on our page follow the site rule, not the CIA's: the first-named side (JNA) red, the second (Croatian forces) blue.
- *Balkan Battlegrounds* is itself public domain (CIA, 2002): a main source for the narration, to be balanced with Croatian and Serbian accounts and the ICTY judgements (Mrkšić et al., the "Vukovar Three").
