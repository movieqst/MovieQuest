# Battle of Vukovar, 1991: sprites for the map (art prompts for ChatGPT)

**How to send them back:**
- One sheet per picture, at least 1024 × 1024, each as its own PNG.
- Name each file with the **bold name**, and send them in small zips.
- No contact sheets or previews: those are too small to cut.

**Balance and respect:**
- Every side is drawn with the same care, as real people, capable and tired. No caricature, no stereotype, no gore.
- No flags, badges, emblems, slogans, crosses, chequerboards, double-headed eagles or red stars on anyone or anything. The map's colours (JNA red, Croatian defenders blue) tell the sides apart, and so do the uniforms.
- Nothing on the map shows the prisoners at Ovčara, the buses, or anyone being mistreated. The debrief tells that part in words only.

## Part 1: soldiers and vehicles

**Accuracy for August–November 1991** (from the research; each "check" item is still to confirm against photos):
- **Season and ground:** late summer into autumn, then early winter. Flat farmland of harvested maize stubble, muddy tracks, rows of poplars, the brick and plaster houses of a Central European town, rubble and broken roof tiles.
- **The JNA:** olive-grey ("SMB") uniforms of the Yugoslav army, the M59/85 steel helmet (rounded, close to the head), canvas webbing, and the Zastava M70 rifle (the Yugoslav AK, with a wooden stock and often a folding stock). Vehicles in one flat olive-green. Many soldiers were young conscripts or reservists called up in 1991.
- **The Territorial Defence and volunteers:** a mix of JNA olive-grey, older uniforms and civilian clothes, some with camouflage jackets, Zastava M70 rifles. Not a unit uniform. (Check: this mixed look is described in the ICTY judgement and photos of 1991; do not draw any one group's insignia.)
- **The Croatian defenders:** no single uniform in 1991. Blue-grey police uniforms (MUP), surplus camouflage jackets in woodland or other patterns, olive JNA-style trousers, jeans and sports shoes, civilian jackets. Many wore no helmet, some JNA-type helmets. Rifles: Zastava M70 and Hungarian AMD-65 (with a short barrel and a front grip). (Check: the AMD-65 against 1991 photos of Vukovar.)
- **Anti-tank weapons:** hand-held, short, tube-shaped disposable rocket launchers. Draw them generically. (Our sources do not yet confirm the exact model, M79 "Osa" or M80 "Zolja", for Vukovar.)
- **Avoid:**
  - flags and insignia of any kind (see above);
  - modern gear: M4 or AR-15 rifles, modern plate carriers, kevlar helmets, digital camouflage, night-vision goggles, drones;
  - weapons and vehicles not of 1991: T-72 with modern reactive armour, BMP-3, RPG-29, Javelin;
  - heroic poses, swagger, cruelty, executions, prisoners, bodies in the street, blood;
  - readable text or numbers;
  - bright green (#00FF00) anywhere on figures or vehicles: paint all olive in dark olive.

## Part 2: leaders (the painted figures on the map)

**Faces:** if you find a photo with a free licence (Wikimedia Commons: public domain, CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA), attach it and paint the face from it. If not, paint a believable face from the description only. It is a figure on a map, not a portrait.
