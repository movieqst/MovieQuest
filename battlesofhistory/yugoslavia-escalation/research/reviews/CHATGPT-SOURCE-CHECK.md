## Omissions and competing accounts

Include Kosovo Albanian delegates’ July 1990 declaration and the subsequent dissolution of Kosovo’s assembly. That gives the audience a political response to the autonomy rollback. Include Slovenia’s retention of a defence structure and the March Presidency split in the main chronology. At the cutoff, identify the June 25 votes as declarations and save the Brioni suspension and Croatia’s October severance for the next page. [ICMP Kosovo history](https://www.icmp.int/wp-content/uploads/2017/05/Kosovo-stocktaking-ENG.pdf), [Slovenian chronology](https://www.slovenija2001.gov.si/10years/path/chronology/), [Sabor independence chronology](https://www.sabor.hr/en/about-parliament/history/important-dates/25-june-independence-day)

- **Serbian leadership:** Milošević cast Serbian constitutional consolidation as restoring unity after provincial autonomy had divided authority. [Gazimestan speech exhibit](https://ucr.irmct.org/LegalRef/CMSDocStore/Public/BCS/Submission/NotIndexable/IT-05-87/MRA11809R0000285550.pdf)
- **Croatian government:** The referendum decree proposed sovereignty with explicit guarantees of cultural autonomy and civic rights for Serbs and other nationalities. **Krajina Serb leaders** asserted collective sovereignty and autonomy and treated Croatia’s constitutional shift as a loss of status. [Decree](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/1991_05_21_646.html), [Martić judgment](https://www.worldcourts.com/icty/eng/decisions/2007.06.12_Prosecutor_v_Martic_2.pdf)
- **Slovenian leadership:** The Slovenian government presented independence as implementing a democratic mandate after negotiations failed to produce an acceptable relationship. [Government chronology](https://www.slovenija2001.gov.si/10years/path/chronology/)
- **Kosovo Albanian account:** Kosovo Albanian activists regarded the 1989 vote as coerced and the reduction in autonomy as a denial of self-government. Federal authorities described suppression of the demonstrations as responding to violence against police. [Kosovo movement archive](https://uckarchive.org/en/history/1981-1989), [Federal reply to UN rapporteur](https://iranhrdc.org/en/wp-content/uploads/pdf_en/UN_Reports/Amos%20Wako/E.CN.4.1991.36--Wako%20Report%201991.pdf)
- **JNA leadership:** Army leaders presented emergency measures as a way to prevent interethnic violence and protect the federation. Croatian accounts describe JNA interventions as obstructing Croatian police and increasingly protecting Serb-held areas. [ICJ case submission](https://icj-web.leman.un-icc.cloud/sites/default/files/case-related/118/14522.pdf), [Martić judgment](https://www.worldcourts.com/icty/eng/decisions/2007.06.12_Prosecutor_v_Martic_2.pdf)

For the debrief, Susan Woodward emphasizes debt, austerity and weakened federal capacity. V. P. Gagnon emphasizes elites using conflict to demobilize domestic challengers. Dejan Jović emphasizes an ideological and constitutional weakening of federal authority. These explanations overlap. Discuss the SANU draft as evidence that its arguments circulated, while treating claims that it directed later policy as interpretive. The causes of Kosovo Serb and Montenegrin emigration also require disaggregation by location, period and evidence.
