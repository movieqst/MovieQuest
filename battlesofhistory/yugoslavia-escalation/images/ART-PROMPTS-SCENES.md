# Yugoslavia: Escalation, 1980–1991: scenes and the title card, one per chat (19 November 2026)

These are the painted scenes for the slideshow (the script's `art-*` slides). **Owner's rule:** real people appear only in
real photographs, never in these paintings. Every scene is **empty of people**, with **no readable text, flags, emblems or
party symbols**. The slide mount is the Ukraine page's.

**How:**
1. Open a NEW chat in ChatGPT for each picture.
2. Paste the text as one message.
3. Download the result as PNG and send it to Claude. Landscape 3:2 (1536 x 1024) fits the 35mm slides.
