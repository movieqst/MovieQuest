# Yugoslavia: Escalation, 1980–1991: the three painted maps, one per chat (19 November 2026)

Made by `tools/draw-maps.py` from real elevation data. The Europe map is the Ukraine page's. **The paintings show land and
water only:** no borders, names or roads. The page draws the borders of the republics and provinces, and the names, on the
projector slides.

**How (for each map):**
1. Open a NEW chat in ChatGPT.
2. Attach `<map>-base.png` first, then `<map>-guide.png`.
3. Paste the text for that map as one message.
4. If ChatGPT offers **Edit** on the first picture, use it on the whole picture.
5. Download the result as PNG and send it to Claude.

If the coast comes back in the wrong place, reply: "The coastline is in the wrong place. Keep it exactly where it is in the
first picture."
