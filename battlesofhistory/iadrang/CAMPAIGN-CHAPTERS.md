# Ia Drang: the new opening, "The Pleiku campaign" (DRAFT for review)

A new first part of the reenactment, played on the new campaign map (Pleiku to the Chu Pong, 83 x 62 km).
It runs before LZ X-Ray and ends by flying the viewer into the X-Ray map. About 3 minutes at normal speed.

Each chapter has: **the narration** (what the voices will read, so this is the text to approve), the date shown on
screen, and **what the map shows**. Tags work as in the rest of the battle: established, us (American account),
pavn (Vietnamese account), estimate, reconstruction.

The intro slide "Plei Me" would become shorter, because the siege is now shown, not only told.

---

## 1. The trap  · 19 October 1965, evening
**Narration:**
> Plei Me was a small Special Forces camp in the hills south of Pleiku: twelve American Green Berets, fourteen South
> Vietnamese, and about four hundred Montagnard militia, behind a triangle of earth walls and sandbags. On the
> evening of 19 October, two North Vietnamese regiments moved into position around it. The 33rd would attack the
> camp. The 32nd would wait beside the road from Pleiku, to ambush the column that would come to save it.

**Map:** the camp at Plei Me; the 33rd Regiment closes round it from the west and south; the 32nd moves to the road
north-east of the camp and goes to ground. Pleiku and Camp Holloway at the top. *Tags: established.*

## 2. The siege  · 19–20 October, night
**Narration:**
> Soon after dark a Montagnard patrol ran into the enemy, and at midnight the attack began: mortars, recoilless
> rifles, and waves of infantry against the wire. The defenders held. Through the night and the days that
> followed, flareships lit the camp and A-1 Skyraiders bombed and strafed the attackers close to the walls.

**Map:** night; flares over the camp; mortar bursts on the triangle; assaults on the walls; Skyraiders diving from
the east. *Tags: established (the attack and the air strikes); the hour of the main assault differs between sources.*

## 3. Help from the air  · 21–22 October
**Narration:**
> On the morning of 21 October helicopters landed Major Charles Beckwith with a Special Forces reaction team and two
> companies of South Vietnamese Rangers a few kilometres north-east of the camp. They fought their way in through the
> jungle and reached the walls. Beckwith took command, and the camp was still surrounded.

**Map:** Hueys from Pleiku land the force north-east of Plei Me; Rangers move to the camp. *Tags: established.*

## 4. The relief column  · 22–23 October
**Narration:**
> Down the road from Pleiku came the relief column: about twelve hundred men under Lieutenant Colonel Nguyen Trong
> Luat, with M41 tanks, M113 armoured carriers, two Ranger battalions and artillery. American guns of the newly
> arrived 1st Cavalry Division moved forward to cover it. This was the column the 32nd Regiment was waiting for.

**Map:** the column (M113 sprites) leaves Pleiku, turns off Route 14 onto the camp road; American artillery sets up
beside the road. *Tags: established; the size of the column is given as 1,200 or 1,400.*

## 5. The ambush  · 23 October, evening
**Narration:**
> In the evening, a few kilometres short of the camp, the 32nd Regiment struck the column from both sides of the road.
> Tanks and carriers fired back point-blank into the jungle while the American artillery and the aircraft hammered the
> ambush. The column held, but it stopped where it was, and for a day and a half it would not move.

**Map:** the ambush on the camp road; fire from both sides; artillery and air strikes on the ambushers.
*Tags: established; the exact ambush site is approximate.*

## 6. The siege is lifted  · 25 October
**Narration:**
> On the 25th, promised American artillery all the way, Luat's column pushed on and reached Plei Me that evening.
> The North Vietnamese had already begun to pull back west, toward the Chu Pong mountain and the Cambodian border.
> The camp had held for six days.

**Map:** the column reaches the camp; the 32nd and 33rd Regiments withdraw west. *Tags: established.*

## 7. The pursuit  · 26 October – 9 November
**Narration:**
> General Westmoreland let the 1st Cavalry Division do what it had been built for. Instead of waiting for the enemy,
> its helicopters went after him. Day after day the brigades leap-frogged west by air, searching the jungle and the
> grass for the regiments that had attacked Plei Me.

**Map:** many Huey flights fan out west from Plei Me and Pleiku; landing zones appear and vanish; search lines move
west. *Tags: established.*

## 8. The hospital  · 1 November
**Narration:**
> On 1 November the cavalry found the field hospital of the 33rd Regiment hidden in the forest, and overran it after
> a hard fight. They took prisoners, weapons and documents, and the documents showed that the enemy regiments were
> gathering near the Chu Pong.

**Map:** helicopters land near the hospital; a sharp fight; the 33rd Regiment's remnants pull west. *Tags:
established; the exact place is approximate.*

## 9. Ambush in the valley  · 3–4 November
**Narration:**
> Two days later the cavalry's own scouts laid an ambush by the Ia Drang near the Cambodian border, and caught a
> column of a fresh North Vietnamese regiment, the 66th, which had just walked in down the Ho Chi Minh trail. That
> night the North Vietnamese counter-attacked the little landing zone, and reinforcements were flown in to hold it.

**Map:** the ambush near the border; night counter-attack on the landing zone; helicopters bring reinforcements.
*Tags: us (the American account; the Vietnamese account differs).*

## 10. The new brigade  · 9–13 November
**Narration:**
> On 9 November the 3rd Brigade took over the hunt. The prisoners and the documents pointed to the Chu Pong, a long
> forested mountain on the border. On 13 November the brigade commander gave Lieutenant Colonel Hal Moore his orders:
> take his battalion into the Ia Drang valley, at the foot of the mountain, and find the enemy.

**Map:** the 3rd Brigade at the artillery bases (LZ Falcon); the Chu Pong massif highlighted; the X-Ray clearing
picked out. *Tags: established.*

## 11. Into the valley  · 14 November, 10:48
**Narration:**
> At 10:48 on 14 November the first sixteen Hueys came down into a clearing at the foot of the Chu Pong. The clearing
> was called X-Ray. On the mountain above it were the three battalions of the 66th Regiment and part of the 33rd.

**Map:** Huey flight from the east into X-Ray, the camera zooms down into the clearing, and the reenactment switches
to the X-Ray map. *Tags: established.*

## Still to check before recording
- **The hour of the first big assault on Plei Me:** sources say around 19:00 (the patrol clash) or midnight.
- **The ambush site:** "about five miles from Plei Me, kilometre 4 of Route 6C" (Vietnamese-history account). I'll
  place it there, marked approximate.
- **The camp commander:** some accounts name the Special Forces captain at Plei Me as Harold Moore. If that holds, it
  is a striking coincidence with Hal Moore of X-Ray, but I have not confirmed it, so the text leaves it out.
- **The figures and units in chapter 9:** the LZ Mary ambush figures come from American accounts only.
