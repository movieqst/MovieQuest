# Métis Fiddling for Dancing (music for Fish Creek and Batoche)

From the album **"Métis Fiddling for Dancing"** by **Alex Kusturok, Donny L'Hirondelle and Garry Pruden**, presented by the **Métis Nation of Alberta (Otipemisiwak Métis Government)**.
Recorded at Astromonical Studio, St. Paul, Alberta; engineered by Calvin Vollrath. Produced by Alex Kusturok and Calvin Vollrath; executive producer, the Métis Nation of Alberta. Fiddle: Alex Kusturok, Donny L'Hirondelle, Garry Pruden. Acoustic, electric and bass guitars, mandolin and spoons: Calvin Vollrath. Clogging: Alex Kusturok.

**Terms:** the album page says it is "FREE to anyone to use for educational purposes, for dancing, for listening, and most importantly, for the preservation of the Métis dance music." Used here on that basis, on free educational history pages, with full credit and links. Not a Creative Commons licence: if the artists or the Métis Nation of Alberta ask, the music comes off.
