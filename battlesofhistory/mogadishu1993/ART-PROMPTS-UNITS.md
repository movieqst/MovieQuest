# Mogadishu 1993: sprite sheets, one per chat (17 November 2026)

Each sheet goes to ChatGPT in its own NEW chat, with one self-contained prompt, the same way as Somalia 1988.
Equipment from the research notes: Task Force Ranger flew MH-60 Black Hawks and MH-6/AH-6 Little Birds of the 160th
SOAR; the rescue convoy of 3-4 October had US Humvees and trucks, Malaysian Condor carriers and Pakistani M48 tanks
(both in UN white); Aidid's militia fought in civilian dress with AK-47s, RPG-7s and pickup "technicals". Owner's rules:
no graphic violence, no one hurt, nothing copied from the film. No Canadian sheets yet: the uniforms and vehicle paint
of the Canadian Airborne Regiment at Belet Huen must be checked against photos first, and the Canada part is told with
maps and photos. Cut with tools/prepare-sprite-sheet.py (people 160 px cells, vehicles 240 x 200, aircraft fit).
