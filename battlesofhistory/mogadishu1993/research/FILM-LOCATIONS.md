# Black Hawk Down (2001): where it was filmed

Relayed by ChatGPT on 17 November 2026 from the film's production notes (production designer Arthur Max, as published on
cinema.com), with the Kénitra airbase also confirmed by the American Film Institute (AFI) catalogue. **Not yet checked by us
against the notes themselves.** No building-level coordinates are established: the comparison map shows districts and
streets, never precise pins presented as confirmed. Arthur Max acknowledged that the street layout and the target building
were adapted for filming, so the film is not a guide to the real 1993 geography.

| Filming location | What it stood for | How precisely confirmed |
|---|---|---|
| Sidi Moussa, Salé | much of the film's Mogadishu streetscape | district confirmed; extensive set dressing |
| Avenue Nasser, Sidi Moussa, Salé | Hawlwadig Road and the raid scenes around it | street named |
| a football field near Avenue Nasser | the target building raided by the Americans | built for the film; exact plot not established |
| Hay Arrahma, Salé | the first helicopter crash site and the "Alamo" defensive complex | built around unfinished housing; neighbourhood confirmed |
| a courtyard off Rue des Consuls, Rabat medina | the Bakara Market | street confirmed; courtyard not identified |
| Royal Moroccan Air Force base, Kénitra (about 35 km north) | the American base: airfield, hangars, barracks | high: production notes and AFI |
| Mehdiya beach, near Kénitra | a military firing range | production notes |
| an abandoned warehouse in Salé's industrial zone | the Joint Operations Center | area confirmed; building not |
| another site in Salé, beside an existing mosque | the second helicopter crash site | production notes; neighbourhood not specified |
| an undeveloped section of Sidi Moussa | the closing "Mogadishu Mile" run | district confirmed; route not |

**For the page:** the real 1993 places go on the Mogadishu maps; the filming places go on the Rabat–Salé comparison map as
shaded districts and named streets, labelled "approximate".
