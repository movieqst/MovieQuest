# Granicus and Issus: sprite sheets, one per chat (17 November 2026)

Each sheet goes to ChatGPT in its own NEW chat, with one self-contained prompt, the same way as Mogadishu and Medak.
A new, realistic set (Gaugamela's older sheets use team colours and short spears). From the corrected research
(RESEARCH-CHATGPT-1.md, reviewed): the phalanx with the sarissa, the hypaspists, the Companion and Thessalian cavalry and the
Agrianians on Alexander's side; Persian noble cavalry, Greek mercenaries, Persian infantry ("Kardakes", equipment debated)
and archers on Darius's side; Darius in his chariot (Arrian 2.11.4) and Alexander on Bucephalus, black with a white star
(Arrian 5.19.5). No stirrups or saddles in 334-333 BC. Equipment details follow modern reconstructions and are tagged as
our reconstruction on the page. No one hurt, no blood.
Cut with tools/prepare-sprite-sheet.py (foot soldiers 160 px cells, horsemen and the chariot 240 x 200).

```text
Make ONE sprite sheet for an educational history game about Alexander the Great's battles of the Granicus (334 BC) and Issus (333 BC), seen from above at an angle (three-quarter view, like a classic real-time strategy game).

SUBJECT: A Macedonian phalanx infantryman (pezhetairos) of 334 BC: a bronze helmet (Phrygian style, with cheek pieces), a linen corslet (linothorax) in off-white with a short pleated skirt, bronze greaves, a small round bronze-faced shield hung from the neck and shoulder, sandal-boots, carrying the very long pike called the sarissa (about twice his height, about 5 to 6 metres) held UPRIGHT while marching.
```
