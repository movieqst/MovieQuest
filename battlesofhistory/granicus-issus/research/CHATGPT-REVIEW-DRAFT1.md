# Granicus and Issus — draft 1 review for Claude

**17 November 2026**  
**Reviewed:** the three files inside `GRANICUS-ISSUS-FOR-CHATGPT.zip`, plus the separately attached `GRANICUS-ISSUS-SCRIPT-DRAFT1.txt`. The separate script and the ZIP's `NARRATION-SCRIPT.md` are identical.

**Verdict: not quite ready to record.** The earlier research corrections are substantially applied. The script is respectful, generally accurate and suitable for ages 12+. Make the limited Required wording and attribution changes below; a wholesale rewrite is unnecessary.

This is a review of the supplied narration and revised research, using the earlier source audit and renewed checks of the passages affected by the draft. It is not a completed specialist survey of the still-unavailable Bosworth, Briant and Heckel books, an archaeological map certification, or inspection of the finished website's numerical tables.

**Tag rule — Required editorial change:** the definition “The ancient writers agree” is not by itself sufficient for **established**. Different writers may depend on the same lost source or repeat a literary story. Replace the definition with: “Secure broad chronology or facts supported by the evidence as a whole. Agreement between ancient writers is considered alongside their sources and reliability.” This changes the editorial rule, not every existing established tag. Where a line says “scholarship,” retain the named work and passage in its source data even if the narrator does not read the citation.

## Research and production items still open

1. **Actual on-screen numerical tables:** the script describes which tables will appear, but does not include their finished contents. The revised dossier's ledger still lacks some Justin/Plutarch/Polybius additions promised in that note. Either populate those rows from the earlier review's source-separated ledger, or shorten the promised source list. This review does not approve numerical graphics not supplied for inspection.
2. **Modern numerical ranges:** still unverified at book-page level. The script appropriately avoids inventing a replacement Persian headcount. That open scholarly task does not prevent recording this cautious narration.
3. **Maps:** no final map images or georeferenced unit overlays are in this ZIP. The Payas hypothesis label and Granicus site caveats are appropriate, but painted accuracy and route positions are not assessed here. Keep the main unit motions labelled reconstruction.
4. **Research source register:** add Badian's “Granicus” and “Darius III,” Devine's 1986 Granicus article, and Körpe 2025 as proper entries because the script now relies on them. A bare `scholarship` tag cannot substitute for those records.
5. **Tag dependencies:** chapter 8's higher ground and chapter 26's seven months illustrate why a source tag must attach to the actual clause rather than to a composite scene. Most other narration attributions correctly preserve this distinction.

## Source links for the fixes

- **B — Age:** Pierre Briant, *Alexander the Great and His Empire: A Short Introduction*, Introduction, **p. 1**, [publisher excerpt](https://assets.press.princeton.edu/chapters/i9178.pdf), gives July 356 birth. [Plutarch 3.5, 11.1](https://penelope.uchicago.edu/Thayer/E/Roman/Texts/Plutarch/Lives/Alexander*/3.html) provides ancient birth/accession traditions.
