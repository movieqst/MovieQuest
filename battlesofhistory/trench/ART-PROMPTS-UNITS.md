# Battle of the Trench, Medina, 627 AD: art prompts for ChatGPT

**How to send them back:**
- One sheet per picture, at least 1024 × 1024, each as its own PNG.
- Name each file with the **bold name**, and send them in small zips.
- No contact sheets or previews: those are too small to cut.

**Style for every sheet (paste once at the top of the chat):**
> Painterly, realistic sprite sheets for a historical strategy game, seen from a 3/4 top-down angle, on a flat pure green (#00FF00) background. Each sheet has 4 rows of 4 frames: rows = facing down, left, right, up; frames = a walk or action cycle. Same scale for every figure (a man about 130 px tall). Soft daylight from the upper left. No text, no numbers, no shadows on the green.

**Accuracy for Medina, winter 627 AD:**
- **Clothing:** long wool or linen tunics and waist-wraps in off-white, brown, ochre, dull red and indigo; wool cloaks (it is cold); turbans or simple head-cloths; sandals. Plain, worn, real.
- **Armour:** only leaders and a few rich men: a knee-length mail shirt and a simple rounded iron cap. Most men have none.
- **Weapons:** straight swords, spears about 2.5–3 m, javelins, bows with quivers on the back, round leather shields.
- **Horses:** small Arab horses with a pad saddle and **no stirrups**. Camels with wooden saddles.
- **Ground:** pale yellow-brown earth, black lava rock, date palms.
- **Treatment:** everyone, on both sides and including Muhammad, is drawn the same way: a real person of 7th-century Arabia, no halo, no veil, no glow, no caricature.
- **Avoid:** curved sabres, stirrups, Ottoman or Mamluk armour, big Ottoman turbans, Crusader helmets, crescents, flags with writing, domes and minarets, gold or fantasy armour, modern items, blood or gore, bright green anywhere on figures.

## Part 1: soldiers, animals and objects
1. **medina-infantry** — Medina spearmen: wool tunic, head-cloth, round leather shield, spear; walk (rows 1–4). Mixed ages, mostly lean and weathered.
2. **medina-infantry-attack** — the same men thrusting spears and raising shields.
3. **medina-archers** — Medina bowmen kneeling and standing at the edge of the trench, drawing and loosing; quivers on the back.
4. **medina-diggers** — men digging with mattocks and spades, and carrying palm-fibre baskets of earth on their shoulders (4 directions, work cycle).
5. **medina-guards-night** — sentries in heavy cloaks holding torches and spears, walking along the trench at night (warm torch light on the figures only).
6. **quraysh-infantry** — Meccan spearmen, slightly better equipped: some mail shirts, dyed cloaks (dull red, indigo), round shields; walk.
7. **quraysh-cavalry** — Meccan horsemen on small Arab horses, no stirrups, long spears, some in mail and iron caps; walk and canter.
8. **quraysh-archers** — Meccan bowmen shooting across the trench.
9. **ghatafan-bedouin** — Ghatafan tribesmen: darker brown and black cloaks, head-cloths wrapped round the face against the cold, spears and bows, lighter kit; walk.
10. **camel-riders** — riders on camels with wooden saddles, and laden baggage camels; walk.
11. **duel** — two men fighting on foot with straight swords and shields (one young, stocky, dark-bearded; one old, big, grey-bearded in mail): rows = strike, parry, circle, the old man falling (no blood).
12. **fallen-medina** and **fallen-confederate** — men lying still, face down or on their side, a dropped spear or shield; no blood.
13. **camp-wind** — objects: tents blown down and flapping, cooking pots overturned, fires blown out and smoking, sand blowing (4 frames each).
14. **tent-red** — a red leather tent (round-topped) on a small rise; **tent-black** — Bedouin black goat-hair tents; **fort-utum** — a square mud-brick and stone tower two storeys high with a small door high up; **trench-section** — tiles of the trench: a straight ditch about 4–5 m wide with a heap of yellow-brown spoil on one side, plus corner and end pieces.
