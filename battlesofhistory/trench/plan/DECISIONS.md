# Battle of the Trench: decisions

- **6 November 2026, agreed with the user:** the Trench is the first battle of Muhammad's lifetime on the site (Badr, Uhud and the others can follow).
- **Muhammad is shown,** with a sprite, a portrait and his name, like any other commander: not reverent and not disrespectful. No honorifics ("peace be upon him", "the Prophet" as a title) in the narration; "Muhammad" throughout, "the Prophet Muhammad" is not used. Religious claims (angels, miracles, revelation) are told as "the Qur'an says…" or "Muslim tradition tells…", never as narrated fact, and never mocked.
- **The same rule for everyone:** Ali, Abu Bakr, Umar, the Jewish leaders, the Quraysh: all shown, all neutral.
- **Proposed (to confirm):** colours by the site's rule, first-named side red. Title sides: **"The Confederates (Quraysh, Ghatafan and allies) vs. Muhammad and the people of Medina"** → Confederates red, Medina blue.
- **Proposed (to confirm):** one page, three maps (Arabia, the Medina oasis, the crossing point), 16 chapters, Banu Qurayza as a short tagged epilogue chapter, not a playable scene.
- **No Arabic text** in the art (a banner with writing would be anachronistic anyway: the Shahada flags are much later).
