# Battle of the Trench: maps

Coordinates are modern (WGS84). Positions of 627 places are **[reconstruction]** from al-Samhudi, Lecker and modern Medina landmarks (the Seven Mosques at the west foot of Sal' mark the Muslim camp by tradition). The map bases come from real elevation (AWS terrain), like the other battles; ChatGPT paints them.

## Map 1: Arabia (the campaign)
- **Frame:** lon 37.0–42.5 E, lat 20.5–26.5 N (about 650 km tall): Mecca, Medina, Khaybar, the Red Sea coast, the Ghatafan and Sulaym lands of Najd.
- **Show:** the Red Sea, the Hijaz mountains, the harra lava fields (black), the wadis, oases as green dots.
- **Do not show:** modern roads, Jeddah's modern sprawl, the Hejaz Railway (1908), borders.

## Map 2: the Medina oasis (main battle map)
- **Frame:** lon 39.540–39.680 E, lat 24.410–24.530 N (about 14 × 13 km): Uhud in the north, Quba in the south, the two harras either side.
- **Show:** Mount Uhud (red granite), Mount Sal' (small black hill), Harrat Waqim (east lava, black), Harrat al-Wabra (west lava), Wadi al-Aqiq (west), Wadi Bathan and Wadi Qanat, palm groves (dense dark green in the south and east, al-'Awali), scattered mud-brick hamlets and utum towers, the small mosque.
- **Trench line [reconstruction]:** an arc of about 5.5 km across the open north and north-west, from the edge of Harrat Waqim (near al-Shaykhan, east) westwards in front of Sal' to the edge of Harrat al-Wabra (near Banu Salama / Masjid al-Qiblatayn). Drawn later as an overlay, not painted.
- **Do not show:** the modern city, the Prophet's Mosque as it is today (domes, minarets), the ring roads, the airport.

## Map 3: the crossing point (close-up)
- **Frame:** about 1.6 km square around the west foot of Sal' (centre about lon 39.590 E, lat 24.476 N).
- **Show:** the trench with spoil bank, the marshy sabkha ground, Sal' and the Muslim camp with the red tent, the plain on the north side.
