French / British / Canadian trenches
Trenches traced from British trench maps (Ordnance Survey / GSGS, 1916–1917) in the National Library of Scotland (CC BY), sheets 36C SW and 51B NW and their 1:10,000 parts, lined up to the ground using the printed map grid and the villages. Positions are accurate to about 100–200 m; the 1917 lines in the north come from a small copy of sheet 36C SW 3 and are less detailed. The attack lines of 9 April are from Nicholson’s Canadian Expeditionary Force 1914–1919, Map 7. Painted map: Battle Games, AI-generated from our guide.
