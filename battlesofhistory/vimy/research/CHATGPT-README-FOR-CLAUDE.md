# Vimy Ridge trench and tunnel development — research package

For Battles of History / Claude Code.

Contents:
- `VIMY-TRENCH-TIMELINE.md`: staged historical development, 1914–April 1917.
- `VIMY-CONSTRUCTION-TYPES.md`: trench, shelter, mine, subway and logistics asset definitions.
- `VIMY-GAME-IMPLEMENTATION.md`: suggested time-slider, layers, and accuracy flags.
- `VIMY-SOURCES.md`: source links and verification priorities.

**Important:** This is a research-informed *overview*, not a georeferenced reconstruction of every trench or mine. Do not position exact trenches, craters or tunnel entrances without dated maps and plans. Dates for general engineering trends are approximate; specific engineering claims should be checked against the linked primary/official materials before publication.
