# Research sources and next verification tasks

1. Veterans Affairs Canada, **Vimy Tunnels and Trenches** (official brochure):
   https://public.cdn.cloud.veterans.gc.ca/pdf/memorials/overseas/TunnelTrenchBrochure-e.pdf
   Useful for communication subways, Grange Subway, underground facilities and preservation caveats.

2. G. W. L. Nicholson, **Canadian Expeditionary Force, 1914–1919** (Canadian Army Official History):
   https://www.canada.ca/content/dam/themes/defence/caf/militaryhistory/dhh/official/book-1964-expeditionary-en.pdf
   Useful for operations, engineering preparations, chronology and assault.

3. Valour Canada, **Tunnels and Trenches**:
   https://vimyridge.valourcanada.ca/the-road-to-vimy-ridge/gearing-up/tunnels-and-trenches/
   Useful as an interpretive secondary account; check numerical claims against official documentation.

4. Veterans Affairs Canada, **Canadian Preparations for the Battle of Vimy Ridge**:
   https://www.veterans.gc.ca/en/remembrance/classroom-materials/lesson-plans/canadian-preparations-battle-vimy-ridge
   Useful for training and logistical preparation.

## Research priorities
- Locate dated trench maps and British Royal Engineers tunnelling plans held by Canadian, British or French archives.
- Check chronology and coordinates of individual mine craters, tunnel entrances and German strongpoints.
- Verify differences between 12-subway and 13-subway totals (definitions, period and scope).
- Use aerial photographs with capture dates to assess landscape damage and trench relocation.
- Treat descriptions of typical Western Front trench construction as illustrative unless specifically attested at Vimy.
