## Research (research/)
- CWM-trench-map-card.pdf: Canadian War Museum education card with a soldier's hand-sketched trench map (George Metcalf Archival Collection, CWM 19810903-001). © CWM, for reference only.
- CWM-reading-a-trench-map-symbol-cards.pdf: CWM symbol cards covering wire, the British front line, enemy trenches, ground cut up by shellfire, tracks, buried cable, trench railway, dumps, observation posts and dug-outs. Used as the **legend** for our tracing (which symbol means what). © CWM, for reference only.
| la-folie-farm-1-5000-small.jpg | **"La Folie Farm", 1:5,000** special trench map, the 3rd Division sector: Neuville-St-Vaast to La Folie Farm and Petit Vimy, with German trenches in red and the Bois de la Folie | Canadian War Museum education sheet (research/CWM-1-5000-trench-map-la-folie-farm.pdf). Only 639 × 525 here. At 1:5,000 it's twice the detail of the 1:10,000 sheets; worth finding the original in the CWM collection |
| advances-canadian-corps-apr-may-1917-small.jpg | **"Map showing advance made by Canadian Corps, April 9th to May 3rd 1917"**, 1:20,000: the front line before the attack, then blue lines with dates (9, 10, 12, 13, 14, 28 April, 3 May), the divisional boundaries, and green lines for corrections. It reaches down onto the Douai plain (Arleux, Fresnoy, Méricourt) | CWM, George Metcalf Archival Collection CWM 19750215-030 (education sheet, research/). 704 × 577. **Gives the dated front lines after 12 April for stage 9** of the timeline |

## More CWM education material (research/, © CWM, reference only)
- CWM-trench-image-cards.pdf: 22 photo cards of trench life, all George Metcalf Archival Collection, with CWM numbers. Among them **"Vimy Ridge from the air"** (mine craters 10–15 m deep, blown by Canadian engineers before and during 9 April) and an **aerial view dated 24 March 1917**. The original wartime photos are Canadian Crown/official photos, so **public domain**; get them from Library and Archives Canada or the CWM online collection by their numbers, and don't take them from the PDF.
- CWM-vimy-ridge-9-12-april-map.pdf: Nicholson's Map 7 again (we have a larger scan).
