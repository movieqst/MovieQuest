# Six-Day War: Israeli Air Force radio recordings, June 1967 (reference library)

Recordings of Operation Focus (Moked), the air strikes of 5 June 1967, released by the IDF and Defense Establishment Archive. Our full Hebrew transcripts and English translations go in `sixday/audio/record/transcripts/` (one file per recording: time · Hebrew · English), made with Whisper and checked by hand.

**Yoram Harpaz (link only):** https://soundcloud.com/the-times-of-israel/yoram-harpaz-1967-war-recordings (Times of Israel Podcasts, 2017). His radio failed after his plane hit power cables during a strike in Jordanian territory; returning over Dimona he was taken for an enemy intruder and shot down by Israeli air defence, and was killed. Not downloaded or hosted.

**Background:** Times of Israel, "Soundtrack to a legendary victory: Israel releases 1967 war recordings", 12 February 2017 (all four 2017 recordings, with notes on each).
