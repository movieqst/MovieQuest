# Ancient texts for the Alexander pages (public-domain translations)

Plain-text copies of the main ancient accounts of Alexander's campaigns, for fact-checking the narration of the
Alexander pages: **Granicus and Issus** (in preparation), **Tyre** and **Gaugamela**. They are the sources the research
dossiers cite as [ARRIAN], [DIODORUS], [POLYBIUS] and [PLUTARCH]. Filed 13 November 2026.

**What is kept:** only the translations, with their chapter numbers written `[17]` and section numbers `{2}`, so a
reference such as "Diodorus 17.19.3" is found by looking for `[19]` and then `{3}`. Page numbers, footnote markers and
the notes (the Loeb editors' and Bill Thayer's) are left out. LacusCurtius marks its public-domain pages with a single
asterisk in the address; all four pages here have one.

**Made by** `make-texts.py` from the downloaded pages (run it on a folder holding them).

**Not here yet:** Curtius Rufus, *History of Alexander* (the Rolfe Loeb translation of 1946 is on LacusCurtius as well;
add it when a page needs it) and Justin's *Epitome*, book 11.

**Remember:** these writers lived centuries after Alexander (Diodorus about 300 years later, Plutarch and Arrian about
400–450). They are literary histories, not eyewitness reports; the pages say which writer says what.
