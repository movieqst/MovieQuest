# Somalia 1988 (War in the North and the Isaaq Genocide): open documents in the library

Public-domain documents gathered for the page "Somalia 1988: War in the North and the Isaaq Genocide". The texts are
kept in `../somalia1988/research/primary/` (extracted text with page markers and a header line giving the source).
Filed 13 November 2026.

| document | what it is | licence |
|---|---|---|
| Robert Gersony, *Why Somalis Flee* (US Department of State, Bureau for Refugee Programs, August 1989), 70 pp. | Interviews with 302 refugees and others; the "at least 5,000 unarmed civilian Issaks … purposefully murdered" finding (p. 61), the Berbera killings, the SNM's killings of non-Isaaq civilians | US government work: public domain |
| *Yousuf v. Samantar*, E.D. Va., Memorandum Opinion, 28 August 2012 (Dkt. 366) | Damages ($21 million) after former Prime Minister Mohamed Ali Samatar defaulted on liability rather than contest it | US court record: public domain |
| *Yousuf v. Samantar*, 699 F.3d 763 (4th Cir. 2012) | No immunity for the violations alleged | US court record: public domain |
| *Yousuf v. Samantar*, default judgment (28 August 2012) and the order of 3 February 2014 | The judgment and the appeal dismissed as moot | US court record: public domain |
| *Yousuf v. Samantar*, default hearing transcript, 23 February 2012 | Samatar's statement that defaulting "doesn't mean … I'm guilty" | US court record: public domain |
| *Warfaa v. Ali*, E.D. Va., amended complaint, 9 May 2014 | The plaintiff's allegations against Col. Yusuf Abdi Ali (a jury found him liable for torture in 2019) | US court filing: public record |
| Convention on the Prevention and Punishment of the Crime of Genocide (1948), articles I–III | The legal definition the page explains | Treaty text |
