# Sekigahara, 1600: soldier and leader art (prompts for ChatGPT)

**How to send them back**
- **One sheet per picture**, each about 1024 × 1024, as separate PNG files.
- Name each file with the **bold name** below, then zip them up.
- I key out the green and cut each sheet to size.

**Accuracy for 21 October 1600** (from our research):
- **Weather:** wet autumn day; rain the night before and, tradition says, fog at dawn. The valley was muddy.
- **Ashigaru (foot soldiers):**
  - lacquered iron or leather armour (okegawa-dō), a simple conical jingasa helmet;
  - the lord's crest on the helmet and armour;
  - a small flag on a pole in a holder on the back (**sashimono**) in the lord's colour, with his crest.
  - **Arms:** long spears (yari, about 5 m), matchlock guns (teppō) or bows.
- **Mounted samurai:** full armour (tōsei-gusoku), a kabuto helmet with a crest, a face mask, a sashimono, a spear or sword, and a small sturdy horse.
- **Two armies with the same kit:** both sides looked alike. So we **colour the sashimono** to tell them apart.
  - **Eastern Army (Tokugawa):** **white** sashimono.
  - **Western Army:** **dark blue** sashimono.
  - The colours are a game choice, not history: each lord really had his own.
- **Avoid:** no ninja, no bright red "movie samurai" for everyone, no katana-only armies, no Chinese or Korean styles, no gore.
