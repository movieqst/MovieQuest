Controls

Select: left-click a formation, or drag a box around several (Ctrl-drag adds to the selection).
Orders: with formations selected, right-click anywhere on the map for the command menu. Attack, move, face, halt and fall back apply to every formation you have selected.
Camera: drag, WASD / arrow keys, minimap click, mouse wheel to zoom. The camera follows the narration until you move it; press F to follow again.
Space: pause · R: restart · E: explain this moment (also the 💡 on the caption) · N: next chapter · . (full stop): fast forward · M: whole map · + / −: zoom · double-click the minimap for fullscreen · Art / Drawn: switch between the painted battlefield with painted units and the computer-drawn version.
Voice: pre-recorded narration with a choice of narrators in the header; falls back to your browser's speech engine for lines without a clip.
Tags: click any coloured tag (Established, Ancient account…) to see the source records behind it.
Text panels: faint by default, solid while the mouse is over them; the ✕ hides a panel and "View text narration" brings it back.

Orders

Choose your side. Click a formation or drag a box around several to select them (Ctrl-drag adds). Right-click the map for the order menu: attack-move, move, face, halt, fall back. Pan with the arrow keys, the minimap or the wheel; Shift-drag draws a zoom box. As the Persians you hold the centre and the chariot force; the Macedonians advance as history had it until they sight you. Chariots are fast and hit marching infantry hardest; infantry that halts and holds its ground takes less damage. A formation below 25% strength routs.
