Historical context

Alexander advanced obliquely toward his right, drawing Persian cavalry outward. A gap opened, and he led the Companion cavalry toward Darius. Parmenion's left wing remained under heavy pressure.

Accuracy note: Exact army sizes, positions, and the location of the battlefield are disputed. Unit symbols and timings are compressed for playability. This first version uses schematic units rather than final sprites.
