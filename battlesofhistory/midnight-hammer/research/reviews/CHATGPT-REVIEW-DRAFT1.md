## Optional improvements

- Tag officeholders as established and the colour legend as page. In Chapter 3, use iaea for the finding, press for the closed-door vote tally and iran-official for Iran’s response. The tally of 19 in favour, three against and 11 abstentions is supported by Reuters. [9]
- In Chapter 9 and the debrief, “about a day and a half” reads naturally. Both 36 and 37 hours appear in official accounts. Show the range in the table. [2, 21]
- For balance, include “Iran said its nuclear programme was for peaceful purposes.” Keep the attribution alongside the IAEA safeguards finding and the dated March U.S. intelligence assessment. Araghchi’s quoted reaction is confirmed by Reuters. [5, 7, 9, 22]
- Chapter 14 can end with the date the ceasefire took effect. The chronology alone leaves its cause open. [15]
- The script is non-graphic. Chapter 11 can keep the historical assessment and trim the explanation of machinery sensitivity. “Grossi expected very significant damage, but said the extent of the damage underground could not yet be assessed.” Keep the external radiation report attributed to Iran. [7]
- Keep source tags, C-numbers and production notes out of the voice track. Use “General Erik Kurilla,” or his full name Michael Erik Kurilla. [1]
