# Music for the video: free, properly licensed choices (22 November 2026)
The safest source found is the Australian composer **Scott Buckley**, who releases documentary and film music under **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0)**. CC BY allows editing, syncing to video and public posting, including on a site that also sells things, provided he is credited. Avoid the many "NC" and "ND" tracks on Jamendo and the Internet Archive: ND forbids cutting the track to picture, and NC is risky for a page on a shop's domain.

This container cannot download from the Archive's file servers (they sit on hosts the environment does not allow), so download these yourself from the links; each page shows the licence.

## Shortlist, in the order I would try them
| Track | Character | Where it fits | Link |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Decoherence** | slow, haunting ambient; ominous strings, solo viola | the site clips (2–4) and the conclusion: restrained, uncertain, no triumph | https://archive.org/details/sb_decoherence (licence shown: CC BY 4.0) |
| **Signal to Noise** | mysterious neo-classical; cello and piano over warm synths | the whole 60-second cut, if one track is wanted | https://archive.org/details/soundcloud-811163236 (titled CC-BY; confirm on scottbuckley.com.au) |
| **Nightfall** | calm cinematic piece written for drone footage | the take-off and the way home | https://archive.org/details/soundcloud-627733662 (titled CC-BY; confirm on scottbuckley.com.au) |
| **Eclipse** | darker, building orchestral | the Fordow clip if more weight is wanted | https://archive.org/details/scott-buckley (CC BY 4.0 library; file "Eclipse (CC-BY)") |
| **Sanctum** | solemn, slow strings and choir | the conclusion and end card | same library item, file "Sanctum (CC-BY)" |
| **Homeward** | warm, resolving | the way home, clip 5 | same library item, file "Homeward (CC-BY)" |
| **Artemis** | minimalist, hopeful; written for the NASA Artemis missions | the take-off and the globe, clip 1 | https://archive.org/details/Scott_Buckley-artemis (CC BY 4.0) |

The composer's own site, scottbuckley.com.au, carries every track with its licence statement and a higher-quality download; it is the authoritative copy, so download there if you can, and use the Archive links as the backup.

## Attribution to put in the video's end card or description
> Music: "Decoherence" and "Signal to Noise" by Scott Buckley (scottbuckley.com.au), licensed under CC BY 4.0.

Replace the titles with whichever you use. The credit must name him and the licence; that is the whole condition.

## Another free source, if the tone is wrong
**Kevin MacLeod** (incompetech.com), CC BY 3.0 or 4.0: his tracks "Ice Flow", "Infinite Perspective" and "Realizer" are on the Archive under his name, among hundreds more on his site; credit "Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com), CC BY". His style is lighter and more familiar from YouTube, so I would start with Buckley.

## Sound effects
The page's rules allow engine sound, wind and quiet dust; no explosions. Freesound.org (CC0 and CC BY sounds; check each file's licence) has jet take-offs, cockpit ambience and sea wash; it is not reachable from this container, so search it directly: "B-2 flyover", "jet take off distant", "ocean swell night", "wind ridge".
