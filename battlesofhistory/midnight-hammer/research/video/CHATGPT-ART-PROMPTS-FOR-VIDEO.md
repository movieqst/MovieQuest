# Art for the video animator: ChatGPT prompts
Two maps are already rendered from public-domain Natural Earth data (`map-iran-neighbours.png`, `map-world-flight.png`); use them as the geography reference and ask ChatGPT to paint over them so nothing moves.

## Map 1: Iran and its neighbours
> Paint a cinematic, atmospheric map for a documentary, 16:9, 4K, based exactly on the attached reference map (keep every coastline, border, label and dot where it is). Style: dark navy seas with subtle depth shading, dry parchment-brown and olive land with gentle relief showing the Zagros and Elburz mountains and the central deserts, thin cream borders, elegant serif labels in warm white, the three site names in red with small dots, the Strait of Hormuz circled in gold, Al Udeid in gold. Title at top right in gold small caps: "IRAN AND ITS NEIGHBOURS · JUNE 2025". No flags, no icons, no ships, no aircraft, no text other than the labels on the reference.

## Map 2: the whole world, flat, with a dark edge
> Paint a cinematic flat world map for a documentary, 16:9, 4K, based exactly on the attached reference (equirectangular; keep every coastline where it is). Style: dark navy oceans, dry olive-and-parchment continents with faint relief, no borders, a very thin dark-space margin at the edges only (the map fills the frame; no stars, no curvature). Mark Whiteman Air Force Base in Missouri and Fordow in Iran with small gold dots and serif labels. Draw the schematic route as a thin gold line from Missouri across the Atlantic and the Mediterranean to Iran, and a dimmer line from Missouri out over the Pacific labelled "decoy", exactly as on the reference. Caption bottom centre, small: "Routes schematic; the real routes were not published." No aircraft on the map (the animator adds them).
