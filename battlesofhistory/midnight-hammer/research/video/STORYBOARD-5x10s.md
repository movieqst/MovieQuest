# Storyboard: five 10-second clips (take-off and globe, reaching Iran, Fordow, Natanz, Isfahan)
Written 22 November 2026. Paste each clip's prompt on its own. Style line for all: *photoreal cinematic documentary, 16:9, 24 fps, 10 seconds, slow camera, night palette of moonlight blue with one warm highlight, light grain, no people, no fireballs, no flame, captions only as listed, no logos.*

## Clip 1: Whiteman take-off, then the globe (0:00–0:10)
> 0–2 s: night, low at the end of a long runway in flat farmland; a B-2 flying-wing rolls toward the camera and lifts off overhead, lights blinking. 2–4 s: the camera tilts up and follows it climbing east; two more B-2s lift off behind it in trail. 4–6 s: the climbing bomber shrinks to a dot against the stars; the frame rises straight up through thin cloud. 6–10 s: match-cut to a flat painted world map filling the frame with a thin dark edge; a gold line draws itself from Missouri out over the Atlantic, and a fainter line from Missouri out over the Pacific marked "decoy". Captions: at 1 s "WHITEMAN AIR FORCE BASE, MISSOURI · 21 JUNE 2025"; at 7 s "seven B-2s east, decoys west (US official) · routes schematic".

## Clip 2: reaching Iran (0:10–0:20)
> 0–3 s: dusk at high altitude over the ocean, a B-2 holding steady under a tanker's refuelling boom, contrails pink, the camera orbiting a quarter turn. 3–5 s: the boom retracts; the bomber slides away and down into darkness. 5–8 s: cut to the painted Iran map, night tones: the gold line arrives from the west over the Persian Gulf and the Strait of Hormuz circled in gold; three red dots pulse once each, north to south: Fordow, Natanz, Isfahan; a small dim submarine mark appears at sea to the south. 8–10 s: the map dissolves into a real moonlit view of bare Iranian mountains from high altitude, a B-2 silhouette crossing silently right to left. Captions: at 1 s "about 18 hours, refuelling in the air (US official)"; at 6 s "three targets · 2:10 to 2:35 a.m., 22 June 2025, Iran time".

## Clip 3: Fordow (0:20–0:30)
> 0–3 s: drone approach at night over a dusty plain toward a bare rocky ridge; a town's lights far left (Qom); a fenced compound on the ridge's flank with dark tunnel mouths. 3–5 s: a B-2 silhouette passes high above, right to left; nothing seen to fall. 5–7 s: on the ridge top, two small grey dust clouds rise quietly a few seconds apart, no flash, and drift. 7–10 s: the mountain turns translucent: two shafts run from the surface down to two plain halls; six small bomb outlines descend each shaft in sequence, the last drawn hollow and marked "spare"; the halls fade to a question-mark outline. Captions: at 1 s "FORDOW · built inside a mountain, north-east of Qom"; at 5 s "12 GBU-57 bombs, 6 per ventilation shaft (Pentagon, 26 June 2025)"; at 9 s "what happened in the halls could not be verified (IAEA)".

## Clip 4: Natanz (0:30–0:40)
> 0–3 s: drone approach at night across pale desert toward a very large walled compound of rectangular buildings, long earth-mounded roofs among them, one building near the front a blackened shell (struck 13 June); a mountain with fresh tunnel portals beyond. 3–5 s: a B-2 silhouette high above. 5–7 s: two small dust puffs on the mounded roofs, a few seconds apart, no flash. 7–10 s: looking straight down, the earth turns translucent: a thick slab over two shallow halls, pierced in two places; the halls dark; the scene dissolves to a second possibility, the halls intact but unlit, with the substation outside burnt; then a question mark. Captions: at 1 s "NATANZ · Iran's main enrichment site · halls under earth and concrete"; at 5 s "2 GBU-57 bombs (US official)"; at 9 s "the deeper new tunnels in the mountain were not reported struck (press)".

## Clip 5: Isfahan (0:40–0:50)
> 0–3 s: night at sea, long swell, a dark submarine hull awash; a cruise missile leaves the water in white spray on a short pale plume, no fire, and tilts north; a second follows. 3–5 s: the missile low over dark hills, the glow of a big city with a river ahead to the right. 5–8 s: drone view of a broad campus of workshops and halls on open ground at the city's edge, a line of tunnel entrances in a hillside beyond; several buildings already roofless and sooty; small dust puffs at the tunnel mouths and on two buildings, no flash. 8–10 s: the hillside turns translucent: containers drawn faintly inside, then a dotted truck trail leading away, then a question mark. Captions: at 1 s "a US submarine, not named · more than two dozen Tomahawks (US official)"; at 6 s "ISFAHAN · the nuclear technology centre, south-east of the city"; at 9 s "where the enriched uranium went is not known (IAEA)".

## Cutting note
Open on the title card "OPERATION MIDNIGHT HAMMER · 21–22 June 2025" (1.5 s), then clips 1 to 5 with 12-frame dissolves, then the end card "The bombers returned after about 37 hours. What the strike destroyed is still disputed. · Battles of History" (3 s). About 55 seconds in all.
