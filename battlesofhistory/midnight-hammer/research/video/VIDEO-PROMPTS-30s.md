# Operation Midnight Hammer: a 30-second video in three 10-second clips
Written 22 November 2026 for a video-generation AI, from the page's checked narration script. The three clips follow the page's own rules: no copyrighted footage or news photographs, no one shown hurt, no explosions beyond a small distant dust cloud, no view of the bombs underground, no ventilation shafts, and the routes drawn as simple schematic arrows because the real ones were never published. Facts on screen are the Pentagon's own figures (7 B-2s, 14 GBU-57 bombs, more than two dozen Tomahawks, 25 minutes, about 37 hours), so they are tagged "US official" where they appear.

**Overall look, paste before each clip if the tool allows a style setting:** cinematic documentary, photoreal, 16:9, 24 fps, 10 seconds, slow camera moves, desaturated night palette with moonlight blue and a single warm highlight, light film grain, no text other than what is listed, no logos, no people's faces, no gore, no flags of real units, no music cues in the prompt.

**Title card and end card (for the editor, not the AI):** open on black with "OPERATION MIDNIGHT HAMMER · 21–22 June 2025" in the page's serif small caps, gold on black, 1.5 seconds, then clip 1. Close on "What the bombs destroyed underground is still disputed. · Battles of History" for 2 seconds. If the tool must stay exactly 30 seconds, trim each clip's tail by half a second.

## Clip 1 (0:00–0:10): Whiteman, Missouri, before dawn, 21 June 2025
Prompt:
> Photoreal cinematic shot, night, a wide concrete runway at a US Air Force base on the flat Missouri plains under a huge sky with a bright half moon and a few stars. A single B-2 Spirit stealth bomber, the black flying-wing with no tail, taxis slowly toward the camera, its anti-collision lights blinking red and white, heat shimmer behind it. The camera holds very low at the runway edge and pans slowly to follow it as it turns onto the runway and begins its take-off roll, blue runway lights streaking past. Far behind it, two more B-2s wait in line with their lights on. No people visible. Mood: quiet, deliberate, secret. Colour: moonlight blue, warm sodium light on the hangars at the far left. End the shot as the first bomber lifts its nose and leaves the ground. Lower-third caption appears at second 3 in white small caps: "WHITEMAN AIR FORCE BASE, MISSOURI · 21 JUNE 2025 · seven B-2s depart (US official)".

What it shows: chapter 5 of the page. Seven B-2s left Whiteman early on 21 June; a decoy group flew west over the Pacific. Showing three in line is a staging choice, not a count.

## Clip 2 (0:10–0:20): across the world, eighteen hours, 21 June 2025
Prompt:
> Photoreal cinematic sequence in two halves. First five seconds: high over the Atlantic at dusk, a B-2 flying-wing holds steady behind a KC-135 tanker, the refuelling boom connected, the sun a thin orange line on the horizon below and the sky above already deep blue; the camera orbits slowly a quarter turn around the pair, contrails pink in the last light. A single thin gold line draws itself from the central United States eastward across the Atlantic and the Mediterranean toward the Middle East, and a second, dimmer line draws itself westward from the United States out over the Pacific and fades, labelled in small white text "decoy". The gold line ends in a small pulsing gold ring over central Iran. No borders, no flags, no text other than "decoy" and the lower-third. Lower-third caption from second 6: "ABOUT 18 HOURS TO IRAN · more than 125 aircraft in all (US official) · routes schematic".

What it shows: chapter 6. The refuelling is documented (several times, with tankers); the orbit shot is staging. The globe lines are the page's own schematic routes, with "routes schematic" on screen because the real route was not published.

## Clip 3 (0:20–0:30): Fordow, ten past two in the morning, 22 June 2025
Prompt:
> Photoreal cinematic shot, night, 2:10 a.m. The camera looks down and across a range of bare, dry, moonlit mountains in central Iran, rocky ridges and a dusty plain with a distant town's lights at the far left (Qom). Very high above, almost invisible against the stars, a single B-2 flying-wing crosses the frame from right to left, silent, no lights. Nothing falls from it that the camera can see. Three seconds later, on a dark hillside in the middle distance, a small grey-brown dust cloud rises quietly and drifts, no fireball, no flash, no debris, the mountains unchanged. The camera stays wide and still, then drifts very slowly upward toward the stars as the dust thins. No buildings shown in detail, no people, no vehicles. Mood: remote, still, uncertain. Lower-third caption at second 1: "FORDOW · 22 JUNE 2025, 2:10 A.M. IRAN TIME · 12 GBU-57 bombs (US official)". Final caption at second 8, centred, white serif: "The bombers returned. What was destroyed underground is still disputed."

What it shows: chapter 7 and the page's rule 3. The bomb count is the Pentagon's. The distant dust cloud is the only allowed sign of the strike; the AI must not show a fireball, a shaft, a crater or the inside of the mountain.
