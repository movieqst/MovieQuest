# Storyboard v2: six 10-second clips
Clip 1 is the take-off and the globe. Clips 2, 3 and 4 are one site each: a basemap of the site first, then the drone view, then what different people believe is there. Written 22 November 2026; the beliefs are attributed and dated and should go through ChatGPT's source check with the rest.

Style line for all clips: *photoreal cinematic documentary, 16:9, 24 fps, 10 seconds, slow camera, night palette of moonlight blue with one warm highlight, light grain, no people, no fireballs, no flame, captions only as listed.*

## Clip 1: Whiteman take-off, then the globe (0:00–0:10)
> 0–3 s: night, low at the end of a long runway in flat farmland; a B-2 flying-wing rolls toward the camera and lifts off overhead, lights blinking; two more lift off behind it. 3–5 s: the camera follows the first climbing east until it is a dot among the stars, then rises through thin cloud. 5–10 s: match-cut to a flat painted world map with a thin dark edge; a gold line draws from Missouri over the Atlantic and the Mediterranean to Iran, a fainter line from Missouri over the Pacific marked "decoy"; the gold line ends in a pulsing ring over Iran, and the map pushes in toward it. Captions: at 1 s "WHITEMAN AIR FORCE BASE, MISSOURI · 21 JUNE 2025"; at 6 s "seven B-2s east, about 18 hours · decoys west (US official) · routes schematic".

## Clips 2 to 4: the same shape for each site
- 0–2.5 s **Basemap.** A painted map of the site and its surroundings, labelled, the site in red. (Map prompts below.)
- 2.5–5.5 s **Drone.** The map tilts and dissolves into a photoreal night drone view of the same ground, which the camera orbits slowly.
- 5.5–10 s **What is believed to be there.** The ground turns translucent and a simple drawing of the inside appears; it changes slowly through three or four versions, each captioned with whose belief it is and when. No shafts routes, no depths to the metre.

## Clip 2: Fordow (0:10–0:20)
> Basemap: a painted map 20 km across of dry hills north-east of Qom: the city's edge at the lower left, the Qom–Tehran motorway crossing, bare ridges, a small village, and a red dot and label FORDOW on a ridge; a north arrow and a 5 km scale bar. Drone: the camera tilts from the map into a moonlit view of the ridge, a fenced compound on its flank, dark tunnel mouths with earth ramps, roads curling up; slow orbit. Inside, in sequence: (1) "IAEA, May 2025: two halls, about 2,700 centrifuges, enriching to 60%": two plain halls drawn under the rock with rows of thin cylinders; (2) "Iran: a declared civilian enrichment plant under IAEA inspection": the same drawing with a small inspector's seal mark; (3) "Pentagon, 26 June 2025: 12 bombs down the two ventilation shafts; 'obliterated' (Trump)": two shafts drawn from the surface to the halls, the halls shaded dark; (4) "DIA, 24 June (low confidence): set back by months · IAEA, Sept 2025: cannot verify": the halls fade to a question-mark outline. Captions as listed; at 1 s "FORDOW · 30 km from Qom · built inside a mountain".

## Clip 3: Natanz (0:20–0:30)
> Basemap: a painted map 20 km across of flat desert at the foot of mountains: the town of Natanz, the Kashan–Isfahan road, the walled complex as a large rectangle in red, and a smaller red mark on the mountain to the south labelled "new tunnels"; north arrow, scale bar. Drone: tilt into a moonlit view of the vast compound, mounded roofs, one blackened roofless building at the front (struck 13 June), the mountain portals with spoil fans beyond; slow orbit. Inside, in sequence: (1) "IAEA: Iran's main enrichment site, large halls under earth and concrete; the above-ground pilot plant destroyed on 13 June": two shallow halls under a slab, the surface building drawn as a ruin; (2) "Iran: damage, extent not stated": the same, hatched; (3) "Pentagon: 2 bombs on the halls; imagery showed two holes": the slab pierced twice, halls dark; (4) "Press, 2025: the deeper tunnels in the mountain were not reported struck": the mountain beside it drawn with long tunnels and a question mark. At 1 s "NATANZ · open desert near the town of Natanz".

## Clip 4: Isfahan (0:30–0:40)
> Basemap: a painted map 30 km across: the city of Isfahan with its river and bridges at the upper left, suburbs thinning to open ground, the nuclear technology centre as a red outline on the south-eastern edge, nearby hills with a red mark "tunnels"; north arrow, scale bar. Drone: tilt into a moonlit view of the broad campus of workshops and halls, several roofless and sooty, the line of tunnel entrances in the hillside beyond; slow orbit. Inside, in sequence: (1) "IAEA: conversion plant, workshops, reactors; much of Iran's enriched uranium reported stored in the tunnels": the hill drawn with containers inside; (2) "Press (Maxar imagery): trucks at the sites in the days before": a dotted truck trail drawn leading away; (3) "Pentagon: more than two dozen Tomahawks on buildings and tunnel entrances": the entrances drawn choked; (4) "IAEA, 2025: continuity of knowledge of the material lost": the hill a plain silhouette with a question mark. At 1 s "ISFAHAN · on the south-eastern edge of a city of two million".

## Clip 5: the way home (0:40–0:50)
> 0–3 s: night over dark mountains, a B-2 silhouette climbing away to the west with the first grey of dawn behind it; no pursuit, no fire. 3–5 s: dusk-to-dawn time-lapse at altitude, a tanker's boom connecting once more. 5–7 s: the painted world map, the gold line now drawing back from Iran across the Mediterranean and the Atlantic to Missouri. 7–10 s: daylight, midday summer, three B-2s in trail on final approach over green fields, the first passing low over the camera and touching down. Captions: at 1 s "the Pentagon said Iran's fighters did not fly and no shots were seen (US official, 22 June)"; at 8 s "WHITEMAN, 22 JUNE 2025 · about 37 hours after take-off".

## Clip 6: conclusion (0:50–1:00)
> 0–4 s: slow push on the painted Iran map in night tones, the three red dots fading to grey outlines; 4–7 s: four short lines of text appear one by one on the left, each with its source: "Trump, 22 June: 'obliterated'" · "DIA, 24 June, low confidence: set back by months" · "Pentagon, 2 July: one to two years" · "IAEA, September: could not verify"; 7–10 s: the map dims to black, one line centred: "The bombers reached their targets and came home. What they destroyed is still disputed." then "Battles of History". No other imagery.
