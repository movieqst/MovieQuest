# Operation Midnight Hammer (June 2025): the three maps, one per chat (18 November 2026)

Made by `tools/draw-maps.py`. Each map goes to ChatGPT in its own new chat, with only its own base and guide. The guides
carry neutral labels only: no targets, aim points, flight paths, explosions or damage. The page draws the aircraft, the
arrows and the events itself. The flight from Missouri is shown on the page's globe, so it needs no painted map.

Colours on the page (owner's choice, 18 November 2026): **Iran red, the United States blue.** The paintings themselves
carry no colours for either side.
