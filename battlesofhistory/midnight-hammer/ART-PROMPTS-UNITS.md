# Operation Midnight Hammer: sprite sheets, one per chat (18 November 2026)

Each sheet goes to ChatGPT in its own NEW chat, with one self-contained prompt, the same way as Mogadishu and Medak.
From the checked report (research/reports/Operation Midnight Hammer 2025.md) and ChatGPT's dossier (section 12): the B-2,
its weapon release (a generic reconstruction: no bay mechanism or target detail), the KC-135 tanker, the F-35A and F-16
(both confirmed taking part), the submarine and Tomahawk for Isfahan (a generic submarine: no hull is named), and a small
dust effect. No soldiers: no ground forces took part. No insignia, flags or tail codes; drawn from public photographs, not
from any film. Page colours (owner, 18 Nov 2026): Iran red, the United States blue; the page adds a coloured ring under
each sprite, so the sprites themselves stay in their real colours.
Cut with tools/prepare-sprite-sheet.py (aircraft and submarine in 240 x 240 cells, the missile and dust in 160 px cells).

# Sprite 01-b2-flight: B-2 Spirit stealth bomber (fly)

**How:** open a NEW chat in ChatGPT for this sheet only. Paste the text below as ONE message. Ask for a PNG, about 1024 x 1024.
Save it as **01-b2-flight.png** and send it to Claude. If it comes back with a different background, a grid of different subjects,
or labels, reply: "One subject only, 4 x 4 cells, pure green background #00FF00, no text."

```text
Make ONE sprite sheet for an educational history page about a 2025 air operation, seen from high above at a slight angle (a near top-down view, like a classic real-time strategy game).
- ONE square image, about 1024 x 1024 pixels: a 4 x 4 grid of equal cells on a flat pure green background (#00FF00). No shadows on the background, no grid lines, no borders, no text. Only ONE sheet in the picture.
- The same single subject in every cell, the same size in every cell, fully inside its cell with generous green space all round.
- Row 1: heading toward the viewer (south). Row 2: heading RIGHT (east). Row 3: heading away (north). Row 4: heading LEFT (west).
- The 4 columns are 4 frames of the movement described below.
- STYLE: painterly but crisp, realistic colours, readable at small size; drawn from public reference photographs of the real aircraft or vessel, not from any film or video game.
- No bright green anywhere on the subject. No flags, no national insignia, no readable text, letters or numbers, no tail codes.
- Calm and neutral: no explosions, no fire, no smoke trails, no damage, no people visible.

Rows as above; the 4 frames show steady level flight: the same aircraft with a very small up-and-down drift between frames, as if gently floating.
SUBJECT: A U.S. Air Force B-2 Spirit stealth bomber: a flying wing with NO tail and NO fins, a smooth dark charcoal-grey surface, a sawtooth (zig-zag) trailing edge, a slightly raised centre section with two small cockpit windows near the nose, the engine intakes as low humps on top of the wing. Seen from above it looks like a wide, flat boomerang with a pointed nose. Night-time colouring: dark grey with soft highlights. No lights. (Not the newer B-21: the B-2's trailing edge has the double zig-zag.)
```
