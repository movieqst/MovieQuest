# Brief for writing the Six-Day War narration (shared by all four writers)

You are writing part of the narration script for an educational web page, "Battles of History" (moviequest.ca/battlesofhistory), about the Six-Day War, 5–10 June 1967. Narrators (text-to-speech voices) read it aloud while a map shows the moves. The site owner wants it **very detailed: as much as the research supports**.

## Read first
- `battlesofhistory/batoche/NARRATION-SCRIPT.md` — the FORMAT to copy exactly (look at the Introduction and a few chapters).
- `battlesofhistory/sixday/plan/timeline.md` — section 0 (tags, clocks), section 1 (master timeline), section 2 (the 26 chapters and their maps), section 4 (contradictions: resolved and open). Follow section 4: where something is open, say so in the narration.
- `battlesofhistory/sixday/plan/characters.md` — section 4, "Sensitive persons and claims: safest wording". **Use that wording.**
- The research files named in your part (in `battlesofhistory/sixday/research/`). Take facts only from these files; do not add facts from memory unless they are well established, and then tag them and list them under "To check".

## Format (copy Batoche)
```
### 12. Jordan opens fire
*Map: Jerusalem · 5 June, about 09:00–13:30*
- One or two spoken sentences. *(established)*
- Next line. *(arab: Jordanian)*
```
- Chapter heading `### N. Title` using the numbers and titles from timeline.md section 2 (you may improve a title slightly). Under it, an italic line with the map and the date/time.
- **8–14 bullets per chapter**, each 1–3 sentences, written to be **spoken**: plain words, short sentences, numbers written so a voice reads them well ("about 200 aircraft", "the 5th of June"); write clock times as words a narrator reads ("at a quarter to eight in the morning", not "07:45"). Spell out ranks once ("Brigadier-General"), then surnames.
- Every bullet ends with ONE tag in italics in brackets: *(established)*, *(israeli)*, *(arab: Egyptian)* / *(arab: Jordanian)* / *(arab: Syrian)* / *(arab: Iraqi)* / *(arab: Palestinian)*, *(disputed)*, *(estimate)*, *(reconstruction)*. Never "*(arab)*" alone. Do not narrate [unverified] material as fact; if you mention it, say "one account claims…" and tag *(disputed)*.
- Clock times are Israel time; where no source gives a time say "morning", "afternoon", "night".
- After the last chapter of your part, add `## Notes for this part` with: (a) "To check": every claim not clearly in the research files, (b) the sources leaned on most for each chapter (file and section), (c) anything you left out and why.

## Tone and balance (the site's rules — important)
- **Neutral.** The page is told from every side's sources and does not take sides. Give Egyptian, Jordanian, Syrian, Palestinian and Israeli experiences and claims each their room. When sides disagree, say who says what ("Israeli accounts say… Jordanian accounts say…").
- **Name people and units**; say what they did. No praise words ("heroic", "brilliant", "cowardly", "terrorist") in the narrator's voice; if a side used such a word, quote it as theirs.
- Soldiers on all sides are described as capable people. Civilians appear on all sides: Israeli towns shelled, Palestinian and Syrian civilians who fled or were expelled, UN peacekeepers killed.
- Names of places: use the names of 1967 plus today's where helpful once ("the Golan Heights", "East Jerusalem", "the West Bank, then ruled by Jordan").
- No graphic gore. Losses as numbers, tagged *(estimate)*.
- Map colour convention on the site: Israel blue, the Arab armies red. Do not mention colours unless needed.

## Output
Write ONLY your file `battlesofhistory/sixday/narration-parts/<your part>.md`. Do not edit any other file. Do not commit. In your final reply give: the file path, the word count, and the 5 shakiest claims you made.
