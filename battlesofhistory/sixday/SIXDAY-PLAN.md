## First release: 5 maps
- **Region:** 650 km, Cairo to Damascus.
- **Air strike:** 622 km, the Egyptian airfields, Sharm el-Sheikh and the USS Liberty.
- **Jerusalem:** 8.8 km, Ammunition Hill to Government House.
- **Golan, Tel Faher:** 10 km.
- **Abu-Ageila / Um Katef:** 10 km. Its frame stays provisional until Gawrych's maps and the Sinai survey sheets are lined up.

**Later:** North Sinai, Central Sinai and the passes, the West Bank, and the Golan front to Quneitra.

**Map build order:** Region, Jerusalem, Air strike, Golan (Tel Faher), Abu-Ageila, then the rest.
