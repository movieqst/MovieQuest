## Corrections to earlier briefs (found by this round)
- Golani Brigade commander was Col. **Yona Efrat** (not Ben-Ari). Golan battalions: 12th (Klein) at Tel Faher, 13th at Tel Azaziat, 51st toward Banias.
- **Amitai** commanded the Jerusalem Brigade and **Ben-Ari** the Harel Brigade. **Rouhi al-Khatib** was mayor of East Jerusalem; **Anwar al-Khatib** was governor.
- Soviet ambassador in Cairo in 1967 was **Dmitri Pozhidaev**; Vinogradov succeeded him.
- Ceasefire: Resolution 236 gives 16:30 GMT = 18:30 local, matching the IDF hour.
- Hotline: FRUS says 5 June was the first substantive message (Wikipedia says 26 May). "Regatta" nickname: unverified.
- Israeli dead: 736 / 776 / 779 / 983 depending on what is counted. Jordan: ~700 killed vs ~6,000 casualties. Egyptian aircraft lost: 293-338.
