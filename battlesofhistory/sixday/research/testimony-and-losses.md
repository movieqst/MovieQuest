# Six-Day War: testimony, reconciled losses, glossary, characters

Prepared November 2026 for Battles of History (moviequest.ca). Companion to RESEARCH.md and RESEARCH-NOTES.md. Nothing here is narrated until checked against the page named.

## Method and limits

- Pages were read through the fetch tool, which returns a short machine summary of each page, not the full text. Every name, number and quote below was taken from such a summary. Check the page before narration.
- Direct quotes are kept under 15 words, one per source page. Everything else is paraphrase. Quotes from Arabic and Hebrew pages are translations as shown by the summary, so treat them as paraphrase [unverified wording].
- Pages that could not be opened: Martin Kramer's Mosaic essay on Soldiers' Talk (not on the allowlist), the original Arab News page (redirected, then refused), Wikipedia pages for the Sinai and Golan campaigns, Odd Bull's memoir, Gabi Bron's 1995 reporting, and the Ahram Gate soldier stories (fetch error). They are listed as gaps.
- Tags: [established] [israeli] [arab] [disputed] [estimate] [unverified]. A testimony is tagged by the side of the speaker. "[arab]" or "[israeli]" means a participant's own account, not a checked fact.
- Oral history warning. Most Arab and Palestinian accounts here were given 40 to 55 years later, to newspapers, with some political purpose (compensation suits, commemoration, anniversary features). Use them for colour and for what people believed they saw. Do not use them for exact times or numbers.

## 1. Testimonies

### 1A. Egyptian soldiers, officers and civilians

**T1. Ibrahim El Sayed.** Egyptian conscript and administrator at the state paper al-Gomhuria; aged 71 in 2017. [arab]
- Witnessed: captured a day or so after 5 June 1967 near the Sinai front by an Israeli soldier who spoke Egyptian Arabic. The soldier reportedly told him which way to flee, but others detained him in El Arish. He says a 12-hour truck convoy to Atlit, carrying 53 other detainees, was ambushed. He was one of three survivors and was shot in the left hand. At Atlit he describes concrete floors, no roof, lice, skin disease, no working toilets, and executions of fellow prisoners over months.
- Quote: "Hundreds of my comrades were executed in cold blood."
- Caution: he also says the USS Liberty signalled Egyptian troops to retreat. That is not supported by any account in the dossier. [unverified]. The convoy ambush and the executions are uncorroborated. [unverified]
- Source: Atlantic Council MENASource, Farid Farid, 2017. https://www.atlanticcouncil.org/blogs/menasource/fifty-years-on-a-defeat-at-israel-s-hands-haunts-egyptian-military-veterans/
- Tag: [arab] [unverified]

**T2. Amin Gomaa.** Egyptian soldier, aged 72 in 2017, later a veteran of 1973. [arab]
- Witnessed: captured on 5 June 1967 and held about five months at Atlit. Also describes his earlier service around the Tiran islands. No quote kept (same source as T1).
- Source: same Atlantic Council article.
- Tag: [arab]

**T3. Amin Abdel Rahman.** Lance corporal, Egyptian military-intelligence reconnaissance battalion. [arab]
- Witnessed: says he was captured on the fifth day and held at a camp at al-Khusa in Sinai. He says the camp commander was Binyamin Ben-Eliezer, who shot an Egyptian captain and then another soldier after prisoners were accused of drinking water without permission, and that he himself was spared by chance. Published March 2007; he said he had filed a Cairo court case against Ben-Eliezer and Ariel Sharon.
- Response: Ben-Eliezer's office denied it and he had denied all such claims before (see T22). The place name "al-Khusa" and the "fifth day" capture date do not match other accounts, since the fighting ended on 8 to 10 June. [unverified]
- Source: Bizportal (Hebrew headline, report of Gulf News piece, 2007). https://www.bizportal.co.il/general/news/article/140241
- Tag: [arab] [disputed]

**T4. Amin Mohammad Darwish, aged 64 in 2007, and his friend Gomaa Hassan.** Egyptian soldiers captured in Sinai; later colleagues at a firm in Alexandria. Not the same man as Amin Gomaa in T2. [arab]
- Witnessed: Darwish was on duty near the border on 5 June, walked three days past abandoned or destroyed Egyptian weapons, was moved to El Arish, then to Israel, where he was held eight months. He says Hassan was beaten repeatedly on the head at Atlit and was left impaired. Both were suing the Egyptian government to pursue those responsible and seeking compensation from Israel.
- Quote: "I was humiliated."
- Source: Al Jazeera English, 12 June 2007. https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2007/6/12/egypt-veterans-fight-for-justice
- Tag: [arab]

**T5. Maj. Gen. Saad el-Shazly.** Commander of a task force of about 7,500 men with a 150-tank brigade, central Sinai; aged 45. [arab]
- Witnessed: was at a headquarters meeting when the war began. He says he never received the withdrawal order of 6 June. With the Israelis already past him, he moved his force into Israeli territory, held a valley about two days, was ordered back on 7 June, marched about 60 miles overnight, was strafed at dawn (over 100 killed in the account), and crossed the Suez Canal by dusk on 8 June.
- Dispute: some Western historians say he stayed idle and then abandoned his command. Egypt's archives are closed.
- Source: War History Online. https://www.warhistoryonline.com/guest-bloggers/egyptian-unit-escaped-destruction-1967-invading-israel.html (his own account is in "The Crossing of the Suez")
- Tag: [arab] [disputed]

**T6. Gen. Mohamed Fawzi, Chief of Staff.** Memoir summarised by al-Majalla. [arab]
- Witnessed: at army headquarters on 5 June, he judged the air force about 85 percent destroyed within four hours. On 6 June Field Marshal Amer gave him 20 minutes to draft a withdrawal plan, then rejected the timetable and said he had already ordered the withdrawal. Fawzi also records that on 2 June Nasser told the generals to expect an attack on 4 or 5 June.
- Source: al-Majalla, "The 1967 Naksa relived through the memoirs of Abdel Nasser's top generals." https://en.majalla.com/node/292786/documents-memoirs/1967-naksa-relived-through-memoirs-abdel-nasser%E2%80%99s-top-generals
- Tag: [arab]

**T7. Abdul Latif al-Baghdadi, Nasser's former deputy.** Diary, Cairo and army command, 5 June. [arab]
- Witnessed: heard anti-aircraft fire at 8:45 am. Israeli radio claimed 150 Egyptian planes hit on the ground. That evening Amer first claimed 73 Israeli planes downed, then gave a list showing 47 Egyptian aircraft left, 35 usable. Badran brought word that Khan Yunis had fallen, Rafah was besieged and Gaza attacked. Nasser withdrew to a bedroom.
- Source: same al-Majalla article (T6). The 73-claim and 47/35 figures are in the 5 June diary as summarised, not checked against the diary.
- Tag: [arab] [estimate]

**T8. Mahmud Riad, Foreign Minister.** Memoir via al-Majalla. [arab]
- Witnessed: on 6 June Nasser asked him to break relations with the US; Riad argued it would backfire. He says the military command ignored warnings and could have limited losses to about 20 percent. He recounts an encrypted warning from Ajloun in Jordan of aircraft heading for Egypt that was not decoded in time because the cipher had changed that day.
- Source: same al-Majalla article (T6).
- Tag: [arab] [disputed] (the Ajloun cable story needs checking against Riad's own book and Jordanian sources)

**T9. Anwar Sadat, then a senior politician.** Memoir via al-Majalla. [arab]
- Witnessed: says he approved closing the Straits of Tiran, that Amer blamed the US Air Force, and on 7 June found Nasser at home, not at headquarters.
- Quote (as rendered by al-Majalla): "started and ended while Amer was still in the sky."
- Source: same al-Majalla article.
- Tag: [arab] [disputed] (Sadat wrote as a rival of the defeated generals)

**T10. Mohamed Hassanein Heikal, journalist.** Memoir "Kalam fel Seyasa". [arab]
- Witnessed: crowds filled the streets as soon as Nasser finished his resignation speech on 9 June, demanding he stay. Heikal says Nasser stayed in shock for hours.
- Quote: "remained in shock for hours." (Egypt Today's wording of Heikal.)
- Source: Egypt Today. https://www.egypttoday.com/Article/4/104830/Why-did-Egyptians-refuse-Nasser’s-step-down-after-the-1967
- Tag: [arab]
- Note: Heikal drafted the speech, according to Al Jazeera Arabic's headline. https://www.aljazeera.net/politics/2021/6/9/في-ذكرى-خطاب-تنحي-عبد-الناصر-كيف-صاغ [headline only; unverified]

**T11. Sabry Mahmoud, aged 21 in 1967, Cairo.** Civilian. [arab]
- Witnessed: heard the resignation speech on 9 June, went into the streets and joined crowds heading to Nasser's house in Manshiyet al-Bakri. He says the protests lasted two days, with people arriving from across Egypt, until Nasser gave way.
- Quote: "I felt dizzy."
- Source: Gulf News. https://gulfnews.com/world/mena/egyptians-recall-rallies-held-to-support-nasser-1.757060
- Tag: [arab]

**T12. Suraya Mahmoud, aged 8 in 1967, now a headmistress; Al Waili, Cairo.** [arab]
- Witnessed: before the war, children in her district chanted that the army would enter Tel Aviv. She saw her father stunned and in tears at the news of defeat. He and her eldest brother then joined the crowds urging Nasser to stay.
- Quote: "He looked stunned and even burst into tears."
- Source: Gulf News, "50 years on, 1967 naksa trauma lingers in Egypt." https://gulfnews.com/world/mena/50-years-on-1967-naksa-trauma-lingers-in-egypt-1.2039725
- Tag: [arab]

**T13. Moussa al-Roweshid (15 in 1967, North Sinai) and Rashid Khalil (75 in 2007).** Sinai Bedouin civilians. [arab]
- Witnessed: al-Roweshid says Egyptians who had surrendered were run over by tanks, pointing to incidents near the Abu Ejerem and Abu Hani wells on the coast. Khalil says surrendering soldiers were lined up, stripped of belongings and shot, and that Bedouins were made to bury the dead.
- Context: given after the 2007 Israeli documentary on Ben-Eliezer's unit (see section 1F). Lawyer Mahmoud Lotfi represented 23 Egyptian families seeking missing relatives.
- Source: Al Jazeera English, 8 March 2007. https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2007/3/8/film-claims-1967-israeli-massacre
- Tag: [arab] [disputed]

### 1B. Jordanian soldiers and Jerusalem officials

**T14. Mahmoud Muali, born 1938, Qarawat Bani Zaid; head of radio communications, Jordanian army, Khan al-Ahmar base near Samu.** [arab]
- Witnessed: judged by Friday 2 June that war was coming. The army in Ramallah held an emergency meeting and issued weapons. On 6 June the army was ordered to retreat. He left his rifle and uniform and walked two days to his village, burning photographs and service records in case the Israelis came. On 8 June about 40 Israeli tanks entered his village of roughly 600; nearly half the village later left for Jordan.
- Quote: "Resistance is in my blood."
- Source: Gulf News, 2017. https://gulfnews.com/world/mena/officer-in-jordanian-army-recounts-war-memories-1.2038030
- Tag: [arab]

**T15. Maj. Gen. Wassef Erekat, aged 19 in 1967, Jordanian artillery battery near Jenin; from Abu Dis.** [arab]
- Witnessed: recalled from a ranger course in Zarqa. On 5 June his battery shelled targets at Marj Bin Amer aimed at the airfield near Afula, into the night. About 6 am on 6 June the battalion pulled back to Zababdeh and burned documents. Israeli tanks entered the base after the camp cook opened the gate thinking they were Iraqi forces. He hid behind haystacks, passed burnt-out Jordanian tanks with dead crews, and was ordered back to Zarqa.
- Quote: "I was young, just 19, and fearless."
- Source: Gulf News, 2017. https://gulfnews.com/world/mena/i-was-young-fearless-and-looking-for-revenge-1.2038059
- Tag: [arab]

**T16. Capt. Sami al-Qadi and Cpl. Hussein Saad al-Nuaimi, Jordanian 40th Armoured Brigade (Lt. Col. Rakan al-Jazi), near Jenin.** [arab]
- Witnessed: during a planned withdrawal, al-Nuaimi, a Bedouin tank gunner, refused to move his tank, saying he had been taught never to turn his back on the enemy. Al-Qadi replied that this did not apply when a commander ordered the move. Al-Nuaimi asked about Jerusalem and was killed later that day. Al-Jazi's son says his father wrote no memoir because many would be upset.
- Quote: "and Jerusalem?"
- Source: Al Arabiya English, 1 August 2012, "How the Arabs lost Jerusalem." https://english.alarabiya.net/articles/2012%2F08%2F01%2F229723
- Tag: [arab]

**T17. Anwar Khattib (Governor of Arab Jerusalem), Brig. Ata Ali, and Hazim Khalidi (former British officer, Tourism director).** Jerusalem, 5 to 7 June. [arab]
- Witnessed (as reconstructed by an American journalist in the Old City): on 5 June Khattib phoned King Hussein, who sounded depressed to Khalidi. Khalidi realised Ata Ali did not have the five brigades he had been told of. In the Awqaf building on 7 June at 00:30, Khattib refused to leave, saying Jerusalem was his adopted city. Ata Ali told him his officers had deserted and quietly took his troops out through the Dung Gate. Khalidi ran the civilian side after the army left. Khattib and Khalidi were captured by Israeli paratroopers at the Awqaf on the morning of 7 June.
- Quote (Khalidi): "if we fight the city will be destroyed."
- Source: S. Abdallah Schleifer, "The Fall of Jerusalem, 1967" (Institute for Palestine Studies). https://www.palquest.org/sites/default/files/The_Fall_of_Jerusalem_1967-S._Abdallah_Schleifer.pdf
- Tag: [arab] (a journalist's reconstruction from Jordanian participants; details such as the Dung Gate exit are [disputed] against Israeli accounts)

**T18. Maj. Mansour Kraishan (commander, Second King Hussein Battalion); Musa Bitar (Civil Defence); Bahjat Abu Gharbiyeh and Ishak Duzdar (civilian resistance committee).** Old City, 5 to 7 June. [arab]
- Witnessed: Abu Gharbiyeh received 260 Enfield rifles, 20 Stens and 20 Brens on 5 June from the Governorate; both civilian leaders slept outside the walls and were cut off that night. On 6 June at Herod's Gate Kraishan reported only 69 men left after his headquarters was bombed. Bitar's unit, working with the Sisters of Zion, tended wounded soldiers and civilians on the Via Dolorosa. Before dawn on 7 June a Jordanian sergeant and two Palestinian soldiers returned to the Awqaf "to seek martyrdom" and dug in near the Golden Gate with a bazooka.
- Source: same Schleifer article (T17). Schleifer says Kraishan died on the Jordan River on 14 February 1968.
- Tag: [arab]

**T19. Azzam Abu Saud, aged 19 in 1967, student from Beit Hanina.** Volunteer with Jordanian forces. [arab]
- Witnessed: his training camp near Jericho was bombed on the afternoon of 5 June. He retreated to Jordan on Wednesday (7 June), burned his uniform and put on civilian clothes. His family's home near the Western Wall was later demolished.
- Quote: "I burned my uniform" (his phrase, shortened).
- Source: AFP via inkl, "Israeli paratrooper and Palestinian recall conflict 50 years on." https://www.inkl.com/news/six-day-war-israeli-paratrooper-and-palestinian-recall-conflict-50-years-on
- Tag: [arab]

### 1C. Palestinian civilians: Jerusalem, West Bank, Gaza

**T20. Haifa Khalidi, aged 19 in June 1967, born in the house beside the Mughrabi Quarter.** Account relayed by the French historian Vincent Lemire, April 2017. [arab]
- Witnessed: watched the demolition from her family's bathroom window over two days and two nights (11 to 12 June in her account). She says residents had two hours to leave and two elderly women were found dead under the rubble of their house. Lemire gives about 135 houses and 700 residents.
- Quote: "the noise, the dust, the screams, the tears."
- Source: Mondoweiss, "Bulldozers on Shavuot 1967." https://mondoweiss.net/2017/04/bulldozers-shavuot-1967/
- Tag: [arab] [disputed] (dates: AP gives 10 to 11 June, see T21; the secondary-source relay may blur dates)

**T21. Mohammad Assawaf (90), Mohammad Ali (77), and Mohammad Abu Znaid (10 in 1967).** Palestinians of the Jewish Quarter and Moroccan Quarter, Old City. [arab]
- Witnessed: Assawaf ran a bakery in the Jewish Quarter. His house was bulldozed, he refused cash offers for the bakery, and gave it up after a 1980s court fight. Ali left soon after the war expecting life to improve. Abu Znaid's family walked barefoot to Shuafat when troops took the Old City; he now lives in the camp. AP states the Moroccan Quarter, about 650 people, was bulldozed on 10 to 11 June and that Israel offered free buses from Damascus Gate.
- Quote (Assawaf): "All those who left regretted it."
- Source: AP via Christian Today. https://www.christiantoday.com/news/palestinians-recall-1967-uprooting-from-jerusalems-old-city
- Tag: [arab]

**T22. Unnamed Old City Palestinian (coffee-store family).** [arab]
- Witnessed: the shop closed when war began. On 6 June, he says, Israeli soldiers in a courtyard shot his two brothers and an uncle. The family fled to Jordan over the Allenby Bridge. Soldiers' order as he recalled it: "Leave here with your hands in the air."
- Source: Khaleej Times (AFP), "Witnesses to history." https://www.khaleejtimes.com/world/mena/witnesses-to-history-israelis-palestinians-recount-six-day-war (one quote per source: paraphrased here)
- Tag: [arab] [unverified]

**T23. Unnamed former PLA fighter, Gaza.** Border base near Khan Yunis. [arab]
- Witnessed: on day one Israeli tanks entered and his unit was unprepared. On day three he returned to Gaza City and found it badly damaged.
- Quote: "I knew we had lost."
- Source: same Khaleej Times article.
- Tag: [arab]

**T24. Unnamed Ramallah-area villager.** [arab]
- Witnessed: Israeli troops entered his village and searched the houses. He says about half the residents left for Jordan. Same pattern as T14.
- Source: same Khaleej Times article.
- Tag: [arab]

**T25. Hajj Jumaa Abu Hadrous ("Abu Hassan"), aged 76 at interview; al-Maghazi camp, Gaza.** [arab]
- Witnessed: saw Israeli tanks gather about 500 metres off at the camp's eastern edge. Tanks entered at night backed by aircraft. He fled with his wife and three children to the coastal village of Zawaida for about a week. He remembers Ramadan 1970 under curfew with little to eat but tomatoes.
- Quote: "We did not think it was a war."
- Source: Electronic Intifada, "We were sleeping when Israel attacked." https://electronicintifada.net/node/12522
- Tag: [arab]

**T26. Ghazai Misleh, aged 10 in 1967; al-Maghazi camp.** [arab]
- Witnessed: carried his little sister to Zawaida. The family returned within days. His father stopped his mother cooking over open fire because Israelis shot at smoke.
- Source: same Electronic Intifada article.
- Tag: [arab]

**T27. Brother Guy Khoury, French Trappist monk, Latroun monastery.** Private diary. Foreign witness to Palestinian villagers. [arab / foreign observer]
- Witnessed: from 5 June villagers of Imwas, Yalo and Beit Nuba fled or were ordered out; about 70 mostly elderly people, women and children sheltered at the monastery. On 12 June an Israeli officer ordered them to leave for Ramallah. Bulldozers demolished the villages on the night of 25 to 26 June, all three gone by 28 June. Monks buried bodies found in the fields. Diary entries name Daoud al-Kbaybi, who could not retrieve his two blind aunts from the rubble, and a young disabled man, Hassan Shoukri, who disappeared.
- Quote (Israeli officer to a monk): "Father, are there any Arabs inside the monastery?"
- Source: Zochrot, from the film "Memory of the Cactus." https://www.zochrot.org/testimonies/view/56362/en
- Tag: [arab] [disputed] (Israeli sources describe the demolitions as a security measure; the 25 June cabinet decision is cited from Al-Haq, not read).

### 1D. Syrian soldiers and civilians, Golan

**T28. Abu Hussein, displaced Quneitra resident.** Child in 1967. [arab]
- Witnessed: remembers a radio statement that led his father to take the family out, expecting a short absence. The statement is the early announcement of Quneitra's fall (Radio Damascus, 10 June), which RESEARCH.md covers.
- Source: Enab Baladi, 2020. https://english.enabbaladi.net/archives/2020/12/quneitra-a-witness-to-two-wars-and-two-displacement-flights/
- Tag: [arab]

**T29. Umm Hussain, woman in her seventies, Quneitra.** [arab]
- Witnessed: blames Hafez al-Assad, then Defence Minister, for losing the Golan. The article does not say what she personally saw.
- Quote: "It was sold."
- Source: same Enab Baladi article.
- Tag: [arab] [disputed] (a popular Syrian belief; historians do not agree on responsibility for the early announcement and the withdrawal order)

**T30. Duraid Lahham, comedian working at Syrian TV.** [arab]
- Witnessed: believed early broadcasts that Syrian forces would soon be in Tel Aviv. He later wrote the satire "Masrah al-Shawk" about the defeat.
- Quote: "I believed them."
- Source: Gulf News, "1967 defeat left huge imprint on Syrian psyche." https://gulfnews.com/world/mena/1967-defeat-left-huge-imprint-on-syrian-pysche-1.2039734
- Tag: [arab]

**T31. Muhieddine Salhani (23), driver at the Presidential Palace, Damascus; and Dr Nicolas Chahine, intern from Beirut.** [arab]
- Witnessed: Salhani says Damascus broke down in grief. Chahine went to Damascus to join the army, was sent by his father to a state hospital, saw chaos there, and from the family summer house at Bloudan watched explosions on Mount Hermon.
- Source: same Gulf News article.
- Tag: [arab]

**T32. Anonymous Syrian battalion at Tel Faher (Tel Fakhr).** [arab / unverified]
- Account: told as "the Syrian story" in an opinion column. The battalion allegedly got a retreat order but dozens disobeyed and kept firing: "they fell one by one" (the column's phrase). The author names no source and no men.
- Source: The National, "The Arab heroes whose names never found mention in history." https://www.thenationalnews.com/opinion/the-arab-heroes-whose-names-never-found-mention-in-history-1.35759
- Tag: [arab] [unverified]

**T33. ICRC delegates in the Golan and Michael Comay (Israeli Foreign Ministry).** Foreign and Israeli official voices on the Syrian civilians. [israeli / foreign observer]
- Account: ICRC sub-delegation reports of July 1967 on Quneitra, Mansura and Farj recorded the remaining civilians. In July 1968 Comay wrote to Shlomo Gazit that expelling Arabs from Quneitra had gone on for months and it was better to end the problem at once, humanely. Gazit argued that Syrians leaving for Syria were not being expelled. Mansura, with 250 to 300 residents before filming, had six left; Farj, with about 60, was found empty. Akevot says most files remain closed.
- Source: Akevot Institute, "Displacement in the Golan." https://www.akevot.org.il/en/article/displacement-in-the-golan/?full
- Tag: [israeli] [disputed]

### 1E. Israeli soldiers, reservists, kibbutzniks

**T34. Col. (res.) Yossi Langotsky, Jerusalem Reconnaissance Unit.** [israeli]
- Witnessed: fought in Jerusalem, won a medal near Ramat Rachel, and took part in close combat.
- Quote: "I personally killed Jordanian soldiers at close range."
- Source: Times of Israel, 2016/2017 Ammunition Hill reunion. https://www.timesofisrael.com/on-ammunition-hill-veterans-from-both-banks-of-the-jordan-find-peace/
- Tag: [israeli]

**T35. Nachum Baruchi, mechanised-infantry company commander, Harel Brigade, north of Jerusalem.** [israeli]
- Witnessed: a Jordanian tank commander aimed his gun at him, then his tank went up in flames. Same article: 36 Israeli and 71 Jordanian soldiers died at Ammunition Hill and the police school on 6 June [estimate, differing counts below].
- Source: same Times of Israel article.
- Tag: [israeli]

**T36. Dr Yitzhak Yifat, corporal, paratroopers.** [israeli]
- Witnessed: called up 23 May. Fought at Ammunition Hill early on 6 June, entered the Old City the next day and reached the Western Wall (in Rubinger's photograph).
- Quote: "We realised that we had conquered another people."
- Source: AFP via inkl (see T19). https://www.inkl.com/news/six-day-war-israeli-paratrooper-and-palestinian-recall-conflict-50-years-on
- Tag: [israeli]

**T37. Yeivin, Israeli mother in a Jerusalem frontline apartment.** [israeli]
- Witnessed: sheltered with 12 people and three children (aged 10, 9, 3) behind sandbags. One heavy mortar barrage came at midnight.
- Source: Khaleej Times (see T22). https://www.khaleejtimes.com/world/mena/witnesses-to-history-israelis-palestinians-recount-six-day-war
- Tag: [israeli]

**T38. Unnamed Israeli armoured soldier, Golan.** [israeli]
- Account: says his unit's tank strength fell from 25 to three in a day of Syrian artillery. Which day and unit are not given. Source as T37.
- Tag: [israeli] [unverified]

**T39. Shai, Asher and Dan: kibbutz soldiers recorded for "Soldiers' Talk" (Siach Lohamim), summer 1967.** [israeli]
- Account: Shai, 27, paratroop lieutenant from Afikim, on the uncertainty before combat. Asher, 32, from Mishmar HaNegev, on being strafed in Sinai, and on seeing Egyptian soldiers marching: they reminded him of Jews wandering through Europe. Dan, a decorated major from Gat, on losing respect for human life. First names only, as published. The book appeared in October 1967 and sold five times its first print run in a year.
- Quote (Asher): "the Jews wandering through Europe."
- Source: Moment Magazine, 2017. https://momentmag.com/six-day-war-veterans-felt-revisiting-soldiers-talk/
- Tag: [israeli]

**T40. Unnamed soldiers in "Censored Voices" (the uncut tapes, about 200 hours).** [israeli]
- Account: collected by Amos Oz and Avraham Shapira after the war. Soldiers speak of orders to empty villages, of killing prisoners (one says his unit killed 50 in a night), of separating men from a convoy and killing them, and of a Jericho column that recalled World War II. Director Mor Loushy says the film used 3 to 4 such testimonies, while the originals held 30 to 40. Shapira says the military censor confiscated material.
- Source: Egypt Independent (from Al-Masry Al-Youm), 6 June 2015. https://www.egyptindependent.com/new-documents-reveal-israeli-war-crimes-against-egyptian-prisoners-war-1967/ (note the headline's "war crimes" is the paper's own word)
- Tag: [israeli] [disputed] (no names, no dates; the "50 in one night" claim is [unverified])

**T41. Elad Peled, commander of the Northern Command division on the Golan.** [israeli]
- Account: per Haaretz's 2026 investigation, he took the decision to bring bulldozers to destroy villages so residents could not return.
- Quote: "so there would be nowhere to return."
- Source: Anadolu Agency summary of Haaretz (Adam Raz, Akevot). https://www.aa.com.tr/en/middle-east/newly-disclosed-israeli-testimonies-detail-expulsions-killings-during-1967-war-report/3957493
- Tag: [israeli] [disputed] (I read only the Anadolu summary, not Haaretz)

**T42. Ishai Amrami, deputy battalion commander, Latrun area.** [israeli]
- Account: per the same summary, he saw the emptying of the Latrun villages as an attempt at mass population transfer. Other unnamed soldiers in the report speak of killings in Gaza camps after the war and of orders to shoot at people crossing back to the West Bank. The report says military records show nearly 150 Palestinians killed trying to return from Jordan by early September 1967.
- Source: same Anadolu/Haaretz summary.
- Tag: [israeli] [disputed]

**T43. Zvi Bloch, aged 90 in 2022, military commander of Kibbutz Nahshon in 1967.** [israeli]
- Account: reported by journalist Yossi Melman. Egyptian commando forces near Latrun clashed with Israeli troops and kibbutz members. Mortar fire lit dry brush and at least 20 Egyptians died in the blaze. Next day soldiers dug a pit with a bulldozer and buried them. Bloch said soldiers looted the dead and that people felt ashamed. He said the silence suited everyone.

### 1F. The El Arish and Sinai prisoner allegations: claims and disputes

The existing brief (RESEARCH.md section 5) names the 1995 and 2007 allegations in two lines. Here are the actors.

**T44. Aryeh Yitzhaki, military historian, Bar-Ilan University (Israel Radio, August 1995).** [israeli]
- Claim: knew of hundreds of Egyptian prisoners killed in 1967. The worst case he knew of was 300 to 400 Egyptian commandos, many having surrendered, killed in El Arish by the Shaked commando unit under Lt. Col. Binyamin Ben-Eliezer. A report he wrote in 1968 was withheld on orders from above.
- Same story: Reserve Brig. Gen. Aryeh Biro admitted ordering 49 Egyptian prisoners shot in October 1956. That is 1956, not 1967.
- Response: Ben-Eliezer said he knew of no such killings. PM Rabin called such incidents exceptions everyone should condemn.
- Source: JTA, 1995. https://www.jta.org/archive/historian-alleges-pow-deaths-in-1956-1967
- Tag: [israeli] [disputed]

**T45. Binyamin Ben-Eliezer, commander of Shaked unit (denial).** [israeli]
- Claim: those killed (about 250 in the 2007 film "Ruach Shaked") were Palestinian fedayeen killed in combat, not Egyptian prisoners. He suggested confusion with an occasion when his unit gave food and water to an Egyptian battalion that had stopped fighting. He postponed a Cairo visit.
- Egyptian reaction: Al-Ahram called it "A Massacre"; the foreign ministry summoned the Israeli ambassador; lawmakers asked for investigation and ICC action. Earlier, a lawsuit against Sharon had been dismissed for lack of evidence (Al Jazeera, T13).
- Source: Daily News Egypt, 6 March 2007. https://www.dailynewsegypt.com/2007/03/06/storm-brewing-between-israel-and-egypt-over-pows/
- Tag: [israeli] [disputed]

**Where it stands.** Yitzhaki says 300 to 400 killed, the film says 250, the Egyptian witnesses say lines of prisoners shot, Ben-Eliezer says armed fedayeen in combat, and Zvi Bloch's Latrun account (T43) is a separate event, not El Arish. No independent inquiry has established the facts. [disputed]

### 1G. Foreign observers

**T46. Marcel Boisard, Swiss ICRC delegate, Cairo.** [established as witness; numbers are his estimate]
- Witnessed: in Cairo at about 8:30 am on 5 June, saw the air raids, a little over two hours. A few days later, ICRC Geneva asked him to organise the return of Egyptian soldiers wandering in Sinai. He made makeshift Red Cross flags from hospital sheets with Jihane Sadat, crossed the canal by boat at El-Kantara, and met Dayan about 10 to 12 June, who promised an ICRC representative aboard each helicopter. At eastern El-Kantara thousands in pyjamas gathered; warning shots kept them from a well; those who spoke English or wore Western underwear were separated as suspected officers. He estimates 20,000 to 30,000 soldiers crossed in two days, with no records kept, and about 100 were taken to Atlit.
- Quote: "Do you speak English?"

## 5. Disagreements and gaps

**Disagreements**
1. Dead on each side. Israeli 736 / 776 / 779 / 983; Egypt 3,000 to 15,000; Jordan 700 versus 6,000; Syria 600 to 2,500. The gap is mostly definitions (killed, died of wounds, missing, civilians, "casualties"). See section 2.1. [disputed] [estimate]
2. Prisoners and killings. El Arish, 250 or 300 to 400 killed in the Shaked allegation; Ben-Eliezer says fedayeen in combat; Latrun, at least 20 Egyptians dead in a fire, which is a separate claim; Bedouin accounts of men shot and run over. No inquiry. [disputed]
3. Egyptian prisoner treatment. El Sayed describes convoy ambush and executions at Atlit; Darwish and Gomaa describe beatings and humiliation; none is corroborated here. Months held: five, eight, or until January 1968. [disputed]
4. Palestinian flight versus expulsion. UN and Palestinian sources describe pressure, leaflets, loudspeakers; Israeli officials' reports differ. Haaretz's 2026 investigation (via Anadolu) says that Imwas, Yalo and Beit Nuba, and Golan villages, were destroyed on purpose. [disputed]
5. Golan population. 90,000 (Military Intelligence), 100,000 to 150,000 (others), 3,936 in the August census, and the question of how many were expelled versus fled. [disputed]
6. Responsibility for the early announcement of Quneitra's fall, and Assad's role (T29). [disputed]
7. Shazly's escape: skilled evasion or abandonment of command. [disputed]
8. Mughrabi Quarter dates: 10 to 11 June (AP) or 11 to 12 June (Haifa Khalidi), houses 135, residents 650 to 700. [disputed]
9. UNEF dead: 15 or 27. [disputed]
10. Dupuy's Ammunition Hill figures versus the memorial figures (T35, section 2.1). [disputed]
