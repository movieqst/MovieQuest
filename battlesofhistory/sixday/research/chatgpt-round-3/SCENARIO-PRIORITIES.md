# Scenario design priorities

**First playable tactical prototype:** Abu-Ageila / Umm Qatef (Sinai, night of 5–6 June). Map must verify road network, trenches, minefields, artillery and armored approaches from 1967-specific sources.

**Campaign timeline:** simultaneous events across air, Sinai, Jerusalem/West Bank and Golan. Do not imply that one force marched continuously from Sinai to Jerusalem to Golan.

**Possible later tactical episodes:** Rafah, Ammunition Hill, Tel Faher, and selected Golan/West Bank positions.

**Non-combat educational scenes:** May crisis, UNEF, Straits of Tiran, USS Liberty, civilian displacement, ceasefire and Resolution 242.

**Sprites:** period-specific Israeli, Egyptian, Jordanian, Syrian and Iraqi equipment, 4x4 on #00FF00 as standard for Battles of History. No post-1967 weapons or flags.
