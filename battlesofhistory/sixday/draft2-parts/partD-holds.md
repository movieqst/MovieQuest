- **Original:** "At about two in the afternoon, two Mirage jets led by Captain Iftach Spector attacked with cannon and rockets. Two Super Mystères followed with napalm and cannon fire."
- **Removed:** Spector's name, "two" Mirages, "two" Super Mystères, rockets, and the Super Mystères' cannon fire.
- **To restore:** the Israeli Air Force operational record (or an Israeli official history citing it) for pilots, numbers and weapons. The Navy's analysis questions rockets on the Mirages.
- **Review:** Chapter 22, "two Mirage jets … cannon and rockets" ("retain aircraft/pilot detail only from the operational record") [Navy-Liberty].

### H2. Ch 23: Syrian radios
- **Original:** "…and units lacked radios between tanks and infantry." (in the *arab: Syrian* purge bullet)

### H7. Ch 25 and Debrief: Syrian dead "up to two thousand five hundred" and "by most counts"
- **Original (ch 25):** "Syrian dead are counted anywhere from about one hundred and fifty to two thousand five hundred; Syria never published its losses."
- **Original (debrief):** "Syria: from about one thousand to two thousand five hundred killed, by most counts; lower figures exist too. Syria never published its losses."
- **Removed:** the 2,500-dead upper bound, the 1,000 lower bound, "by most counts", and "never published".
- **To restore:** an identified source giving 2,500 (or 1,000) Syrian dead, as opposed to wounded. Dunstan p. 79 gives 2,500 as the number **wounded**, so the upper bound may come from a misreading. "By most counts" also needs a survey of sources. The 145/150 low figure (golan.md S33) can go on the source card.
- **Review:** Debrief Losses, Syria; Dunstan row 35 and section 4.
