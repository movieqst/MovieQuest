## What to do
1. Copy your part of the script (line range given in your task) and apply **every** change the review asks for in it: its replacement wording, tag changes, deletions and splits. Where Dunstan's check applies to your chapters, apply its corrections too, subject to its own reservations; where the review and Dunstan conflict, follow the review and note it.
2. **"Hold" items:** remove the held detail from the narration (rewrite the sentence around it so it still reads well, or drop the line). Do not keep it with "one account says". List each removed detail in your holds file.
3. **Do not add new facts from memory.** Only the review's or Dunstan's wording, or simplifications of what is already there. If the review gives no replacement, make the minimal edit that removes the problem.
4. **Spoken English for students aged 12+:** short sentences (split anything over about 25 words), explain a term the first time it is heard, say numbers the way a narrator would read them. Keep the human voices and testimony, introduced as "later recalled", "later wrote", "said" with the speaker named.
5. **Keep the format exactly**, because tools parse it:
   - headings as they are (`## Part …`, `### N. Title`, the `*Map: … · date*` line under each chapter heading);
   - one bullet per sentence-group, each ending with its tag in `*(…)*` at the end of the line;
   - tags only from the review's table: `established`, `israeli`, `arab: Egyptian` / `Jordanian` / `Syrian` / `Iraqi` / `Palestinian`, `disputed`, `estimate`, `reconstruction` (a note after a semicolon inside the brackets is fine, e.g. `*(estimate; UN figures)*`).
6. Keep the chapter numbering and titles unless the review asks to change a title.
