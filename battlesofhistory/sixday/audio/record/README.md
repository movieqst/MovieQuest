**Still to find:** the fourth recording released in 2017 (Times of Israel, "Soundtrack to a legendary victory", 12 February 2017): **pilot Yoram Harpaz** (1 h 2 min). He was shot down and killed over Dimona by Israeli air defence after he was taken for an enemy intruder: his radio had failed when his plane hit power cables during a strike in Jordanian territory. Probably on Commons as "Voice recordings from Operation Focus. II.webm" (to check). Use only the Commons copy (CC BY-SA 3.0, VRT ticket #2019122710000829), never the SoundCloud one. A friendly-fire death: if used at all, handle it with care and context.
Times of Israel's notes on the others agree with ours: Danny Manor was hit by a missile from an Egyptian fighter over Abu Suweir and released in a prisoner exchange after the war; Mordechai Lavon's jet was damaged by debris from an Egyptian plane whose pilot ejected, he came down in the sea off Gaza, was picked up by a UN vessel and returned through the International Red Cross, and was killed nine years later on reserve duty.
