## Who and what (from ChatGPT's review, with its sources)
- **Barkan** (ברקן), not "Parkan": Yitzhak Barkan, commander of the Israeli Air Force's northern air-control unit at Mount Meron, named in the 2017 coverage of these recordings. A surname, not a call sign. Do not assign lines to particular speakers without listening.
- **נ״מ** ("Nun-Mem"): anti-aircraft defence (guns or missiles), not a call sign.
- **Allegra, Yona, 110, 6662:** unresolved designations; left as heard, not explained.
- **Megiddo:** a little-used airstrip in the Jezreel Valley (some accounts call it a decoy field), not the same as Ramat David air base. **Hatzor:** the air base near Ashdod.
- **Who attacked Megiddo:** Syrian MiG-17s, on 5 June 1967 (Hartoch, *A History of the Syrian Air Force 1947–1967*, PhD thesis, King's College London, 2015; *Air Force Magazine*, October 1967). One 2017 article (Ynet) links the recording to the Iraqi Tu-16 raid of 6 June instead: a conflict to note. The radio alarm itself names no nationality.

**Suggested context for the page (our words, not a quotation):** On 5 June 1967, Syrian MiG-17s attacked the Megiddo airstrip in northern Israel. In this recording, Israeli air controllers follow the attack and move their fighters, while most of the air force is away striking Egypt's airfields.
