# "Focus IV" (Danny Manor), 5 June 1967: transcript and English translation (version 2)

**Recording:** focus-iv-danny-manor-radio-1967.mp3 (1 h 02 min). IDF Spokesperson's Unit / IDF and Defense Establishment Archive, CC BY-SA 3.0 (Wikimedia Commons; VRT ticket #2019122710000829).
**How this was made (12 November 2026):** the audio was cleaned (band-pass and noise reduction) and transcribed by machine (OpenAI Whisper, large-v3-turbo, with strict settings to stop it inventing words); the English is Claude's translation, reviewed line by line by ChatGPT from the text (`focus-iv-danny-manor-radio-1967-chatgpt-review.md`), which compared the ejection exchange (9:27–9:49) with the excerpt published by NRG on 12 February 2017. **No Hebrew speaker has listened to the audio.** On the page, only lines marked *sure* may be captions, labelled "machine transcript and translation, not checked by a Hebrew speaker".
**What is in this table:** a control room directing Israeli strike formations against Egyptian airfields. Someone reads out the clock about once a minute ("השעה עשר ושבע דקות", "the time is seven minutes past ten"); Whisper mishears "השעה" in many ways (התנאה, הטעה, עצה, הרפאה…), but the readings run in order from about 9:50 to about 10:55, so the recording covers about 09:50 to 10:54 Israeli time on the morning of 5 June, during the second wave of the strikes (the first attacks went in at 07:45, the second wave at 09:34, IDF timeline). The minute readings drift by about two minutes against the recording, so do not work out exact seconds. The two controllers most often named are **Robert** and **Menachem** (very likely Robert Amit, commander of the southern control unit at Hafetz Haim, and Menachem Zahavi at Mitzpe Ramon, both named in the 2017 coverage; the chief controller, Shmuel Kislev, is named in the published ejection exchange). Voices are not identified line by line. Formations have code names (Balfour, Dira, Kotel or Kutna, Kishon, Tmicha, Mashkof, Tzaif, Gader, Savion, Perfect, Talem, Yitzlav, Lola…): call signs, not explained, and not to be translated literally. מבנה here means a formation of aircraft, not a building; "number 3", "Four" are places in a formation. Most lines are call signs, grid squares and headings; the table keeps those that carry meaning, and the rest is in the raw file, `focus-iv-danny-manor-radio-1967.srt`. Whisper still wrote a few false "תודה רבה" (thank you) over silences; these are ignored.
**Places heard:** Egyptian airfields Inshas, Abu Suweir, Kabrit, Fayid, El Mansura, Bir Gifgafa (also called Melez: one airfield), Bir Thamada, El Arish, perhaps El Minya; also Bir Lahfan, Port Said, the Suez Canal, Bardawil, the Kuntilla road, Nitzana, Gaza, Hatzor and Ramat David.
**Marks:** *sure* = a short phrase with one obvious reading and meaning; *likely* = partly misheard but the meaning is clear from context; *unclear* = not to be quoted. [ ] = gaps or added words; [?] = a reading to check.

**What it shows (from ChatGPT's review, with its sources):** Israeli air controllers on the morning of 5 June 1967, about 09:50 to 10:54, during the second wave of Operation Focus. They send formations to Egyptian airfields, change targets, watch fuel and communications, check unknown aircraft so as not to fire on their own, and discuss sending aircraft against armour on the Kuntilla road in Sinai; a separate exchange concerns a Nord and a helicopter searching the sea off Gaza (possibly the Lavon search; not proved). Early on (9:27–10:20) the controllers receive a report that a pilot named Manor has ejected west of Abu Suweir, west of the Suez Canal, after coming out of an attack; the published 2017 excerpt matches this exchange. What the controllers say is what they were told at the time, not confirmed results.

**Manor: a conflict to resolve.** The archive's title calls him Danny Manor, and the 2017 press (Ynet, Times of Israel) says an Egyptian missile hit his aircraft on an Abu Suweir mission, he ejected, was held in Abbassia prison in Cairo, and was later released. But the official memorial records **First Lieutenant Amiram Manor**, aged 21, killed on 5 June 1967, flying a Super Mystère in a formation attacking Kabrit, which was intercepted by MiG-21s on the way back (Izkor; IDF investigation of his leader, Alexander Armon). One specialist list questions whether a separate "Danny Manor" prisoner existed. ChatGPT could not settle this. **Until it is checked against IAF loss and prisoner records, the page should say only "a pilot named Manor", and give no aircraft, fate or biography.**

**For the page (our words, not a quotation):** On the morning of 5 June 1967, during Israel's air attacks on Egyptian airfields, air controllers directed and redirected formations, watched their fuel and looked out for enemy aircraft. In this recording they also try to find out what happened to a pilot named Manor, reported to have ejected near Abu Suweir.

**Do not say:** that the SA-2 site south of Port Said was destroyed, that a strike on the Kuntilla road succeeded, that the El Arish runway was destroyed (it was spared for Israeli use), or which Manor this was.
