**How this was made (12 November 2026):** the audio was cleaned (band-pass and noise reduction) and transcribed by machine (OpenAI Whisper, large-v3-turbo, with strict settings to stop it inventing words); the English is Claude's translation. **Not yet reviewed by ChatGPT. No Hebrew speaker has listened to the audio.** On the page, only lines marked *sure* may be captions, labelled "machine transcript and translation, not checked by a Hebrew speaker".
**What is in this table:** a long recording of an air-control room's telephone lines through the afternoon of 5 June 1967 (one line says "it's five in the evening", 32:56). Most of it is call signs, grid squares, board numbers (147, 116, 110…) and code names (Margo, Miriam, Marian, Allegra, Shoshan…) that mean nothing without the controllers' boards, and much is too noisy for Whisper. The table keeps only the lines that carry meaning; the rest is in the raw file, `focus-iii-mordechai-lavon-radio-1967.srt`. Whisper still wrote a few false "תודה רבה" (thank you) over silences; these are ignored. Names heard: Amnon, Alex, Ela, Lazar, Amitai, Sharon (not identified; first names, so probably not the well-known people of those names).
**Marks:** *sure* = a short phrase with one obvious reading and meaning; *likely* = partly misheard but the meaning is clear from context; *unclear* = not to be quoted. [ ] = gaps or added words; [?] = a reading to check.

**What it seems to show (to be checked):** an air-control room on the afternoon of 5 June 1967, following a downed pilot named Lavon. A helicopter and a Nord transport search the sea off Gaza and find a yellow object like a dinghy; then word comes that the pilot was picked up by a boat and is in Gaza harbour, apparently in Egyptian hands, while Israeli armour moves on Gaza and El Arish, and the search aircraft are sent home. Around this, the controllers pass on radio news (Egyptian claims of Israeli aircraft shot down; shelling of Jerusalem and of Tirat Zvi in the Beit She'an Valley), reports of MiGs over the Galilee and near Megiddo, and the evening's count of enemy aircraft destroyed. **The identity of Mordechai Lavon, how and where he came down, and what happened to him are not yet checked**; the recording's Commons title is the only source so far that he is the "Lavon" named.

**For the page (our words, draft, not a quotation):** On the afternoon of 5 June 1967, Israeli air controllers searched the sea off Gaza for a downed pilot, then learned that he had been picked up and taken into Gaza.
