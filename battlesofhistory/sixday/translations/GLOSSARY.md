# Six-Day War narration: Arabic and Hebrew glossary (for the translators)

The English script (`../NARRATION-SCRIPT.md`, draft 3) is fact-checked and deliberately neutral: it says who says what, and
never takes a side. The translations must keep exactly that: same facts, same attributions, same hedges ("about", "roughly",
"accounts differ", "X said"), same tags. Do not add, drop, soften or sharpen anything. Where English uses a word chosen with
care (occupied, displaced, fled or were expelled, captured, retaken, armistice line), use the matching neutral word below.

Register: Arabic in Modern Standard Arabic (فصحى), plain and clear for school students. Hebrew in plain modern Hebrew. No
vowel points (nikud / tashkeel) in the text, except where a name would otherwise be misread. Numbers: write them as the English
does (words stay words, figures stay figures). Dates: "the 5th of June" -> Arabic «في الخامس من يونيو/حزيران» (use يونيو), Hebrew «ב-5 ביוני».

Tags: keep every *(tag)* exactly as in English (established, israeli, arab: Egyptian, disputed, estimate, reconstruction, and any
note inside the brackets), in English, at the end of the line, so the lines can be matched with the English ones.

Place and person names not in this list: use the usual Arabic / Hebrew spelling for Arab and Israeli names (Arabic names in
Hebrew as Israeli media write them; Hebrew names in Arabic as Arab media write them); for others, the usual transliteration.
Quotations: the script's quotations are mostly translated from Hebrew, Arabic or English already. Translate them faithfully; if
a quotation was originally spoken in your target language and you are confident of the original wording, you may use it, and
add a note at the end of your file listing which ones you restored and why. Otherwise translate and do not pretend it is the original.
