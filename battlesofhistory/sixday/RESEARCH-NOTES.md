# Six-Day War: comparing the two research rounds

Two independent first rounds: my brief (RESEARCH.md, built from search summaries; the main reference sites could not be
opened from here) and ChatGPT's dossier (2 November 2026, based on Oren, Segev, Laron, Popp, Shlaim, Khalidi, Barker,
the UN, FRUS vol. XIX, UNRWA, the NSA's Liberty archive and the IDF). Where they agree, the point is stronger; where
they differ, both are kept and the difference noted. Nothing is narrated until it is checked against a named source.

## Where the two agree (confirmed by both)
- **The chain of the crisis:**
  - the false Soviet report (13 May);
  - Egyptian troops into Sinai (14–15 May);
  - Fawzi's request for the UNEF frontier posts (16 May), then Riad's termination of consent and U Thant's order (18 May);
  - the Straits of Tiran closed (22–23 May);
  - the Egypt–Jordan pact (30 May), with the Egyptian general Abdul Munim Riad commanding the Jordanian front;
  - Dayan becomes defence minister (1 June);
  - the cabinet decides on war (4 June).
- **The disputed questions, in the same terms:** did Nasser intend war; was the strike pre-emptive or preventive; Moscow's
  motive; responsibility for the Syrian border clashes (Dayan's interviews with Rami Tal, published 1997); why Jordan
  entered; the USS Liberty; why Quneitra's fall was announced early; Resolution 242's wording; Khartoum's "three no's";
  the killing of prisoners; how many Palestinians were displaced, and how.
- **Operation Focus:** about 07:45 Israeli time; the IDF's figure of 197 Egyptian aircraft in the opening wave; about
  400 Arab aircraft over the war (IDF). The per-airfield sorties are not established in either round.
- **The three Sinai thrusts:** Tal (Rafah–El Arish), Yoffe (between, through the dunes) and Sharon (Abu-Ageila/Um Katef,
  night of 5–6 June). Then Amer's withdrawal order (6 June), the passes, and the canal.
- **The other fronts:** Ammunition Hill (6 June); the Lions' Gate and the Western Wall (7 June). The Golan on 9–10 June
  (Tel Fakhr and Tel Azaziat by Golani, the 8th Armoured at Qala, Banias, Quneitra announced early). The ceasefire at
  about 18:30 on 10 June (IDF chronology).
- **The USS Liberty:** 34 killed, 171 wounded (171–174 in some sources); deliberate or mistaken is disputed.

## Additions from ChatGPT (to keep)
- **UNEF did not vanish on 16 May:** the withdrawal was phased and was completed on **17 June** (UN A/6730, paras
  24–30). The map should show the decision on 18 May, then a phased withdrawal.
- **Murtagi** as commander of the Sinai front is named by ChatGPT too. My round could not confirm it: keep it as
  "Oren" until a page is checked.
- **Casualty figures:** ChatGPT explains the Jordanian discrepancy. About **700 killed** (Oren, via Mutawi) and the US
  Navy's **6,000** are different counts: the US Navy figure is casualties (killed, wounded and missing), not deaths.
  Israeli dead: **736** (Israeli Ministry of Defence timeline), **776** (US Naval History and Heritage Command), **779**
  (other sources). Show the range, and name the counts.
- **Displacement:** UNRWA's 1967 report gives more than 300,000 people made homeless or leaving, about 120,000 of them
  already Palestine refugees (displaced a second time). The Secretary-General gives about 113,000 registered refugees
  plus 210,000 others. Kept as **estimate**, with "flight, expulsion and barriers to return" as **disputed** by place.
- **Photos with licences stated on their Commons pages:** a Government Press Office photo of soldiers in the field,
  28 May 1967 (D325-068, CC BY-SA 4.0; the user's screenshot of the page confirms it); Boris Carmi's Jerusalem,
  6 June 1967 (National Library of Israel, CC BY 4.0); a paratrooper's wedding at the Western Wall, 9 June 1967
  (GPO D327-048, CC BY-SA 4.0). Each is to be downloaded and checked before use.

## Where my round adds detail (to verify)
- **Map coordinates** for the places on each front (RESEARCH.md §8). ChatGPT rightly declined to give coordinates it
  had not verified. Mine are flagged where I estimated them (Um Katef, Sharm el-Sheikh, Tel Fakhr, Butmiya); they are
  to be checked against the real-terrain guides when the maps are built.
- **UNEF's own losses:** 15 killed, 14 Indian and 1 Brazilian (UN S/7930, S/7957).
- **Jerusalem, Israeli count:** about 6,000 shells fell on the Israeli side and about 900 buildings were damaged.
  This is an Israeli estimate.
- **Imwas, Yalu and Beit Nuba:** emptied and destroyed; the site later became Canada Park. Both rounds flag these
  villages for individual research.

## What neither round has yet
- the unit-level order of battle and hour-by-hour timings on each front;
- the per-airfield results of the air strikes;
- reconciled tables of equipment losses;
- more Egyptian, Jordanian, Syrian and Palestinian first-person testimony;
- checked coordinates for 1967 positions (as opposed to modern town centres).

All of these would come from the books both rounds name (Oren, Barker, Mutawi, Shlaim), the IDF and UN records, and
dated maps.
