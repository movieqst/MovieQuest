# Batoche in other languages

| language | status | files |
|---|---|---|
| **French** | Translated (6 November 2026), ready to record with the two French voices used for Waterloo. A native French speaker should still read it; the place names follow Parcs Canada (la Jolie Prairie, la crête de la Mission, l'anse aux Poissons for Fish Creek). | narration-fr.json, ../../tools/batoche-fr-voices-lines.tsv |
| **Michif** | To be translated and recorded by Michif speakers. Not machine-translated: Michif has few written texts and spellings vary by community. | TRANSLATOR-PACKAGE.md, LETTER-TO-TRANSLATORS.md |
| **Plains Cree (nēhiyawēwin)** | To be translated and recorded by Cree speakers. Not machine-translated, for the same reason. | TRANSLATOR-PACKAGE.md, LETTER-TO-TRANSLATORS.md |

When a Michif or Cree recording arrives, it goes in audio/<lang>-<speaker>/ like the other voices, and the text becomes lang-crk.js or lang-crg.js (Cree is crk, Michif crg), with the translators credited on the page.
