# Batoche 1885: research notes

## Places (WGS84, from Google Maps screenshots, reference only; not to be shown on the page)
- Church of Saint-Antoine-de-Padoue and rectory: about 52.7525, -106.1182
- Batoche cemetery: just south of the church, on the slope above the river (about 52.750, -106.121)
- Parks Canada visitor centre: about 52.7542, -106.1126 (Highway 225)
- River view from the bluff (Street View, Aug 2015): 52.749161, -106.117755, looking south-southwest over the South Saskatchewan
- Batoche lies about 75 km northeast of Saskatoon, on the east bank of the South Saskatchewan River.

## Map sources
- images/battlefield-sketch-1885.jpg (1885 sketch map, Plate VII): river, crossing, rifle pits, church, cemetery, entrenched camp, attack directions. Main guide for the battle map, to be matched to the DEM and the modern river.

## More reference points (Google Maps / Street View, reference only)
- Footpath from the visitor centre west to the church (Street View, Aug 2015): 52.75367, -106.11292. Looking WSW, the church sits on a low rise across open, flat prairie edged with aspen bluffs, much like the "open prairie" on the 1885 sketch.
- The river: west and south of the site the South Saskatchewan makes a broad bend around the village, with steep wooded banks and sandbars (2.5 km-wide view centred 52.7530, -106.1346); flow direction to be checked against the DEM.
- Highway 225 runs north to south about 1 km east of the church.
- Back to Batoche festival grounds (modern, not part of the battle): about 52.767, -106.117, 1.5 km north of the church.
