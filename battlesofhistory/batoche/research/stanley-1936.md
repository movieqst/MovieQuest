# George F. G. Stanley, *The Birth of Western Canada: A History of the Riel Rebellions* (1936)

A reference for **Red River (1869–70)**, **Batoche** and **Fish Creek** (1885). The same file is in redriver/research/.

- **Edition seen:** University of Toronto Press reprint (1960 edition, reprinted 1992) with a new introduction by Thomas Flanagan; maps by Major C. C. J. Bond. Internet Archive, borrowable: https://archive.org/details/birthofwesternca0000stan_o6b2 (another copy: birthofwesternca0000unse).
- **How to use it:** for facts, dates, numbers and Bond's maps only. Stanley's 1936 framing ("primitive" against "civilized" peoples, "half-breeds") is rejected today; Flanagan's own introduction says many readers find it condescending. Never quote its judgments; keep the site's neutral wording.
- **Flanagan's introduction** points to the later standard works: Hildebrandt, *The Battle of Batoche*; Beal and Macleod, *Prairie Fire*; Woodcock, *Gabriel Dumont*; Dempsey on Big Bear; Diane Payment's history of the Métis of Batoche (*The Free People – Otipemisiwak*); Morton, *The Last War Drum*; Giraud, *Le Métis canadien*; the *Collected Writings of Louis Riel* (1985); Sprague, *Canada and the Métis*; Flanagan, *Riel and the Rebellion*.
