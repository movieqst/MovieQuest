# Battle of Bunker Hill (1775): the battle maps, one per chat (18 November 2026)

Made by `tools/draw-maps.py`. The 1775 shoreline is not today's (about a sixth of Boston is made land), so these bases are
cut from **Lieutenant Thomas Hyde Page's manuscript "A Plan of the Action at Bunkers-Hill" (1775, after Captain John
Montresor's survey; Library of Congress, Geography and Map Division, gm71000613; public domain)**, not from modern maps.

The plan was lined up with two points known today: the redoubt (the Monument) and Copp's Hill. Its scale then comes out
within 4% of the plan's own scale of yards, and it is almost exactly north-up. The summit of Bunker Hill on the plan falls
within about 50 metres of the hill's summit today.

Each map goes to ChatGPT in its own new chat, with only its own base and guide. The page draws the armies, the ships, the
arrows and the fire itself, so the paintings show the land only, as it was on the morning of 17 June 1775, before the
battle.

Page colours (owner's default, to confirm): British red, provincials blue.

A third map, Boston and the siege lines (Cambridge, Roxbury, Dorchester), needs a 1775 or 1777 plan of Boston and its
surroundings as its base (see the note to the owner).
