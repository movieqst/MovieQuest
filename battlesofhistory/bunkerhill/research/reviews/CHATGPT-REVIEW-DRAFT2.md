## Final-check items

1. **Burgoyne’s words.** Draft 2 was right to paraphrase. The first review treated the NPS wording too definitively. Drake prints “a large and noble town in one blaze.” The NPS gives “a large and a noble town in one great blaze.” Published accounts preserve different versions, including a letter-book version Burgoyne called its “substance.” The printed variants were verified. The wording of the manuscript sent to Lord Stanley was not verified. Keep the paraphrase for recording. [Drake transcription](https://brittlebooks.library.illinois.edu/brittlebooks_open/Books2009-04/draksa0001bunhil/draksa0001bunhil.pdf) · [NPS quotation](https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/the-burning-of-charlestown.htm) · [Winsor on the versions](https://www.cyberlibrary.org/en/books/narrative-and-critical-history-of-america-vol-6-of-8-the-united-e3f876d4/chapters/24/) · [Library of Congress 1775 printing with letter extract below the map](https://www.loc.gov/resource/g3764b.ar091800/)
2. **Title card.** The owner’s decision to keep Yale’s 1786 Trumbull resolves this item. Credit it as a later painting. [Smithsonian catalogue entry](https://www.si.edu/object/death-general-warren-battle-bunkers-hill-17-june-1775-painting%3Asiris_ari_181553)
3. **Boston map.** The script correctly limits June scenes to siege works supported for that date. Before publishing, check the painted background and overlays for later fortifications. Pelham’s 1777 source plan includes works from both 1775 and 1776. This review covered the script and production notes. The finished map layers were outside the check. [Library of Congress, Pelham’s plan](https://www.loc.gov/item/gm71000619/)
