# Title-card artwork and rights notes

## Preferred historical title image
**John Trumbull, *The Death of General Warren at the Battle of Bunker's Hill*** — famous retrospective painting (versions made from the 1780s onward). No photographs exist from 1775. The Commons file linked below labels its reproduction public domain; verify file-specific attribution before publication.
- Direct file information and downloadable image sizes: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Death_of_General_Warren,_Battle_of_Bunker%27s_Hill,_June_17,_1775.jpg
- Larger group of Trumbull-related reproductions: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:The_Death_of_General_Warren_at_the_Battle_of_Bunker%27s_Hill_by_John_Trumbull
- Credit suggestion: “John Trumbull, *The Death of General Warren at the Battle of Bunker's Hill*, public-domain reproduction, via Wikimedia Commons.” Include particular painting version/date as shown by selected file.
- Historical warning: Artistic staging and uniforms must not be treated as exact battle evidence.

## Alternative primary-period visual
- **A correct view of the late battle at Charlestown (1775)**: https://www.loc.gov/item/gm71005455/ — contemporaneous bird's-eye print with ships, troops, burning Charlestown. Excellent for historical-context slide, though less dramatic than Trumbull.

## Design guidance
Title card 16:9, >=1920×1080. For authentic painting: crop carefully to retain Warren and British-American encounter; overlay the BATTLES OF HISTORY logo in a darkened sky region, not across central figures. Subtitle: “BATTLE OF BUNKER HILL — 17 JUNE 1775.” For an original reconstruction: show redoubt on Breed's Hill, British advancing uphill, smoke and burning Charlestown behind; no 19th-century monument or modern shoreline.
