# Siege of Tyre: art brief (for later: maps first)

**Before any art:** the island outline, the strait and the harbours must be checked against Brocard et al. 2024 (see TYRE-MAP-GUIDE.md).

## Look (332 BCE)
- **The city:**
  - A crowded island city of tall flat-roofed houses, several storeys high.
  - Massive stone walls, highest on the side facing the mainland.
  - A great temple of Melqart (Heracles).
  - Two enclosed harbours.
  - No medieval crenellations or round castle towers.
- **The mole:**
  - Stones (from Old Tyre) and earth on timber piles, about 60 m wide (Diodorus).
  - **Not** a wooden trestle pier.
  - Screens of hides and **two tall wooden siege towers** at its head.
- **Ships:**
  - Triremes and quadriremes/quinqueremes with rams and oars; Phoenician ships have horse-head prows.
  - Ships lashed in pairs carrying rams and artillery (illustrative).
  - The fireship is a broad **horse transport** with two masts and blazing cauldrons on the yards.
- **Macedonians:**
  - **Phalangites:** bronze or iron helmets, linothorax, and sarissas. At a siege they would carry spears and shields.
  - **Hypaspists:** hoplite shields.
  - Engineers and labourers.
  - **Alexander:** as in the Gaugamela sprites. Clean-shaven, a linen corselet, a purple cloak.
- **Tyrians:**
  - Phoenician soldiers and sailors in tunics, conical helmets and round shields; archers and slingers on the walls.
  - Divers.
  - **King Azemilcus:** a Phoenician king in robes and a tall cap.
- **Civilians:** women and children (many were sent to Carthage); people sheltering in the temple of Melqart.

## Sheets that would be wanted
- **Macedonians:** phalangite and hypaspist (walk and attack), engineer or labourer carrying stones, and a siege tower that moves on rollers.
- **Ships:** Macedonian-allied trireme (Phoenician, Cypriot), Tyrian trireme, ram-ship pair, and the fireship (burning).
- **Tyrians:** spearman, archer on the walls, and diver.
- **Leaders:** Alexander (on foot at Tyre), Azemilcus, Admetus and Coenus.
- **Fallen:** Macedonian and Tyrian.

## Avoid
- The "Vergina sun" flags everywhere. It is a royal Macedonian emblem, but there is no evidence for army flags.
- Roman galleys and Viking-style ships.
- Medieval castles.
- Muscle cuirasses on every soldier.
- Gore.
- Glorifying the sack.
