## B. Paintings to make (ChatGPT): paste the block once, then each prompt
```text
Illustrations for an educational history page about the Siege of Tyre, 332 BCE. Same painted style as the page's battle maps: realistic, warm Mediterranean light, wide 16:9 landscape, 1536 x 864 or larger. No text, no captions, no blood or gore. Historically careful for 332 BCE: Macedonian soldiers with long pikes (sarissas) and bronze helmets, Phoenician Tyrians in linen and purple, ancient oared warships with bronze rams. Tyre's walls of large stone blocks rising straight from the sea, NOT medieval castles; the mole is a causeway of stones and earth, NOT a wooden bridge.
```
