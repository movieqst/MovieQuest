```text
Illustrations for an educational history page about the Siege of Tyre, 332 BCE, in the same painted style as the two pictures you already made for it (Alexander meeting the Tyrian envoys, and Tyre seen from the mainland). Realistic, warm Mediterranean light, wide 16:9 landscape, 1672 x 941 or larger. No text, captions, blood or gore.
HISTORY RULES:
- Macedonians of 332 BCE: linen corslets (layered white armour), Phrygian or Boeotian bronze helmets, round bronze-faced shields, long pikes (sarissas). NOT Roman armour, NOT plumed Roman crests.
- Tyrians are Phoenicians: linen and wool in white and Tyrian purple, bronze helmets, round shields, short dark beards.
- Tyre looks as in your mainland painting: an island about 1.5 km long, 700 m off the shore; a plain massive stone sea wall rising straight from the water (no castle towers or battlements); a small walled harbour at each end (north and south) with a narrow mouth; crowded flat-roofed houses; the temple of Melqart in the middle.
- The mole (causeway) is a road of stones and earth with wooden stakes along its sides, built out from the mainland beach toward the middle of the island's east wall. NOT a wooden bridge.
- Warships: oared galleys with a bronze ram at the waterline, painted eyes on the bow, sails furled in battle.
```
