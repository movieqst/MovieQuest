# Siege of Tyre, 332 BCE: picture licences, what is verified and what is not
**Claude historical QA, 7 November 2026.**

**Access:** commons.wikimedia.org does **not resolve** from this session, and neither do the Commons mirror tried or LacusCurtius. **No Commons licence field was read live.** Every Commons entry below is therefore **"not verified here — the site owner will screenshot the licence"** from the live file page (the Summary box, the Licensing templates and the file history).

**Rules applied:**
- No licence is inferred from age alone.
- A photograph of a three-dimensional object (a coin, a mosaic surface, a sarcophagus or a ruin) carries the **photographer's** licence, whatever the age of the object.
- A faithful scan of two-dimensional public-domain art adds no new copyright in the US (*Bridgeman v. Corel*), and Commons generally follows that. The Canadian position is less settled, so record the scan's source anyway.
