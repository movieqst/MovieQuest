### Priority checks

1. **Final assault:** Arrian 2.22–24 distinguishes an earlier repelled assault from the successful ship-borne breach and the simultaneous entries into both harbours. Compare Diodorus 17.46, which gives a different assault description; do not fuse them into a single eyewitness account.
2. **Harbours:** Arrian 2.24 says Phoenicians forced the Egyptian-facing harbour barrier, whereas Cypriots entered the Sidonian-facing harbour *without a barrier at that moment*. Distinguish this from earlier blockade descriptions.
3. **Casualties:** Present Arrian's ~8,000 killed/~30,000 sold and Diodorus's >7,000 killed/>13,000 captives/2,000 crucified as incompatible literary claims, not measured totals. Check whether the crucifixions are included in or additional to killed figures.
4. **Dating:** Check the Athenian archon and Hecatombaeon wording directly; no modern day-of-month without calendar scholarship.
5. **Geography:** Compare Brocard et al. (2024) with Marriner et al. (2007/2008), especially island southern extent, the ~750m versus ~1km shore separation, sea level and uncertain harbour locations.
6. **Names and duties:** Check Azemilcus, Admetus, Coenus, Craterus, Andromachus, Cypriot kings, and any claim assigning Diades, Hephaestion or Perdiccas a specific Tyre command.
7. **Engineering:** Verify two-plethra mole width as Diodorus's literary figure, and separate attested fireship/anchor-diver tactics from plausible but unattested visual details.
8. **Images:** For each proposed Wikimedia Commons item supply exact Commons file page, author, licence and any attribution requirements. No licence inference from image age alone.
