PART A — THE STORY, CHAPTER BY CHAPTER (the most important part)
Give 14–18 chapters in time order, each with: a title; when (season/month if known, else the order); what happened, in 5–10 short factual bullets; who was involved; the sources for each bullet; and where on the map it happens (island, strait, mole, north harbour, south harbour, mainland camp, Old Tyre / Palaetyrus, Sidon, the open sea). Cover at least:

2. The Tyrian embassy, Alexander's wish to sacrifice to Heracles/Melqart in the island temple, and the refusal (Arrian 2.15–16; Curtius 4.2), including the claim that Tyrians killed Alexander's envoys (Curtius) — say which sources tell it.
3. The decision to build a mole; demolishing Old Tyre on the mainland for stone; cedar from the Lebanon; the labourers.
4. The Tyrian counter-attacks: ships with archers and slingers, the two towers on the mole, the fireship (a horse-transport filled with brushwood, pitch and sulphur), the storm that damaged the mole (Diodorus/Curtius), the widened mole.
5. Alexander's expedition to the Antilebanon against the Arabs during the siege (Arrian 2.20.4; Plutarch 24) — how long, and the story of Lysimachus.
6. The fleet changes sides: the kings of Arwad, Byblos and Sidon, then the Cypriot kings (about 120 ships), the Rhodians, Lycians, Cilicians; numbers by source.
7. The blockade of the two harbours (the Sidonian/north and Egyptian/south), the Tyrian booms and sunken stones, the cranes that cleared them, divers who cut the cables.
8. The Tyrian sortie from the north harbour at midday against the Cypriot ships, and Alexander's counter-attack.
9. The siege engines on ships, rams lashed to pairs of ships, artillery; the Tyrian defences: nets, grappling hooks, red-hot sand, wheels, padded leather.
10. The first breach attempts; the breach on the south side; the final assault: Admetus killed, Alexander on the wall, the ships forcing the harbours, the fighting in the streets and at the shrine of Agenor.
11. The end: the number killed and enslaved by source (8,000 killed, 30,000 sold — Arrian; Diodorus' 2,000 crucified; Curtius' Sidonians saving Tyrians); King Azemilcus and the Carthaginian envoys spared in the temple; Alexander's sacrifice and games.
12. Losses on Alexander's side (Arrian's 400) and why the figure is doubtful.
13. The duration (about seven months) and the month of the fall (Arrian: Hekatombaion, archonship of Nicetes = July/August 332 BCE).
14. Aftermath: Gaza, Egypt; the mole that turned the island into a peninsula; Tyre afterwards (refounded, Hellenistic and Roman Tyre); the city today (Lebanon, UNESCO site).
