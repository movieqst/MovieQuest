PEGASUS BRIDGE

Night of 5–6 June 1944 · Bénouville and Ranville, Normandy
Coming soon

Six Horsa gliders land beside two bridges at sixteen minutes past midnight. D Company, 2nd Oxfordshire and Buckinghamshire Light Infantry, under Major John Howard, takes both bridges in a quarter of an hour and holds them through the night against counter-attacks, until Lord Lovat's commandos march in from Sword Beach at one in the afternoon.

The battle is in production: the painted battlefield, the sprite sheets and the photographs are in; the narrated introduction, the reenactment and the command game follow.
