# Medak Pocket 1993: sprite sheets, one per chat (17 November 2026)

Each sheet goes to ChatGPT in its own NEW chat, with one self-contained prompt, the same way as Mogadishu and Somalia 1988.
From the research report: the Croatian Army (HV) and special police against the army of the Republic of Serbian Krajina
(SVK); the UN battalion of 2 PPCLI (Canadian) with two French companies (FREBAT 1 and 3). Equipment types are not named
in the sources for this operation: the T-55, the towed howitzer, the TAM truck, the M70 rifle and the rocket launcher are
what these forces had in 1993, and the page tags them as our reconstruction. The Canadians used M113 carriers; the
French, VABs. No graphic violence, no one hurt; the destroyed villages are not drawn as sprites.
Cut with tools/prepare-sprite-sheet.py (people 160 px cells, vehicles 240 x 200).
