## Memory in Croatia: naming, commemoration, official statements, historians

### Takeaway
In Croatia the operation is usually called **"Medački džep"**, and its military code name is given as **"Džep-93"**. The state, veterans and the Church mark it every September in Gospić as a legitimate liberation action against positions that shelled Gospić. The Serb civilian victims are generally absent from the official ceremonies and are remembered separately by civil-society groups and the Serb minority. The 2013 and 2019 anniversaries drew public controversy over praise for Mirko Norac, a convicted commander.

### Cited Findings
- **Names.** "Medački džep" is the Croatian name. The Zvonimir Despot collection is titled with the code name *hrvatska operacija "Džep-93"* — [search summary citing Despot, *Medački džep 1993.* (2012)](https://www.slobodnaevropa.org/a/1991678.html) **[CHECK: exact book title and publisher]**. YIHR also uses "Operation Džep 93" — [YIHR](https://www.yihr.hr/en/we-remember-the-civilian-victims-of-the-military-action-operation-dzep-93).
- **Dates and forces as officially described.** The Croatian Army (HV) and the Special Police of the Interior Ministry (MUP) conducted the operation from 9 to 17 September 1993 in the area around Gospić — [tportal](https://www.tportal.hr/vijesti/clanak/medalja-za-medacki-dzep-20130909); [Novi list](https://www.novilist.hr/?p=67761).
- **Annual commemorations in Gospić.** Each anniversary features a mass in Gospić cathedral for fallen soldiers and police, attended by generals. The 26th anniversary was in 2019 — [Novi list, "Misa za poginule"](https://www.novilist.hr/?p=67761). Wreaths and candles are laid at the memorial to fallen defenders, as at the 13th anniversary in 2006 — [Dnevnik.hr](https://dnevnik.hr/vijesti/hrvatska/proslava-13-obljetnice-akcije-medacki-dzep.html).
- **Official statements.** At the 20th anniversary in 2013, Veterans' Minister Predrag Matić called it an "unjustly marginalised" action and announced a commemorative medal. He described it as legitimate because enemy forces had "treacherously shelled the town of Gospić and surrounding places" — [tportal](https://www.tportal.hr/vijesti/clanak/medalja-za-medacki-dzep-20130909); [Dnevnik.hr](https://dnevnik.hr/vijesti/hrvatska/ministar-matic-medacki-dzep-neopravdano-marginalizirana-akcija---302169.html).
- **Controversy at the 2019 anniversary (26th).** Index.hr criticised the fact that schoolchildren in Gospić heard justifications for Norac on the first school day — [Index.hr](https://www.index.hr/vijesti/clanak/zasto-su-gospicka-djeca-na-prvi-dan-skole-slusala-opravdanja-za-zlocinca-norca/2115394.aspx). Serbian tabloid Kurir reported that a Croatian minister greeted Norac, and that Norac said the operation was conducted "according to the Geneva Conventions" — [Kurir](https://www.kurir.rs/region/hrvatska/3117979/skandal-na-obelezanju-medackog-dzepa-hrvatski-ministar-pozdravio-ratnog-zlocinca-norca-koji-je-ubijao-srpske-civile-i-pravdao-ga); [Kurir](https://www.kurir.rs/region/hrvatska/3117467/ratni-zlocinac-norac-se-rugao-srpskim-zrtvama-na-godisnjici-medackog-dzepa-rekao-da-je-pogrom-srba-bio-po-zenevskoj-konvenciji-i-da-neki-izmisljaju-i-prave-cirkus-video). **[CHECK: Kurir is a sensationalist tabloid. Use its headline wording only as attributed Serbian press framing.]**
- **Civilian victims are absent from state events.** The Youth Initiative for Human Rights (YIHR) says the Serb civilian victims "are not mentioned at the official state commemorations". In 2023 the group marked the 30th anniversary separately — [YIHR](https://www.yihr.hr/en/youth-paid-their-respects-to-the-civilian-victims-of-the-serbian-national-minority-who-died-in-the-medacki-dzep-operation).
- **Croatian official line on crimes.** A Croatian official said at an event that Croatia "has no connection with such crimes" and that the judiciary will prosecute those responsible — [N1 via search summary](https://n1info.rs/vesti/a91268-Odrzan-parastos-zrtvama-Medackog-dzepa/) **[CHECK: speaker and year]**.
- **The Croatian Memorial-Documentation Centre of the Homeland War** (domovinskirat.hr) has an English page, "Operation Medak Pocket", from 9 September 2020 — [domovinskirat.hr](https://domovinskirat.hr/en/2020/09/09/operation-medak-pocket/). **The page could not be fetched, so its content is unknown.**
- **Croatian historians and documentation.** Zvonimir Despot's 2012 book *Medački džep 1993.* collects Croatian operation documents, documents of the "Serb Army of Krajina" and a 1994 UN report — [search summary](https://www.slobodnaevropa.org/a/1991678.html) **[CHECK]**. *National Security and the Future* (nsf-journal.hr, vol. 3, no. 3-4, 2002) ran a special issue of Croatian articles on Medak Pocket, including Miroslav Međimorec's critique of the Canadian account (see Disputes) — [nsf-journal](https://www.nsf-journal.hr/issues/v3_n3-4/01.html). Croatian scholarly papers also appear on Hrčak — [hrcak 18489](https://hrcak.srce.hr/en/18489); [hrcak 28845](https://hrcak.srce.hr/index.php/clanak/28845) **[CHECK: titles and authors unknown]**.
- **Croatian human-rights NGO view.** Documenta (Zagreb) published a case study on the operation on 9 September 2023, *Studija slučaja: Vojna operacija Medački džep*. It notes that "Gospić was a frequent target of artillery attacks by the Serb Army of Krajina in mid-1993" — [Documenta PDF](https://documenta.hr/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/Studija-slucaja-Vojna-operacija-Medacki-dzep.pdf). Zagreb's Centre for Peace also documented the trial — [Centar za mir](https://www.arhiva2.centar-za-mir.hr/index.php.1047.html).
- **Trials as part of memory.** The ICTY transferred the Rahim Ademi and Mirko Norac case to Croatia on 1 November 2005, the first ICTY case referred to Croatia — [Human Rights House](https://humanrightshouse.org/articles/first-icty-case-transferred-to-croatia/). At the Zagreb County Court, Ademi was acquitted and Norac received 6 years for command responsibility — [search summaries](https://www.slobodnaevropa.org/a/medazcki-dzep-presuda/28051232.html); [FPA](https://fpa.org/icty-refers-two-generals-to-croatia-for-war-crimes-charges/) **[CHECK: verdict date 2008 and the appeal outcome]**.

### Inferences
- There are two competing memories inside Croatia. The official and veterans' memory frames a legitimate operation that ended the shelling of Gospić. The civil-society and Serb-minority memory frames civilian killings and the destruction of villages. A neutral page can present both and attribute each.

## Memory among Serbs: commemorations, returns, the villages today

### Takeaway
Serb organisations mark the anniversary every September. Veritas, the Belgrade-based Krajina documentation centre, organises a requiem (parastos) in St Mark's Church, Belgrade. In Croatia the Serb National Council (SNV), led by Milorad Pupovac, holds a commemoration, and YIHR youth groups lay flowers in Divoselo. Veritas gives 88 dead in Divoselo, Čitluk and Počitelj. The villages were almost completely destroyed, and Divoselo now has only a handful of residents.

### Cited Findings
- **Veritas count.** Veritas says 88 Serbs were killed in Divoselo, Čitluk and Počitelj. Its breakdown is 46 soldiers, 6 police and 36 civilians, of whom 26 were older than 60 — [N1 Serbia, "Veritas: 31. godišnjica"](https://n1info.rs/vesti/veritas-u-ponedeljak-31-godisnjica-stradanja-srba-iz-medackog-dzepa/) (2024). Kurir gives 36 civilians (17 of them women) and 52 soldiers — [Kurir](https://www.kurir.rs/vesti/drustvo/2434535/srbe-zive-bacali-u-vatru-23-godine-od-hrvatskih-zlocina-u-medackom-dzepu). **[CHECK: the soldier/police split differs between the two, but both total 88. Do not use Kurir's headline language; it is graphic.]**
- **Veritas framing.** The villages are described as "Serbian Podvelebit villages" that had been "under UN protection for 18 months". Veritas also says some accused in Croatian courts were acquitted, some received minimal sentences and some were never prosecuted — [N1 Serbia](https://n1info.rs/vesti/veritas-u-ponedeljak-31-godisnjica-stradanja-srba-iz-medackog-dzepa/). **[CHECK: "UN protection" wording. The area was within the UN Protected Area zone ("pink zone" status is debated). Attribute it to Veritas.]**
- **Belgrade requiem.** A parastos for the victims is held at St Mark's Church, Belgrade. The 2015 service was for the 22nd anniversary — [N1](https://n1info.rs/vesti/a91268-Odrzan-parastos-zrtvama-Medackog-dzepa/); [Telegraf (EN)](https://www.telegraf.rs/english/1744992-commemoration-of-serbs-killed-in-medak-pocket). Večernje novosti covers it with the headline "Pred očima UNPROFOR-a…" — [Novosti.rs](https://www.novosti.rs/planeta/region/1152640/pred-ocima-unprofora-hrvatska-vojska-pljackala-palila-rusila-kuce-trovala-bunare-ubijala-masakrirala-parastos-ubijenim-srbima). **[Note: the Novosti headline alleges looting, burning and poisoned wells. Treat these as allegations reported by Serbian press.]**
- **SNV commemoration in Croatia.** SNV president Milorad Pupovac spoke at a commemoration for those killed and said representatives of the authorities should have attended — [Index.hr](https://www.index.hr/vijesti/clanak/pupovac-govorio-na-komemoraciji-za-ubijene-u-medackom-dzepu/2495865.aspx); [N1 Croatia (EN)](https://n1info.hr/english/news/serb-national-council-commemorates-victims-killed-in-operation-medak-pocket). SNV cites the court verdict that most killings happened from 9 to 11 September 1993, and that Počitelj, Čitluk and Divoselo were burned as units withdrew on 17 September — [N1 Croatia](https://n1info.hr/english/news/serb-national-council-commemorates-victims-killed-in-operation-medak-pocket). **[CHECK: year of the Pupovac speech]**
- **YIHR and young people.** In 2022 (the 29th anniversary), YIHR visited Divoselo to lay flowers — [YIHR](https://www.yihr.hr/en/we-remember-the-civilian-victims-of-the-military-action-operation-dzep-93). In September 2023 (the 30th), YIHR and SNV ran a study trip — [YIHR](https://www.yihr.hr/en/youth-paid-their-respects-to-the-civilian-victims-of-the-serbian-national-minority-who-died-in-the-medacki-dzep-operation).
- **Villages today.** Divoselo is a village in the Gospić municipality, Lika-Senj County. Its population was 4 in the 2011 census and 1 in the 2021 census — [Wikipedia "Divoselo" (snippet)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Divoselo). The Serb villages near Gospić, including Divoselo, were "completely devastated" on 9 September 1993 — [N1](https://n1info.rs/vesti/a91268-Odrzan-parastos-zrtvama-Medackog-dzepa/).
- A Canadian DND historian (Bill Rawling) is quoted as saying Croatian forces were ethnically cleansing the area before withdrawing — [Canada.ca, Maple Leaf 2023 (snippet)](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-national-defence/maple-leaf/defence/2023/09/remembering-medak-pocket-battle.html).

### Inferences
- Serb memory centres on the civilian victims and on impunity. The parastos in Belgrade, the SNV event and the YIHR visits are the main forms. Unlike the Croatian memory, it is largely a religious and civil-society commemoration, not a state one.

## Memory in Canada: the "forgotten battle", the 2002 commendation, books and films, critiques

### Takeaway
In Canada, Medak Pocket is remembered as a "forgotten" or "secret" battle. It was the hardest fighting by Canadian soldiers between Korea and Afghanistan, it went largely unreported until 1996, and it was belatedly honoured in 2002 with the Commander-in-Chief Unit Commendation to 2 PPCLI. Critiques of the narrative come mainly from Croatian authors (2002). Some Canadian sources are themselves more cautious about the "biggest since Korea" phrasing.

### Cited Findings
- **2002 recognition.** The Governor General awarded the Commander-in-Chief Unit Commendation to the 2nd Battalion, Princess Patricia's Canadian Light Infantry (2 PPCLI), in 2002 — [Canada.ca Maple Leaf, 2023 (snippet)](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-national-defence/maple-leaf/defence/2023/09/remembering-medak-pocket-battle.html); [Legion Magazine](https://legionmagazine.com/?p=494). Legion Magazine reports the commendation was created in 2002 **[CHECK]**. The unit also received the UN Force Commander's Commendation, one of only three in UNPROFOR's history — [Canadian Encyclopedia](https://thecanadianencyclopedia.ca/en/article/battle-of-medak-pocket).
- **Force and command.** UNPROFOR sent 875 troops of the 2 PPCLI battle group into the pocket, under Lt-Col James Calvin, who later received the Meritorious Service Cross — [Valour Canada](https://valourcanada.ca/military-history-library/the-medak-pocket-croatia/); [Legion Magazine](https://legionmagazine.com/heroes-and-villians-james-calvin-vs-rahim-ademi/) **[CHECK: 875 figure and that the MSC was awarded in 1995]**. The battle group included French-Canadian companies from the Royal 22e Régiment (Van Doos) **[CHECK, not sourced in this search]**.
- **"Forgotten" or "secret" story.** The first Canadian news reports came only when the *Ottawa Citizen* broke the story in November 1996 — [search summary of NATO Association / Legion sources](https://live.natoassociation.ca/?p=39296) **[CHECK]**. Coverage was overshadowed by the Somalia Affair — [Canadian Encyclopedia](https://thecanadianencyclopedia.ca/en/article/battle-of-medak-pocket). Global News ran "Medak Pocket: the forgotten battle" in 2021 — [Global News](https://globalnews.ca/news/8368436/medak-pocket-forgotten-battle).
- **Scale wording varies.** The Canadian Encyclopedia says the most significant combat for Canadians since Korea — [Canadian Encyclopedia](https://thecanadianencyclopedia.ca/en/article/battle-of-medak-pocket). DND's 2023 article says "considered by many to be the toughest action faced by Canadian troops between Korea and Afghanistan" — [Canada.ca](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-national-defence/maple-leaf/defence/2023/09/remembering-medak-pocket-battle.html). RCI's 2019 story is headlined "Canada's biggest battle since the Korean War" — [RCI](https://www.rcinet.ca/en/2019/09/13/on-this-day-sept-15-1993-canada-history-sept-15-1993-canadas-biggest-battle-since-the-korean-war).
- **Book.** Carol Off, *The Ghosts of Medak Pocket: The Story of Canada's Secret War* (2004), is listed as a further reading by the Canadian Encyclopedia — [Canadian Encyclopedia](https://thecanadianencyclopedia.ca/en/article/battle-of-medak-pocket). Lee Windsor wrote on the Canadian implementation of the Medak Pocket Agreement, cited in *Canadian Military History* — [search summary](https://cmfmag.ca/?p=24555) **[CHECK]**. Sean Maloney has a Medak PDF — [seanmmaloney.com](https://new.seanmmaloney.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/03/Medak.pdf) **[CHECK: content not read]**.
- **Art.** Katherine Taylor's painting "Firefight in the Medak Pocket" — [Legion Magazine / Valour search summary](https://legionmagazine.com/fighting-the-monsters-of-medak/) **[CHECK]**. The Military Museums (Calgary) mural panel "M185 Medak Pocket" — [The Military Museums](https://mural-atrium.themilitarymuseums.ca/m185-medak-pocket).
- **Canadian Army's own report.** A 2021 Canadian Army *Line of Sight* article reproduces a report on Medak Pocket operations — [Canada.ca Line of Sight](https://www.canada.ca/en/army/services/line-sight/articles/2021/11/report-on-medak-pocket-operations.html).
- **Critique (Croatian).** Miroslav Međimorec, writing in *National Security and the Future* (2002), argues the Canadian account contains "half-truths" and cites a Croatian MoD press release saying Croatian soldiers were not engaged against the Canadians. He interprets the "forgotten battle" narrative as political — [nsf-journal 01](https://www.nsf-journal.hr/issues/v3_n3-4/01.html); [nsf-journal 02](https://www.nsf-journal.hr/issues/v3_n3-4/02.html). **[Partisan source, snippets only. Attribute.]**

Find a primary source or drop it.]** At the Zagreb trial, General Petar Stipetić disputed the Canadian troops' account — [IWPR](https://iwpr.net/ofgylwsr) **[CHECK]**.

### Inferences
- The Canadian "forgotten battle" story has three parts. Soldiers fought and were ignored, recognition came late (1996 press, 2002 commendation), and veterans later linked the experience to post-traumatic stress. The last part is commonly told but was not confirmed here.
- For students, "a firefight lasting several hours on 15–16 September, the most serious Canadian combat between Korea and Afghanistan in the view of the Canadian Forces" is safer than "the biggest battle since Korea".

## Disputed claims: who says what

### Takeaway
Four points are contested. (1) Croatian losses in the 15 September firefight: "27 killed or wounded" is a Canadian figure attributed to Croatian media, while Croatian authors say about 10 Croatian soldiers died in the whole operation, from Serb fire. (2) The motive: Croatian sources cite the shelling of Gospić, while Serb sources stress an attack on protected villages. (3) Civilian deaths: the ICTY says at least 29, Veritas says 36, and a narrow Croatian charge covers 5. (4) Whether it was a "battle" for Canada: Canada says yes, Croatian authors say no.

### Cited Findings
**1. Croatian casualties in the 15 September Canadian–Croatian firefight**
- Canadian Army report: "It is not confirmed if Croatian soldiers were injured, however the Croatian media reported 27 soldiers killed or wounded during this operation" — [Canada.ca Line of Sight 2021 (snippet)](https://www.canada.ca/en/army/services/line-sight/articles/2021/11/report-on-medak-pocket-operations.html).
- Canadian Encyclopedia: the Croatian army later attributed these deaths to Serbian fire, and it is unknown whether Canadians contributed to the total — [Canadian Encyclopedia](https://thecanadianencyclopedia.ca/en/article/battle-of-medak-pocket).
- Croatian authors (nsf-journal, 2002): about 10 Croatian soldiers were killed in the whole operation, and they ask where the bodies of the 27 allegedly killed by Canadians are — [nsf-journal 02](https://www.nsf-journal.hr/issues/v3_n3-4/02.html) **[CHECK]**. The Croatian MoD said its soldiers were not engaged against UNPROFOR — [nsf-journal 01](https://www.nsf-journal.hr/issues/v3_n3-4/01.html).
- Canadian losses: four Canadians wounded — [search summary of cmfmag](https://cmfmag.ca/?p=8524); none killed in the firefight — [NATO Association (summary)](https://live.natoassociation.ca/?p=39296) **[CHECK]**.

**2. Was the operation a response to the shelling of Gospić?**
- Croatian state view: the area was taken because Gospić was continuously shelled from it. The Zagreb prosecution treated the action itself as legitimate — [RFE/RL (Croatian)](https://www.slobodnaevropa.org/a/1991678.html); Minister Matić (2013) — [Dnevnik.hr](https://dnevnik.hr/vijesti/hrvatska/ministar-matic-medacki-dzep-neopravdano-marginalizirana-akcija---302169.html).
- Documenta (Croatian NGO): Gospić was a frequent target of Serb Army of Krajina artillery in mid-1993 — [Documenta](https://documenta.hr/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/Studija-slucaja-Vojna-operacija-Medacki-dzep.pdf).
- Serb view (Veritas): the operation was an attack on villages under UN protection for 18 months — [N1 Serbia](https://n1info.rs/vesti/veritas-u-ponedeljak-31-godisnjica-stradanja-srba-iz-medackog-dzepa/).
- Courts: the legitimacy of the operation was not on trial. The charges concerned killings of civilians and prisoners and the destruction of property — [ICTY Ademi & Norac case sheet](https://icty.org/x/cases/ademi/cis/en/cis_ademi_norac.pdf).

**3. How many civilians were killed?**
- ICTY indictment (Ademi; later consolidated Ademi & Norac): "at least 29 Serb civilians were killed" and others seriously injured, plus at least 5 captured or wounded Serb soldiers killed, 9–17 September 1993 — [ICTY case sheet](https://icty.org/x/cases/ademi/cis/en/cis_ademi_norac.pdf); [ICTY indictment](https://icty.org/x/cases/ademi/ind/en/ade-ci040730e.htm).
- Zagreb indictment (agreed with ICTY): about 30 civilians and prisoners of war killed — [Hina](https://hina.hr/OTS/4109644) **[CHECK]**.
- The ICTY's Bobetko indictment (a broader charge against another accused, who died before trial): "at least 100 Serbs" including 29 local civilians — [ICTY Bobetko case sheet](https://www.icty.org/x/cases/bobetko/cis/en/cis_bobetko_en.pdf).
- A narrower charge in the Zagreb case: indiscriminate shelling of Čitluk and Divoselo killed 5 civilians — [RFE/RL](https://www.slobodnaevropa.org/a/medazcki-dzep-presuda/28051232.html) **[CHECK]**.
- Veritas: 36 civilians (26 aged over 60) among 88 Serb dead — [N1 Serbia](https://n1info.rs/vesti/veritas-u-ponedeljak-31-godisnjica-stradanja-srba-iz-medackog-dzep/).

**4. Was it a "battle" for Canada?**
- Canada: yes. The 2002 unit commendation and the DND, Canadian Encyclopedia and RCI wording are cited above.
- Croatia (Međimorec 2002, the MoD): Croatian forces did not fight UNPROFOR, so the "battle" is a constructed narrative — [nsf-journal](https://www.nsf-journal.hr/issues/v3_n3-4/01.html).
- Canadian sources also call it a "firefight" in places (e.g. the Taylor painting title). The scale was 4 Canadians wounded and no deaths — sources above.

### Inferences
- The figures do not fully conflict. "At least 29" (ICTY) and 36 (Veritas) differ in method. Veritas may count anyone it classes as a civilian, while the ICTY counts only proven cases. The page should say "at least 29 according to the UN tribunal's indictment; Serb organisations count 36".

### Gaps
- No independent body has verified the exact Croatian casualties on 15 September.
- The final appellate outcome for Norac in the Medak case and the Croatian Supreme Court ruling were not confirmed.

- **Composition of the pocket.** It consisted of the localities of Divoselo, Čitluk and part of Počitelj, plus numerous small hamlets — [Wikipedia (snippet)](https://en.wikipedia.com/wiki/Medak_Pocket).
