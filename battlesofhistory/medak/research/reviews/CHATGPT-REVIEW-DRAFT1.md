# Medak Pocket narration — draft 1 review for Claude

Reviewed 17 November 2026. Input: MEDAK-SCRIPT-DRAFT1.txt, supplied inside MEDAK-SCRIPT-DRAFT1.zip.

## Verdict

**Not yet ready to record.** The structure is usable, the language is generally respectful, and the separation between indictments and convictions is strong. The principal definite error is the description of Josip Krmpotić’s acquittal. Several other lines merge a source’s allegation with a finding, turn a scheduled ceasefire into an accomplished one, or use quotations and institutional claims that remain unverified.

Below, **CONFIRMED** means the stated, limited proposition is supported; **WRONG** identifies an error or materially misleading formulation; **NOT FOUND** means this review did not establish the claim from a sufficiently identifiable source. It does not mean that the claim is necessarily false. Where original judgments were unavailable, the source register explicitly identifies monitoring or press reporting instead.

## Optional improvements and tags

- **Keep the acquittal prominent.** The presentation correctly distinguishes Ademi’s acquittal from Norac’s conviction. Do not suggest that either court found Ademi guilty in substance despite acquitting him.
- Chapter 16: “The referral bench considered that a fair trial and witness protection could be provided” is clearer than an unconditional promise that protection “was assured.” The referral was not a verdict.
- Chapter 17: retain the distinction between the trial sentence and the reduced final sentence. The monitoring report is a report about domestic judgments, not an ICTY conviction. Explain commander responsibility as failure to take required action, rather than automatic guilt for every subordinate’s act.
- Chapter 11: the operational report distinguishes earlier injuries from subsequent exchanges. Four wounded across the operation and a smaller earlier incident count need not contradict each other. For listening, simply omit the additional injury sentence; it also conflicts with I5’s promise to describe no injuries.
- For balance, the Canadian report itself discusses Serb sniping near UN positions as a possible initial contributor, followed by continued Croatian fire. If included, preserve its qualifications. Do not transform its suggested explanations into proven motives. [S4]
- Use `organisation: Documenta/YIHR/Veritas/SNV` instead of a generic `press` or nationality label when those bodies are the actual source. Reserve `court: finding` for findings; add `reported by monitoring report` where appropriate.
- No graphic description needs adding. “Killed,” “bodies,” and “destroyed homes” are sober and age-appropriate in context. The script does not need euphemisms that conceal crimes.
- For TTS, introduce “Croatian Army” before HV, “Krajina Serb Army” before SVK, and say CANBAT “One.” Keep legal terminology and source tags on screen rather than reading all metadata aloud.
