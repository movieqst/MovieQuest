[Ademi surrender](https://www.icty.org/en/press/voluntary-surrender-rahim-ademi-international-criminal-tribunal-former-yugoslavia) · [ICTY annual report](https://www.icty.org/x/file/About/Reports%20and%20Publications/AnnualReports/annual_report_2002_en.pdf) · [Bobetko case sheet](https://www.icty.org/x/cases/bobetko/cis/en/cis_bobetko_en.pdf)
