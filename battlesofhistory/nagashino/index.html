BATTLE OF NAGASHINO

28 June 1575 · Shitaragahara, Mikawa province, Japan
In progress

Takeda Katsuyori lays siege to Nagashino castle. Oda Nobunaga and Tokugawa Ieyasu march to relieve it with 38,000 men and three thousand matchlock gunners, and dig in behind a palisade on the plain of Shitaragahara. On the morning of 28 June the Takeda cavalry, the most feared in Japan, charge the line. The battle that is said to have ended the age of the mounted samurai.
