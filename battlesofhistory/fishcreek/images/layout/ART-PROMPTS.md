# Fish Creek 1885: painting the battle map

`fishcreek/tools/draw-layout-guides.py` draws the map from **real elevation data**, with the same tools as Batoche's maps:
- **`battle-base.png`:** terrain and river only. This is the picture ChatGPT repaints.
- **`battle-guide.png`:** labels, places, arrows and a latitude/longitude grid.

Both are 2400 × 1800 pixels (4:3), north at the top. They cover 52.5641°N 106.2220°W (top left) to 52.5270°N 106.1405°W (bottom right), about 5.5 × 4.1 km (`maps.json`).

**Still to check:** the positions of both columns and of the fighters in the coulee are approximate, from general accounts. The river and Fish Creek's coulee are real.

## The battle map
Attach **battle-base.png** first and **battle-guide.png** second, then paste:

```text
Repaint the first image (battle-base.png, the plain terrain map) as a realistic aerial view of Fish Creek (Tourond's Coulee) on the South Saskatchewan River, in today's Saskatchewan, Canada, on 24 April 1885: a cold, early spring day on the northern prairie. The second image (battle-guide.png) shows what is where. It is only a guide: do not copy its labels.

ALIGNMENT IS THE MOST IMPORTANT THING. Your picture is a repaint of the first image, not a new map. It will be laid exactly on top of it in a game.
- The same size and shape: 4:3 landscape, exactly the same framing. Do not crop, zoom, rotate or shift. North at the top, seen straight down like an aerial photograph.
- The river stays EXACTLY where it is in the first image, every bend in the same place and the same width: a broad, cold, muddy-brown spring river with sandbars, between steep wooded valley banks.
- The coulee of Fish Creek (the winding ravine running from the river at the top centre down to the bottom right) stays EXACTLY where the shading shows it: a deep, narrow ravine with steep sides.

WHAT TO PAINT (April 1885, no modern things):
- The coulee: thick brush and young poplar (aspen), willow and birch along its steep sides and floor, still bare, with a small creek winding along the bottom. This is where the defenders hid, so it should look dense and hard to see into.
- The river valley: steep banks with bare poplar and willow, a little old snow in shaded hollows.
- On top: open prairie of last year's dry, tawny grass, the first green barely showing, scattered aspen bluffs and small sloughs (shallow ponds).
- A few log farm buildings and a rail fence near the coulee (the Tourond family farm), and a dirt cart trail coming up from the south (bottom of the picture) across the prairie toward the coulee.

STYLE: photorealistic aerial view, soft cool spring light, real grass, water, wood and earth textures, natural shadows. No people (the game draws the soldiers).
NO text, labels, letters, numbers, grid lines, arrows, dashed lines, coloured circles or boxes. Paint everything right to the edges.
NO modern things: no highway, paved roads, monument, parking lot, modern houses, power lines or crop circles.
```
