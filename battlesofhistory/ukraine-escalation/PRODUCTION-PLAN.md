# Ukraine and Russia: Escalation, 1991–2014: production plan (owner's decisions, 18 November 2026)

A **slideshow page**, not a battle map.

## Format
- **Old-style slide projector:** a dark room, a screen, 35mm slides in card mounts. Each change has the projector's
  click-clunk and fan whirr, a short light flicker and a little dust.
- **Timed by the narration:** the slides advance automatically. The usual chapter controls stay.
- **About 13 chapters, 3–5 slides each.**
- **Slide types:**
  - photo slides;
  - painted-map slides that zoom and pan slowly inside the frame (Europe 1991, then Ukraine, then Crimea, then
    Sevastopol);
  - title and date slides with short captions and key numbers, sources in small print.
- **Sounds:** made in code, so there are no licence issues.

## Pictures
- Wikimedia Commons photos: public domain, CC0, CC BY and CC BY-SA, each credited (register in research §14).
- Real politicians appear only in real photos, never in AI art.
