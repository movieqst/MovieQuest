## §16 — Open items

1. **January 2006 gas.** Before the dispute, about **$50 per 1,000 m³** was the commonly cited price under the earlier arrangements. Supplies for Ukraine were cut on **1 January**. Under the **4 January** deal, Gazprom’s Russian gas sale to RosUkrEnergo used **$230**, while the intermediary’s initial blended sale to Ukraine used **$95**, with cheaper Central Asian supplies in the mix. Keep counterparties and time periods explicit. [Stern, Oxford Institute for Energy Studies](https://www.oxfordenergy.org/wpcms/wp-content/uploads/2011/01/Jan2006-RussiaUkraineGasCrisis-JonathanStern.pdf)
2. **Crimea, May 1992.** The Crimean parliament adopted its conditional independence act and planned a referendum on **5 May**, then adopted a constitution on **6 May**. Kyiv rejected the 5 May measures on **13 May**. On **21 May**, the Crimean parliament revoked the act and imposed a moratorium on the referendum. Use “shelved the planned referendum” in narration. [Kyiv resolution 2333-XII](https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/2333-12) · [Crimean resolution 86-1](https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/krym/show/rb0086002-92) · [Chronology](https://dash.harvard.edu/server/api/core/bitstreams/7312037e-7340-6bd4-e053-0100007fdf3b/content)
3. **January 2014 deaths.** Serhii Nihoyan and Mykhailo Zhyznevskyi died after being shot on **22 January**. Roman Senyk was shot that day and died on **25 January**. Yurii Verbytskyi was abducted with Ihor Lutsenko on **21 January** and found dead the following day. Give that death separately from the shootings. Prefer these dated statements to a claim about the first deaths of the entire protest movement. [OHCHR investigations briefing](https://ukraine.un.org/sites/default/files/2021-01/Briefing%20note%20on%20Maidan%20investigations%20ENG%20final.pdf) · [ECHR, Lutsenko and Verbytskyy](https://hudoc.echr.coe.int/?i=001-207417)
4. **Instytutska appeal.** Case **759/3498/15-к**. A docket mirror listed an appeal hearing for **6 November 2026**. The outcome of that hearing and any final appellate ruling remain unverified. Call the **18 October 2023** decision a trial-court verdict and leave finality open. [Trial coverage](https://suspilne.media/kyiv/596667-sprava-majdanu-sud-ogolosiv-viroki-patom-berkutivcam/) · [Kyiv Court of Appeal](https://www.kas.gov.ua/)
5. **2001 census.** Ukraine: **77.8% Ukrainian, 17.3% Russian**. Autonomous Republic of Crimea, excluding Sevastopol: **58.3% Russian, 24.3% Ukrainian, 12.0% Crimean Tatar**. The categories describe declared nationality. [Official census publication](https://2001.ukrcensus.gov.ua/d/mono_eng.pdf) · [UN census summary](https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/793593)
6. **Pictures.** See the verified file register below. Separate headshots for every president and an eligible photo of the 2010 Rada brawl remain open.
7. **Treaty dates and text.** Both proposed signature dates are correct. See the timeline table.
