## Tags and balance

- **Chapter 1:** The census categories and their limits should use **record**. **Chapter 9:** Use **established** for the Oxford study’s chronology. **Chapter 10:** Use **estimate** or a dedicated **poll** tag for Pew.
- **Chapter 13:** The line on Yanukovych should name the **Rada on February 22** and **Russia’s UN representative on March 3**. Use **record** for the Rada’s vote and **Russian account** for the coup characterization. Keep the trial verdict under **court**. Phrase the uncertainty as “The verdict did not identify every shooter.” [Rada resolution](https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/757-18) · [UN debate](https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/766547/files/S_PV.7125-EN.pdf) · [Trial report](https://suspilne.media/kyiv/596667-sprava-majdanu-sud-ogolosiv-viroki-patom-berkutivcam/)
- **Chapter 14 and debrief:** Name Putin as the speaker for both the early denial and the later acknowledgment. Use **court** for the independent finding. Attribute the Budapest response jointly to Ukraine, Britain and the United States. A mixed **account** tag fits better than **Ukrainian account** alone. [Joint statement](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/joint-statement-by-foreign-ministers-of-uk-us-and-ukraine-on-budapest-memorandum)
- “About ninety per cent,” “about four-fifths,” and “cheaper gas” are suitable spoken approximations. The final debrief can name historians Richard Sakwa and Serhii Plokhy for the two interpretations.
