# Somalia 1988: the five maps, one per chat (version 3, 16 November 2026)

Each map goes to ChatGPT in its own new chat, with only its own base and guide. The town maps (3-5) now carry the real
streets, riverbeds and runways (OpenStreetMap), limited to the town of about 1990 (GHSL); the prompts give the coast and
rivers as measured positions. The guides carry only neutral labels: the page adds the history.
Made by tools/draw-town-bases.py and tools/draw-layout-guides.py.
