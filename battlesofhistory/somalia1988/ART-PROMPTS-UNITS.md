# Somalia 1988: sprite sheets, one per chat (16 November 2026)

Each sheet goes to ChatGPT in its own new chat, with one self-contained prompt. Equipment from the research report:
the MiG-17 is confirmed (the Hargeisa memorial; the pilot who refused and flew his to Djibouti); Hawker Hunter claims
are unverified, so there is no Hunter. The army's tank and gun types are not named in the sources: the T-54/55 and a
Soviet towed field gun are what the Somali army had then, and the page tags them as our reconstruction. The armed
refugee militia recorded by Gersony wore uniforms and use the army infantry sheet. Cut with tools/prepare-sprite-sheet.py.
