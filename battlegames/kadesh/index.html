Orders

Choose your side. Click a formation or drag a box around several to select them (Ctrl-drag adds). Right-click the map for the order menu: attack-move, move, face, halt, fall back. Pan with the arrow keys, the minimap or the wheel; Shift-drag draws a zoom box. As the Hittites you start concealed behind the city with the whole chariot force in hand. Chariots are fast and hit marching infantry hardest; infantry that halts and holds its ground takes less damage. A formation below 25% strength routs.

    Controls

    Camera: drag, WASD / arrow keys, minimap click, mouse wheel to zoom.
    Space: pause · F: follow the action · E: explain this moment · N: next chapter · . (full stop): fast forward · M: whole map · + / −: zoom · double-click the minimap for fullscreen · Art / Drawn: switch between the painted battlefield with painted units and the computer-drawn version.
    Voice: pre-recorded narration with a choice of narrators in the header; falls back to your browser's speech engine for lines without a clip.
    Tags: click any coloured tag (Established, Egyptian account…) to see the source records behind it.
    Text panels: faint by default, solid while the mouse is over them; the ✕ hides a panel and "View text narration" brings it back.

    Standalone HTML · no install, account, network or WebGL required · historical facts are tagged by confidence; positions, timings and the map are a reconstruction.
